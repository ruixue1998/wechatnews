OpenAI 发布 GPT-6 Cyber，首批开放给获批的安全团队

OpenAI has released the cybersecurity model GPT-6 Cyber, with the first batch available via API and Codex to selected approved Daybreak Red clients. OpenAI rated its cybersecurity capabilities as the highest "Critical" level, while the previous generation GPT-5.6 Cyber was rated "High." OpenAI 发布网络安全模型 GPT-6 Cyber，首批通过 API 和 Codex 向部分获批的 Daybreak Red 客户开放。OpenAI 将它在网络安全方面的能力评为最高的「关键」级，上代 GPT-5.6 Cyber 的评级为「高」。

In OpenAI's Linux kernel research, GPT-6 Cyber reproduced 1,724 vulnerabilities and developed working local privilege escalation exploits for 948 of them. The research team disclosed 304 critical findings to maintainers according to Linux community guidelines and provided candidate patches. 在 OpenAI 的 Linux 内核研究中，GPT-6 Cyber 复现了 1724 项漏洞，为其中 948 项开发出可运行的本地提权利用方式。研究团队按 Linux 社区指引向维护者披露了其中 304 项关键发现，并提供候选补丁。

The model also found 81 previously unknown high-severity vulnerabilities in Firefox and assisted in drafting patches. OpenAI states that Mozilla has fixed many of these vulnerabilities. 模型也在 Firefox 中找到了 81 项此前未知的高危漏洞，并协助拟定补丁。OpenAI 称，Mozilla 已修复其中多项漏洞。

三季度全球 DRAM 营收逼近 2000 亿美元，价格上涨是主因

A tracking report released by Counterpoint Research shows that global DRAM revenue in Q3 2026 approached $200 billion, a year-on-year increase of 369%. During the same period, DRAM bit shipments only increased by about 4% quarter-on-quarter, and the average selling price rose to $1.9 per Gb. Counterpoint attributes this surge in revenue primarily to rising prices, with demand from AI accelerators and data centers intensifying the competition among manufacturers for supply. Counterpoint Research 发布的跟踪报告显示，2026 年第三季度全球 DRAM 营收接近 2000 亿美元，同比增长 369%。同期 DRAM 位元出货量环比仅增约 4%，平均售价升至每 Gb 1.9 美元。Counterpoint 将这轮营收大涨主要归因于价格上升，AI 加速器和数据中心的需求加剧了厂商对货源的争夺。

By revenue, Samsung ranked first with a 38% share, while SK Hynix's revenue grew by over 40% quarter-on-quarter, narrowing the gap with Samsung. ChangXin Memory's revenue share decreased from 10% in the previous quarter to 9%. 按营收计算，三星以 38% 的份额居首，SK 海力士营收环比增长逾 40%，缩小了与三星的差距。长鑫存储的营收份额从上季度的 10% 降至 9%。

全球平板出货创近三年新低，前五厂商仅联想增长

Data released by Omdia shows that global tablet shipments in the third quarter were approximately 32 million units, a year-on-year decrease of 23%, marking the lowest level in nearly three years. The decline in the previous quarter was 10%, with particularly noticeable drops in the Middle East, China, and Western European markets this quarter. Omdia 公布的数据显示，第三季度全球平板电脑出货量约 3200 万台，同比下降 23%，为近三年来的最低水平。上一季度的跌幅为 10%，中东、中国和西欧市场本季度的跌幅尤其明显。

Apple still led with a 36% share, but its shipments decreased by about 28% year-on-year. Huawei saw the largest decline among the top five manufacturers, down 43% year-on-year. Lenovo ranked third with approximately 4.1 million units, with shipments growing by about 12% year-on-year, making it the only brand among the top five to grow, primarily driven by mainstream tablets. 苹果仍以 36% 的份额居首，但出货量同比下降约 28%。华为同比下降 43%，在前五家厂商中跌幅最大。联想以约 410 万台排在第三，出货同比增长约 12%，是前五家中唯一增长的品牌，增量主要来自普通平板。

Omdia pointed out that tight component supply has limited the configuration upgrades of new products, and some consumers may continue to postpone replacing their devices. Omdia 指出，元器件供应紧张限制了新品的配置升级，部分消费者可能继续推迟换机。

💡 星尘智能创始人来杰：能进入家庭的机器人可能在明年出现

Lai Jie, founder and CEO of Stardust Intelligence, explained why the team chose a rope-driven structure using a cup as an example during an exclusive interview with iFanr. When a robot places a cup on a table, if it can perceive that the table is already supporting the cup, it can then release its grip. Lai Jie hopes robots learn about the changes in force when objects make contact. 星尘智能创始人兼 CEO 来杰在接受爱范儿专访时，用一个杯子解释了团队为什么选择绳驱结构。机器人把杯子放到桌上时，如果能感知桌面已经承托住杯子，就可以据此松手。来杰希望机器人学到的是物体接触时的受力变化。

In his view, transmitting power to joints with ropes allows robots to perceive external forces more clearly and also moves motors away from areas like wrists. He also divides embodied models and foundational large models into two layers: the former handles perception and action, the latter plans tasks, and the operating system connects the two. 在他看来，用绳索把动力传到关节，可以让机器人更清楚地感知外力，也能把电机从手腕等部位移开。他还把具身模型和基础大模型分在两个层次：前者处理感知和动作，后者规划任务，操作系统负责把两者接起来。

Lai Jie uses learning to cook from a video as an analogy for this division of labor: the person on the other end of the phone can tell you to first put oil in a hot pan, but picking up the oil bottle and controlling the pouring still needs to be done by yourself. He believes that having foundational large models receive all tactile data would lead to bandwidth and cost issues. Robots can first determine if they have a firm grip on a bottle, and then ask the large model for the next step only when needed. 来杰用视频学做饭比喻这种分工：电话另一端的人可以告诉你先往热锅里放油，拿起油瓶和控制倒油仍要由自己完成。他认为，让基础大模型接收全部触觉数据，会带来带宽和成本问题。机器人可以先判断瓶子有没有抓稳，需要时再向大模型询问下一步。

Lai Jie believes that current robots are still some distance from what he calls the 'iPhone 4 moment.' He predicts that robots capable of entering homes might appear next year. 来杰认为，当前的机器人离他所说的「iPhone 4 时刻」还有距离。他预计， 能进入家庭的机器人可能在明年出现。

This film won the Golden Shell for Best Film and the Jury Prize for Best Screenplay at this year's San Sebastian International Film Festival, with O'Flynn jointly receiving the Silver Shell for Best Lead Performance. 本片今年获得圣塞巴斯蒂安国际电影节最佳影片金贝壳奖及评审团最佳编剧奖，奥弗林并列获得最佳主演银贝壳奖。

《复仇者联盟 5》公布全球宣传行程，上海列入九站名单

Marvel announced the global promotional tour for "Avengers 5," with Shanghai listed among nine stops alongside cities like Seoul, London, and Rome. Marvel also updated its official countdown live stream, where the skyline of each city on the tour will rotate behind Doctor Doom. 漫威公布《复仇者联盟 5》的全球宣传行程，上海与首尔、伦敦、罗马等城市一起列入九站名单。漫威也更新了官方倒计时直播，毁灭博士身后的画面会轮换显示各站城市的天际线。

The film is directed by the Russo Brothers, with Robert Downey Jr. playing Doctor Doom. The North American theatrical release date listed on the Disney Movies official website is December 18. 这部电影由罗素兄弟执导，小罗伯特·唐尼饰演毁灭博士。迪士尼电影官网列出的北美院线上映日期为 12 月 18 日。
