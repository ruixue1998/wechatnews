iPhone 18 Pro 系列首批多平台售罄，部分型号发货延至 10 月

Apple iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max pre-orders began at 8 PM on September 12th. The two models start at 9999 yuan and 10999 yuan respectively, both with 256GB storage, and are available in black, silver, glacier blue, and burgundy red. They will officially go on sale on the 18th of this month. 苹果 iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max 已于 9 月 12 日晚 8 点开启预购，两款机型起售价分别为 9999 元和 10999 元，均从 256GB 存储起步，提供黑色、银色、冰川蓝色和勃艮第酒红色。本月 18 日正式发售。

When pre-orders began, Apple's official website and the Apple Store App were temporarily inaccessible due to high traffic, gradually recovering after about 6 minutes. The first day's release batch quickly sold out on multiple platforms, and the shipping time for some popular models on Apple's official website has been extended to late September or October; glacier blue and burgundy red were the first to run out of initial stock. 预购开始时，苹果官网和 Apple Store App 一度因访问量较大无法进入，约 6 分钟后才陆续恢复。首日发售批次在多个平台快速售罄，苹果官网部分热门型号的发货时间已顺延到 9 月底或 10 月；其中冰川蓝和勃艮第酒红色首批库存最先耗尽。

Gurman：iPhone Duo 有望成为苹果历史级产品

Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman reviewed the newly released foldable phone iPhone Duo in his "Power On" newsletter, and disclosed information about Apple's retail store renovation plans and the brand attribution of new game controllers. 彭博社记者 Mark Gurman 在其「Power On」通讯中评价了新发布的折叠屏手机 iPhone Duo，并披露了苹果零售门店改造计划及新款游戏手柄的品牌归属信息。

Gurman believes that the iPhone Duo is significant enough to secure a place in Apple's historical product lineup, second only to the 1998 iMac, 2001 iPod, 2010 iPad, and 2015 Apple Watch. Gurman 认为，iPhone Duo 足以在苹果历史级产品序列中占据一席之地，仅次于 1998 年的 iMac、2001 年的 iPod、2010 年的 iPad 和 2015 年的 Apple Watch。

He predicts that foldable phones will account for the majority of new phone sales within the next decade, but due to their higher price, the adoption rate will be slower than when smartphones replaced feature phones. He also mentioned that Face ID and telephoto lenses are expected to be realized in the third-generation iPhone Duo through hardware miniaturization and improved AI computing capabilities. 他预计，未来十年内折叠屏手机将占据新机销量的多数，但由于售价较高，普及速度将慢于当年智能手机对功能机的替代过程。他还提到，Face ID 与长焦镜头预计要到第三代 iPhone Duo 才能通过硬件小型化与 AI 计算能力的提升实现。

The report also mentioned that Apple is preparing for a major store overhaul, including restoring independent Genius Bars, removing large video walls, and adding dedicated areas for online pickup and "Today at Apple." These plans will be gradually rolled out to stores worldwide over the next few years. 报道同时提到，苹果正在筹备门店大改造，包括恢复独立 Genius Bar、拆除大型视频墙，并新增线上取货与「Today at Apple」专属区域，相关计划将在未来数年内逐步向全球门店推广。

Additionally, according to leaks, two iPhone game controllers currently under development by Apple are expected to be released under the Beats brand, rather than the Apple brand, to align with third-party accessory market pricing. Gurman also mentioned that a new Apple TV set-top box is expected to be launched this autumn along with a new version of Siri AI. 此外，据爆料，苹果开发中的两款 iPhone 游戏手柄预计将以 Beats 品牌发布，而非 Apple 品牌，以便贴近第三方配件市场定价，该说法尚未获得官方确认。Gurman 还提到，新款 Apple TV 机顶盒预计将于今年秋季随新版 Siri AI 一同推出。

智谱完成约 50 亿美元融资，主要用于下一代模型研发

According to a Reuters report on September 13, Zhipu disclosed in a Hong Kong Stock Exchange filing that it has raised approximately US$5 billion in total through a US$2 billion Hong Kong share placement and a US$3 billion convertible bond issuance. Both transactions were initiated on September 11, and documents disclosed on Sunday confirmed the fundraising results. 据路透社 9 月 13 日报道，智谱在港交所文件中披露，已通过约 20 亿美元港股配售和约 30 亿美元可转债发行，合计募集约 50 亿美元。两项交易于 9 月 11 日启动，周日披露的文件确认了募资结果。

The share placement price is HK$714 per share, a discount of approximately 10% from last Friday's closing price. Zhipu stated that approximately 60% of the net proceeds will be used for the research and development of next-generation models and a fully self-training system, another 15% for business expansion, and the remainder for optimizing capital structure, supplementing daily working capital, and other general corporate purposes. 智谱表示，约 60% 的净募集资金将用于下一代模型和完全自训练体系研发，另有约 15% 用于业务扩张，其余用于优化资本结构、补充日常营运资金及其他一般公司用途。

黄仁勋重申：2030 年全球 AI 基础设施年支出将达 3 万亿至 4 万亿美元

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reiterated his prediction at the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference on September 10 that global annual spending on AI infrastructure will reach US$3 trillion to US$4 trillion by 2030. He believes that computing is shifting from retrieving existing information to generating real-time answers, and with the slowdown of Moore's Law, chips, complete machines, and data centers need integrated collaborative design. 英伟达 CEO 黄仁勋在 9 月 10 日举行的高盛 Communacopia + Technology Conference 上，重申全球 AI 基础设施年支出到 2030 年将达到 3 万亿至 4 万亿美元的预测。他认为，计算正在从检索已有信息转向实时生成答案，叠加摩尔定律放缓，芯片、整机与数据中心需要一体化协同设计。

Jensen Huang stated that supply constraints for AI infrastructure exist simultaneously upstream and downstream: advanced packaging, DRAM, LPDDR memory, connectors, voltage regulators, and wafers are all under pressure, and there are also shortages in land, power, and factory buildings required for data centers. Nvidia is expanding its delivery channels through cloud service providers, OEMs, sovereign AI projects, and new cloud vendors, and is tracking the construction progress of data center projects in various regions. 黄仁勋表示，AI 基础设施的供给约束同时存在于上下游：先进封装、DRAM、LPDDR 内存、连接器、电压调节器和晶圆都面临压力，数据中心所需的土地、电力与厂房也存在缺口。英伟达正通过云服务商、OEM、主权 AI 项目和新型云厂商扩大交付渠道，并跟踪各地数据中心项目的建设进度。

Regarding market doubts that Nvidia's investment in customers might form "circular financing," Jensen Huang stated that the company's invested capital accounts for only a small portion of the projects, and decision-making is based on factors including customers' signed computing power orders and actual demand. He also predicted that physical AI applications such as autonomous driving would make significant progress in the next two to three years. 对于市场质疑英伟达投资客户可能形成「循环融资」，黄仁勋称，公司投入的资金只占项目的一小部分，决策依据包括客户已经签订的算力订单与实际需求。他还预计，自动驾驶等物理 AI 应用会在未来两到三年取得明显进展。

奥特曼确认 OpenAI 今年不会上市，将先完成 AI 安全工作

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman confirmed in an interview published by Fortune on September 12 that the company will not go public in 2026. He stated that considering the current AI safety issues, now is not the right time for an IPO, and OpenAI is not under pressure to go public. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 在《财富》 9 月 12 日发布的专访中确认，公司不会在 2026 年上市。他表示，考虑到目前的 AI 安全问题，现在并非合适的上市时机，OpenAI 也没有急于 IPO 的压力。

Altman said that the IPO would have to wait until the business, the company itself, and society's readiness for the technology are all prepared. When asked by reporters about this year's IPO plans, he explicitly replied, "Not in 2026," adding that the company still has a lot of work to do. Altman 说，上市要等到业务、公司自身，以及社会面对这项技术的状态都准备好。当记者追问今年的上市安排时，他明确回答「不是 2026 年」，并称公司还有大量工作要做。

During the interview, he also stated that the most advanced, unreleased models are already very powerful; before further enhancing their capabilities, it is necessary to first improve the ability to monitor model behavior, align them, and ensure they adhere to human values and user intentions. If safety requires pausing or even stopping AI development, he is willing to explain it to investors. 采访中，他还表示，最先进、尚未发布的模型能力已经很强；在进一步提升能力之前，需要先改善监控模型行为、对齐，以及确保模型遵循人类价值与用户意图的能力。如果安全需要暂停甚至停止 AI 开发，他愿意向投资者说明。

Regarding suggestions for jointly developing safety plans with peers like Anthropic and Google DeepMind, Altman expressed an expectation of cooperation but did not disclose private discussions in advance. He emphasized that the industry cannot allow personal animosity or profit incentives to impede safety responsibilities. 对于与 Anthropic、Google DeepMind 等同行共同制定安全方案的建议，Altman 表示预期会有合作，但没有提前公布私下讨论。他强调，行业不能让个人意气或利润激励妨碍安全责任。

美国律师因 ChatGPT 编造刑事案件证词被罚 5000 美元

The New Mexico Supreme Court ruled that attorney Stephen Aarons submitted police testimony fabricated by ChatGPT and fictitious witness statements in a murder appeal. The court found him in contempt for failing to verify the accuracy of the documents, fined him $5,000, and referred the case to the attorney disciplinary board. 美国新墨西哥州最高法院认定，律师 Stephen Aarons 在一宗谋杀案上诉材料中提交了由 ChatGPT 编造的警察证词和虚构证人陈述。法院以未核实文件准确性为由判其藐视法庭，并处以 5000 美元罚款，同时把案件转交律师纪律机构处理。

Aarons told Reuters that when he took over the appeal last year, he used ChatGPT to summarize trial records and was unaware that the system would fabricate facts out of thin air. The court found that he still lacked remorse after the problem was exposed and did not adequately consider the client's interests; the appeal of the original criminal case is still ongoing. Aarons 对路透社表示，他在去年接手上诉时用 ChatGPT 总结庭审记录，没有意识到系统会凭空编造事实。法院认为，他在问题暴露后仍缺乏悔意，也没有充分顾及客户利益；原刑事案件的上诉仍在审理中。

集邦咨询预计 iPhone Duo 今年出货 500 万台，占折叠屏市场近四分之一

TrendForce's report, published on September 10, estimates that Apple's first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, will ship approximately 5 million units this year, accounting for about 24.8% of the global foldable phone market. In the same forecast, Samsung is expected to ship about 7.1 million units, accounting for 35.1%; Huawei about 5 million units, accounting for 24.8%. 集邦咨询在 9 月 10 日发布的报告中预计，苹果首款折叠屏手机 iPhone Duo 今年出货约 500 万台，在全球折叠屏手机市场的份额约为 24.8%。同一份预测中，三星约为 710 万台、占 35.1%；华为约为 500 万台、占 24.8%。

The report estimates that global foldable phone shipments will be approximately 20.2 million units in 2026, a year-on-year increase of 0.5%. After excluding the increase brought by Apple, Android brands combined will account for approximately 15.2 million units, a year-on-year decrease of over 20%. TrendForce attributes the pressure on Android shipments mainly to rising component costs, with most brands seeing flat or declining shipments. 报告预计，2026 年全球折叠屏手机出货量约为 2020 万台，同比增长 0.5%。剔除苹果带来的增量后，安卓品牌合计约为 1520 万台，同比下降逾 20%。集邦咨询将安卓阵营出货承压主要归因于零部件成本上涨，多数品牌的出货量持平或下降。

The report also analyzed the screen structure of the iPhone Duo: the inner screen uses an OLED stack composed of ten layers of ultra-thin materials, combined with Nano-texture surface treatment to reduce glare and the visual visibility of creases. TrendForce believes that crease improvement requires the joint design of glass, adhesive layers, display modules, and support structures. 报告还分析了 iPhone Duo 的屏幕结构：内屏采用十层超薄材料组成的 OLED 堆叠，并配合 Nano-texture 表面处理，降低反光和折痕的视觉可见度。集邦咨询认为，折痕改善需要玻璃、胶层、显示模组与支撑结构共同设计。

从 Demo 到规模化复制，蚂蚁灵波外滩大会三问具身智能

On September 11, Ant Lingbo hosted an Embodied AI Forum at the 2026 Inclusion·Bund Conference. Participating researchers and enterprise leaders discussed general intelligence, training data, and scenario replication in their public speeches, including the capabilities model architectures should possess, data filtering methods, and commercialization standards. 9 月 11 日，蚂蚁灵波在 2026 Inclusion·外滩大会举办具身智能论坛。与会研究者和企业负责人在公开发言中讨论了通用大脑、训练数据和场景复制，包括模型架构应具备的能力、数据筛选方法和商业化标准。

Shen Yujun, Chief Scientist of Ant Lingbo, believes that embodied models need to extract effective representations from continuous sensor signals, integrate modalities such as vision and touch, and generate executable actions. Measuring model progress should not only look at single demos but also consider whether partners' scenario deployment costs have decreased, and whether robots are safe and stable when operating for extended periods in open environments. 蚂蚁灵波首席科学家沈宇军认为，具身模型需要从连续传感器信号中提取有效表征、融合视觉与触觉等模态，并生成可执行动作。衡量模型进步不能只看单次 Demo，还应看合作伙伴的场景部署成本是否下降，以及机器人在开放环境中长时间运行时是否安全、稳定。

Regarding data, Huang Yongtao, Chief Data Scientist of Ant Lingbo, stated that the number of robots in real production and service scenarios is still small, and failures and feedback have not fully flowed back. After data quality surpasses engineering baselines like frame drops and clock desynchronization, it should be determined by the training objective and repeatedly verified through targeted datasets and training results. 数据方面，蚂蚁灵波首席数据科学家黄用韬称，真实生产与服务场景中的机器人数量仍少，失败和反馈没有充分回流；数据质量越过掉帧、时钟不同步等工程底线后，应由训练目标决定，并通过定向数据集与训练结果反复验证。

Several enterprise leaders advocated for robots to enter real-world scenarios as early as possible but also pointed out that current generalization capabilities are mainly limited to single scenarios and single tasks. Wang Xiaogang, Chairman of Daxiao Robotics, proposed that a business model is only sustainable if thousands of robots appear in a single scenario and purchasers can achieve break-even. Ant Lingbo also announced the launch of an embodied large model challenge based on LingBot-VLA 2.0. 多位企业负责人主张让机器人尽早进入真实场景，但也指出目前的泛化能力主要停留在单场景、单任务。大晓机器人董事长王晓刚提出，只有单一场景出现数千台机器人、购买方能够实现盈亏平衡，商业模式才算具备持续性。蚂蚁灵波还宣布启动基于 LingBot-VLA 2.0 的具身大模型挑战赛。

💡 Dario Amodei：AI 模型能力提升速度应主动放慢

Dario Amodei, CEO of Anthropic, published a lengthy article on September 12, advocating that frontier AI companies proactively slow down the pace of model capability improvement. He emphasized that "slowing down" does not mean stopping training or technological progress, but rather allocating time for alignment, safety protection, and third-party evaluations. Amodei summarized his concerns into two points.
