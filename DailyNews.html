星舰首次进入地球轨道，部署 26 颗 Starlink V3 卫星

SpaceX's Starship launched from Texas, USA on September 28, completing its first orbital ignition in its 14th test flight and deploying 26 Starlink V3 satellites. These satellites entered orbit, distinguishing them from test payloads deployed in previous test flights that subsequently returned to the atmosphere along a suborbital trajectory. SpaceX 的星舰 9 月 28 日从美国得克萨斯州发射，在第 14 次试飞中首次完成入轨点火，并部署 26 颗 Starlink V3 卫星。这批卫星进入轨道，区别于此前试飞中部署、随后随亚轨道轨迹返回大气层的测试载荷。

After satellite deployment, SpaceX shortened the upper stage's originally planned nearly 10-hour flight, arranging for it to deorbit early and splash down in the North Pacific. 上面级爬升期间有一台猛禽发动机提前关闭。飞控团队核对状态后，决定继续用单台发动机完成入轨点火；「超级重型」助推器则按计划在墨西哥湾受控溅落。卫星部署完成后，SpaceX 将原定近 10 小时的上面级飞行缩短，安排其提前脱轨，在北太平洋溅落。

Counterpoint：2030 年低于 200 美元的手机年出货量或减少逾 2.3 亿部

Counterpoint Research stated in a forecast released on September 24 that by 2030, the annual global shipments of smartphones priced below $200 will decrease by over 230 million units compared to 2025, a drop of approximately 40%. During the same period, total global smartphone shipments are expected to recover to about 1.2 billion units, close to 2025 levels; models priced at $200 and above are expected to grow by about 26%. Counterpoint Research 在 9 月 24 日发布的预测中称，到 2030 年，全球售价低于 200 美元的智能手机年出货量将比 2025 年减少超过 2.3 亿部，降幅约 40%。同期全球智能手机总出货量预计恢复至约 12 亿部，接近 2025 年水平；200 美元及以上机型预计增长约 26%。

Research institutions attribute the contraction in low-priced phones to rising memory and chipset costs, increased minimum device configuration requirements, and manufacturers reducing investment in non-key markets. Counterpoint points out that not all lost demand for low-priced phones will shift to mid-range phones: some users will extend their replacement cycles or opt for used and refurbished devices. In low-income markets, the reduction in new low-priced phones may also slow down first-time purchases. 研究机构将低价机收缩归因于存储器和芯片组成本上升、设备最低配置要求提高，以及厂商减少对非重点市场的投入。Counterpoint 指出，流失的低价机需求不会全部转向中端机：部分用户会延长换机周期，或改买二手和翻新机。在低收入市场，低价新机减少还可能拖慢首次购机。

七部门发布新型电池产业规划，提出 2030 年全固态电池初步规模化

Xinhua News Agency reported on September 28 that seven departments, including the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, issued the '15th Five-Year Plan for the Development of the New Battery Industry'. The plan proposes that by 2030, all-solid-state batteries will initially achieve large-scale application, long-life lithium batteries will reach a cycle life of 15,000 times, and leading enterprises' product defect rates will reach PPB level. 新华社 9 月 28 日报道，工业和信息化部等七部门印发《新型电池产业发展「十五五」规划》。规划提出，到 2030 年，全固态电池初步实现规模化应用，长寿命锂电池循环寿命达到 15000 次，头部企业产品缺陷率达到 PPB 级。

The plan also lists advanced electrode materials, new electrolytes, and high-end auxiliary materials as directions for technological breakthroughs, requiring new batteries to accelerate integration with clean energy and energy consumption terminals. The phrase 'initially achieve large-scale application' here refers to the planning goal for 2030, and does not mean that all-solid-state batteries are currently mass-produced on a large scale. 规划还将先进电极材料、新型电解质和高端辅材列为技术突破方向，要求新型电池与清洁能源、用能终端加快融合。这里的「初步实现规模化应用」是 2030 年的规划目标，不代表全固态电池目前已大规模量产。

💡 李想：自研电池不等于「去宁德时代化」

Regarding the industry debate on whether in-house battery development would exclude suppliers, Li Xiang, CEO of Li Auto, published a lengthy response on September 28. He believes that vehicle manufacturers are responsible for defining product experience, system architecture, and quality standards, while suppliers leverage their manufacturing processes and large-scale delivery capabilities; summarizing Li Auto's battery strategy with 'de-XX-ization' misrepresents the relationship between the two as a zero-sum game. 围绕自研电池是否会排斥供应商的行业争议，理想汽车 CEO 李想 9 月 28 日发表长文回应。他认为，整车企业负责定义产品体验、系统架构与质量标准，供应商发挥制造工艺和规模交付能力；用「去 XX 化」概括理想的电池战略，是把双方关系误写成零和博弈。

Examples cited by Li Xiang include the division of labor in the chip industry: Apple, Nvidia, and Qualcomm design chips, while TSMC is responsible for manufacturing; Tesla develops battery technology and also collaborates with companies like CATL for production. He used this to illustrate that car manufacturers delving into component R&D do not need to replace manufacturing partners. 李想举的例子包括芯片产业的分工：苹果、英伟达和高通设计芯片，台积电负责代工；特斯拉开发电池技术，也与宁德时代等企业合作生产。他以此说明，车企深入研发零部件，无需取代制造伙伴。

Speaking of Li Auto and CATL, Li Xiang recalled the high-power, large-capacity extended-range battery of Li ONE and the 5C super-charging battery of Li MEGA. Pictures indicates that the animated adaptation of Dr. Seuss's children's book "The Cat in the Hat" will be introduced to mainland Chinese cinemas, with the specific release date yet to be announced. The film is the first feature-length animated film produced by Warner Bros. Pictures Animation, scheduled for North American release on November 6th. 华纳兄弟电影发布的消息显示，苏斯博士童书改编动画《戴帽子的猫》将引进中国内地院线，具体上映日期尚未公布。影片是华纳兄弟影业动画制作的首部长篇动画电影，北美定于 11 月 6 日上映。

The film features Bill Hader as the voice of the Cat in the Hat, and is directed by Erica Rivinoja and Alessandro Carloni. In the story, the Cat in the Hat is tasked with helping siblings Gabby and Sebastian, who have moved to a strange town, get back on their feet; the official announcement also revealed voice actors such as Matt Berry, Quinta Brunson, and Bowen Yang. 电影由 Bill Hader 为戴帽子的猫配音，Erica Rivinoja 与 Alessandro Carloni 执导。故事中，戴帽子的猫受命帮助搬到陌生小镇的姐弟 Gabby 和 Sebastian 重新振作；官方还公布了 Matt Berry、Quinta Brunson、Bowen Yang 等配音演员。

2026 年 MTV 音乐录影带大奖揭晓

The 2026 MTV Video Music Awards were held on the evening of September 27th, US local time. The complete list of winners announced by the organizers showed that Madonna was the most awarded artist of the night with seven awards, winning categories such as Artist of the Year and Best Dance; Taylor Swift won Video of the Year for "The Fate of Ophelia" and was honored with the inaugural Artist Director Award. 2026 年 MTV 音乐录影带大奖于美国当地时间 9 月 27 日晚举行。主办方公布的完整获奖名单显示，麦当娜以七奖成为当晚获奖最多的艺人，拿下年度艺人、最佳舞曲等类别；泰勒·斯威夫特凭《The Fate of Ophelia》获得年度音乐录影带，并获颁首届艺人导演荣誉。

In the list, LISA won Best Pop Video for "Dream," a collaboration with Kentaro Sakaguchi, and BTS's "Swim" won Song of the Year and Best K-pop Video. Swift also won Best Director for "Opalite." 名单中，LISA 凭与坂口健太郎合作的《Dream》获得最佳流行音乐录影带，BTS 的《Swim》获年度歌曲和最佳 K-pop 音乐录影带。斯威夫特还凭《Opalite》获得最佳导演奖。
