中国手机三季度出货量创 14 年同期新低，华为与苹果份额合计超四成

Preliminary data released by Counterpoint Research on October 19 indicates that China's smartphone shipments in the third quarter of 2026 decreased by 10% year-on-year, falling to their lowest level for the same period since 2012. The agency attributes the market decline to increased material costs due to rising storage prices and exacerbated supply shortages. Counterpoint Research 10 月 19 日发布的初步数据表明，2026 年第三季度中国智能手机出货量同比下降 10%，降至自 2012 年以来的同期最低水平。该机构将市场下滑归因于存储价格上涨带来的物料成本增加，以及供应短缺加剧。

Huawei ranked first with a 21.3% market share, with shipments growing by 19% year-on-year. Counterpoint believes that relatively stable prices in the early part of the quarter, along with strong sales performance from the Changxiang, nova, and Mate product lines, supported this growth; however, Huawei's price increase starting in September may put pressure on subsequent sales. 华为以 21.3% 的份额位居第一，出货量同比增长 19%。Counterpoint 认为，季度前期相对稳定的价格，以及畅享、nova 和 Mate 产品线的销售表现支撑了增长；华为自 9 月起涨价，则可能给后续销售带来压力。

Apple ranked second with a 19.5% share. The report states that sales of the iPhone 18 Pro series in the Chinese market during the first three weeks after launch increased by 23.5% compared to the previous generation's Pro series in the same period; the delayed release of the base model also prompted some consumers to switch to Pro models. This initial sales data uses a different statistical scope than the full-quarter shipments. 苹果以 19.5% 的份额排名第二。报告称，iPhone 18 Pro 系列上市前三周在中国市场的销量，比上一代 Pro 系列同期增长 23.5%；基础款延后发布，也促使部分消费者转向 Pro 机型。这项首发销量数据与全季出货量采用不同统计范围。

The combined share of the two manufacturers exceeded 40%. Counterpoint pointed out that in response to rising component prices, other Android manufacturers are adjusting their product portfolios by adding Pro Max models, launching lower-priced models, or delaying standard versions; consumer behavior such as delaying phone upgrades, waiting for promotions, or switching to refurbished phones may continue to suppress demand in the fourth quarter. 两家厂商的份额合计超过四成。Counterpoint 指出，面对零部件涨价，其他安卓厂商正通过增加 Pro Max、推出更低价机型或推迟标准版等方式调整产品组合；消费者延后换机、等待促销或转向翻新机的行为，可能继续压制第四季度需求。

OpenAI 为 OpenCode 受攻击用户退还被消耗的 API 额度

On October 19, Tibo Sottiaux, a member of OpenAI's Codex and ChatGPT team, stated that the team has partnered with OpenCode to help affected users who also use the OpenAI API restore services and refund API quotas consumed during the attack. 10 月 19 日，OpenAI 的 Codex 与 ChatGPT 团队成员 Tibo Sottiaux 表示，团队已与 OpenCode 合作，帮助同时使用 OpenAI API 的受影响用户恢复服务，并退还攻击期间被消耗的 API 额度。

OpenCode previously disclosed that a security incident related to a large-scale model distillation attack affected 1.4% of Console users, specifically some users who had previously linked external model service providers in the cloud. The team stated that they have sent emails individually and are collaborating with Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google for resolution. OpenCode 此前公开披露，一次与大规模模型蒸馏攻击有关的安全事件影响了 1.4% 的 Console 用户，受影响范围是曾在云端关联外部模型服务商的部分用户。团队称已逐一发送邮件，并与 Anthropic、OpenAI 和 Google 合作处置。

Sottiaux also stated that security teams can apply for Daybreak Blue in advance, using model configurations for defensive tasks with relaxed cybersecurity restrictions, without having to wait until an incident occurs to apply. Dax Raad, co-founder of OpenCode, replied below the post, stating that Daybreak played a crucial role in this defense and is currently still running. Sottiaux 还表示，安全团队可以提前申请 Daybreak Blue，使用面向防御任务、放宽部分网络安全限制的模型配置，无须等到事件发生后才申请。OpenCode 联合创始人 Dax Raad 在帖下回复，称 Daybreak 在此次防御中发挥了关键作用，目前仍在持续运行。

This refund statement targets affected OpenAI API customers, and its scope has not been extended to all third-party model accounts. 这次退款表态针对受影响的 OpenAI API 客户，范围并未扩展到所有第三方模型账户。

「海面漂满 iPhone」视频系 AI 编造，原账号已标注 AI 内容

A verification report by Red Star News pointed out that the relevant images are AI-generated fictional content, and some videos can be traced back to the Instagram account Nativora, where the creator has already added an "AI Content" label. 近日，声称「霍尔木兹海峡沉船后，海面漂满 iPhone 和法国香水」的视频在社交平台流传。红星新闻的核查报道指出，相关影像是 AI 生成的虚构内容，部分视频最早可追溯至 Instagram 账号 Nativora，创作者已添加「AI Content」标识。

Investigators found the original account through reverse image search. The video itself also has several anomalies: the text on the phone packaging boxes is distorted, the floating objects resemble plastic models, and the clothing of characters in the same scene shows a mix of winter and summer attire. 调查人员通过反向搜索找到原始账号。视频本身也有多处异常：手机包装盒文字扭曲，漂浮物形态像塑料模型，同一场景的人物衣着则出现冬装、夏装混杂。

The cargo ship accident and these "salvaged phone" images need to be viewed separately. The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore previously reported that a Singaporean cargo ship was damaged, caught fire, and sank while passing through the Strait of Hormuz on October 13, with all 21 crew members safely evacuated. The occurrence of the accident does not prove the authenticity of the circulated cargo images. 货轮事故与这些「打捞手机」影像需要分别看待。新加坡海事及港务管理局曾通报，一艘新加坡货轮在 10 月 13 日通过霍尔木兹海峡时受损起火并沉没，21 名船员安全撤离。事故发生并不能证明网传的货物画面真实。

Modal 将采用英伟达 Vera CPU，继续保留 Intel 与 AMD 机群

According to a Reuters report on October 19, cloud computing startup Modal plans to adopt Nvidia's Vera central processor for the first time to provide computing power for AI agents capable of continuously executing tasks. Previously, all of Modal's such workloads ran on Intel and AMD chips. 据路透社 10 月 19 日报道，云计算初创公司 Modal 计划首次采用英伟达 Vera 中央处理器，为能够持续执行任务的 AI 智能体提供算力。此前，Modal 的这类工作负载全部运行在 Intel 和 AMD 芯片上。

Modal stated that in early tests, Vera doubled the workload that agents could complete, leading the company to decide to adapt its underlying software for the Nvidia architecture. Modal 表示，早期测试中，Vera 让智能体能够完成的工作量翻倍，公司因此决定为英伟达架构进行底层软件适配。

CEO Erik Bernhardsson emphasized that the company will combine Vera with existing Intel and AMD products to form a hybrid cluster, allocating hardware based on the actual performance of different tasks. The tests compared Intel and AMD chips that were already on the market at the time; Modal will retest when the two companies release new models. 首席执行官 Erik Bernhardsson 强调，公司会把 Vera 与现有 Intel、AMD 产品组合成混合机群，按不同任务的实际表现分配硬件。测试对比的是当时已经上市的 Intel 和 AMD 芯片；待两家推出新型号，Modal 也会重新测试。

Nscale 与 Anthropic 洽谈 450 亿美元新算力交易，涉及第二座 Monarch 设施

According to a Reuters report on October 19, Nscale is negotiating a potential AI computing power deal worth approximately $45 billion with Anthropic, with services to be provided by the second facility at the Monarch data center park in West Virginia, USA. 据路透社 10 月 19 日报道，Nscale 正与 Anthropic 商谈一笔可能价值约 450 亿美元的 AI 算力交易，拟由美国西弗吉尼亚州 Monarch 数据中心园区的第二座设施提供服务。

Sources said the facility will use Nvidia Vera Rubin AI chips, providing 460 megawatts of computing power. Anthropic also holds a right of first refusal for a third facility planned for construction in the park; negotiations are ongoing, and terms may change. 知情人士称，这座设施将采用英伟达 Vera Rubin AI 芯片，提供 460 兆瓦算力。Anthropic 还持有园区计划建设的第三座设施的优先购买权；谈判仍在进行，条款可能变化。

This will be another collaboration between the two parties at Monarch. In August, Anthropic reached a $45 billion computing power lease agreement; this negotiation is for the second facility and should not be considered the same transaction as the previous agreement. 这将是双方在 Monarch 的另一笔合作。今年 8 月，Anthropic 已达成一项价值 450 亿美元的算力租赁协议，本次商谈针对第二座设施，不能与此前协议视为同一笔交易。

Reuters also mentioned that Microsoft recently withdrew from another flagship data center collaboration with Nscale due to power access delays, with the project's expected power supply date pushed back to the 2030s. 路透社同时提到，微软近期因电力接入延误退出了 Nscale 的另一项旗舰数据中心合作，该项目预计供电时间已被推迟至 2030 年代。
