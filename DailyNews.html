曝苹果新 CEO Ternus 希望更快发产品、精简组织

According to Bloomberg, Apple's new CEO John Ternus is planning to adjust the company's organization and product release rhythm, hoping to make Apple more streamlined and decision-making faster, in response to the competition in the AI era. 据彭博社报道，苹果新任 CEO John Ternus 正筹划调整公司组织与产品发布节奏，希望让苹果更精简、决策更快，以应对人工智能时代的竞争。

Ternus believes that Apple needs to launch products faster and increase experimentation; he is also evaluating reducing reliance on traditional spring and autumn release cycles, opting instead for more frequent product releases throughout the year. Ternus 认为，苹果需要更快推出产品、增加试验性；他也在评估减少对春季和秋季传统发布周期的依赖，改为全年更频繁地发布产品。

Apple plans to launch multiple devices next year, including AirPods with cameras, its first smart glasses, and a redesigned model prepared for the iPhone's 20th anniversary. The report states that Ternus is reconsidering new product release arrangements, but changing Apple's long-established development process could take several years. 苹果明年计划推出多款设备，包括带摄像头的 AirPods、首款智能眼镜，以及为 iPhone 诞生 20 周年准备的重新设计机型。报道称，Ternus 正重新考虑新品发布安排，但改变苹果长期形成的开发流程可能需要数年。

Organizational adjustments have also begun. Apple's hardware engineering department has laid off several engineering project managers in the past two weeks, including about 6 high-level executives; employees who cannot find other positions within the company may be dismissed later. 组织调整也已开始。苹果硬件工程部门近两周裁撤了多名工程项目经理，其中包括约 6 名高级别主管；未能在公司内部找到其他岗位的员工，之后可能被解雇。

Apple has also recently conducted layoffs in teams such as Siri, Vision Pro, and AI-related software engineering, and has scaled back or canceled some early projects. 苹果近期还在 Siri、Vision Pro 和 AI 相关软件工程等团队进行裁员，并缩减或取消部分早期项目。

Meanwhile, Ternus, along with Eddy Cue, head of services, and Kevan Parekh, head of finance, are discussing new services and ways to increase revenue from existing products and services. The slowdown in Apple's services business growth and rising memory costs are also putting pressure on the company. The report notes that Apple is also controlling costs, but has not yet announced a specific timeline for broader adjustments. 与此同时，Ternus 与服务业务负责人 Eddy Cue、财务负责人 Kevan Parekh 正讨论新的服务，以及从现有产品和服务中增加收入的方式。苹果服务业务增长放缓、内存成本上升，也给公司带来压力。报道指出，苹果还在控制成本，但尚未公布更广泛调整的具体时间表。

携程鼓励员工休「无理由事假」：每休 1 天，团队获 600 元奖励

According to Jiemian News, Trip.com recently notified employees that starting last week, for every day an employee takes 'unjustified personal leave,' their team will receive a 600 yuan reward for team building and other activities. The reward is given to the team, not the employee taking leave, addressing concerns that employees might worry about burdening their team or inconveniencing their supervisor by taking time off. 据界面新闻报道，携程近日通知员工，自上周起，每有员工休 1 天「无理由事假」，其所在团队可获得 600 元奖励，用于团建等场景。奖励给团队而非请假员工，针对的是员工担心请假拖累团队、给主管添麻烦的顾虑。

This incentive follows Trip.com's vacation experiment launched this year. The company previously announced that starting May 1st, a one-year randomized controlled trial would be conducted in some business departments, with approximately 6,000 employees divided into experimental and control groups. In addition to their existing paid leave, the experimental group can take up to 45 days of unjustified personal leave annually, without needing to state a reason; salary and attendance during leave will still follow current personal leave rules. 这项激励接在携程今年启动的休假实验之后。公司此前宣布，从 5 月 1 日起，在部分业务部门开展为期 1 年的随机对照实验，约 6000 名员工分入实验组和对照组。实验组在原有带薪假期之外，每年最多可休 45 天无理由事假，无需说明事由；请假期间的薪酬与考勤仍按现行事假规则执行。

OpenAI 将恢复 200 美元 Pro 订阅，但用量减半

Tibo Sottiaux, OpenAI's product lead for Codex and ChatGPT, stated on September 29th that the $200/month Pro subscription would reopen to new users the next day, along with a change in usage calculation. Based on API list prices, the new plan offers about half the usage of the old version; this is an equivalent consumption metric and does not mean that the actual task count for all models will be neatly halved. OpenAI 的 Codex 与 ChatGPT 产品负责人 Tibo Sottiaux 9 月 29 日表示，月费 200 美元的 Pro 订阅将于次日重新向新用户开放，同时改变用量计算方式。按 API 标价折算，新方案提供的用量约为旧版的一半；这是等值消费口径，不代表所有模型的实际任务次数都会整齐减半。

Sottiaux explained that OpenAI has already halved the API prices for GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna this week, and improved model efficiency and price reductions might allow users to accomplish more work with fewer API equivalent credits. The company promised not to reinstate the Pro's 5-hour usage limit and previewed new content that won't consume plan usage, with details yet to be announced. OpenAI has not yet provided actual completion data for various tasks after the adjustment. Sottiaux 解释，OpenAI 本周已将 GPT-6 Sol 和 GPT-6 Luna 的 API 价格降至此前约一半，模型效率和降价可能让用户用更少的 API 等值额度完成更多工作。公司承诺不会恢复 Pro 的 5 小时用量限制，并预告将加入不消耗套餐用量的新内容，细节尚待公布。OpenAI 尚未给出调整后各类任务的实际完成量数据。

比亚迪西咸电池基地全面投产，为第二代刀片电池首批生产基地

According to a September 29th announcement from Xi'an Release, BYD's new energy vehicle power battery project in Qinhan New City, Xixian New Area, has been fully completed and put into production. 中国生成式 AI 用户突破 7 亿，智能问答仍是最常见用途

The 'Generative AI Application Development Report (2026)' released by the China Internet Network Information Center on September 29th shows that as of the first half of this year, the scale of generative AI users in China has exceeded 700 million, with a penetration rate of over 50%. The report, announced at the China Internet Basic Resources Conference, defines its statistical scope as users who use generative AI, which is not equivalent to active users of a single application. 中国互联网络信息中心 9 月 29 日发布的《生成式人工智能应用发展报告（2026）》显示，截至今年上半年，我国生成式 AI 用户规模突破 7 亿，普及率超过 50%。报告在中国互联网基础资源大会公布，统计范围是使用生成式 AI 的用户，不等同于单一应用的活跃用户。

Among surveyed users, 76.0% use generative AI to answer questions; the proportions for processing images or videos, processing text, and creating work summaries, meeting minutes, and PPTs are 47.8%, 37.6%, and 32.5% respectively. The report also states that the usage frequency of AI comprehensive assistants and AI efficiency office applications both increased by over 100% year-on-year. These data illustrate the usage structure of generative AI expanding from Q&A to content processing and office tasks. 在受访用户中，76.0% 会用生成式 AI 回答问题；处理图片或视频、处理文本、制作工作总结或会议纪要及 PPT 的比例分别为 47.8%、37.6% 和 32.5%。报告还称，AI 综合助手与 AI 效率办公应用的使用次数同比均增长超过 100%。这些数据呈现了生成式 AI 从问答向内容处理和办公任务扩展的使用结构。
