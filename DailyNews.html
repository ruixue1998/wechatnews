iPhone 18 Pro 昨日开卖：Pro Max 仍有加价，Pro 已出现破发

The iPhone 18 Pro series went on sale in China on September 18. Reporters from Cailian Press visited Apple Stores in Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen, where queues for pickup were seen outside all stores; some stores had no stock, and the official website's estimated arrival time for the Pro Max was already scheduled for mid-October, while the Pro was relatively easier to purchase. iPhone 18 Pro 系列 9 月 18 日在中国开售。财联社记者走访上海、广州和深圳三地 Apple Store，门店外均出现排队取货；部分门店没有现货，Pro Max 的官网预计到货时间已排到 10 月中旬，Pro 相对更容易买到。

The difference in demand in the second-hand market is even more pronounced. Several recyclers in Shanghai quoted a premium of about 350 to 400 yuan for the red Pro Max 256GB, while in Guangzhou, the red model generally commanded a premium of over 500 yuan. The blue model, which initially saw a premium of 1,000 yuan at launch, fell back to about 550 yuan in the morning; in Shenzhen, the red model had a premium of about 500 to 700 yuan. Many recyclers refused to accept the Pro, or were only willing to buy it back at its original price, stating that some models had already "fallen below issue price." 二手市场的冷热差异更明显。上海多名回收商给红色 Pro Max 256GB 报出加价约 350 至 400 元，广州红色款普遍加价 500 元以上，蓝色款开售时一度加价 1000 元、上午回落至约 550 元；深圳红色款加价约 500 至 700 元。多名回收商拒收 Pro，或只愿按原价回收，并称部分机型已经「破发」。

Apple's official website shows that the red 256GB Pro can be shipped in about 11 business days, and the Pro Max in about 15 business days; most colors of the Pro at JD.com's self-operated flagship store can be shipped within 1 to 2 days. The starting prices for the new domestic models are 9,999 yuan and 10,999 yuan, respectively, both 1,000 yuan higher than the previous generation. 苹果官网显示，红色 256GB Pro 约 11 个工作日可发货，Pro Max 约需 15 个工作日；京东自营旗舰店的 Pro 多数颜色可在 1 至 2 日内发货。新机国行起售价分别为 9999 元和 10999 元，均比上一代高 1000 元。

比亚迪召回 18.32 万辆唐、秦车型，涉及制动灯常亮隐患

The State Administration for Market Regulation announced on September 18 that BYD Auto Industry Co., Ltd. and BYD Automobile Co., Ltd. have filed two proactive recall plans, involving a total of 183,211 domestically produced vehicles, with the recall effective immediately. 市场监管总局 9 月 18 日公布，比亚迪汽车工业有限公司和比亚迪汽车有限公司备案两项主动召回计划，共涉及 183211 辆国产汽车，召回自即日起实施。

This includes 142,895 units of certain Tang, all-new Tang DM, and all-new Tang EV models produced between March 2015 and January 2022; and another 40,316 units of certain Qin series vehicles produced between June 2014 and June 2019. 其中包括 2015 年 3 月至 2022 年 1 月间生产的部分唐、全新一代唐 DM 和全新一代唐 EV，共 142895 辆；另有 2014 年 6 月至 2019 年 6 月间生产的部分秦系列汽车 40316 辆。

The reason for the recall is that some vehicles' brake pedal stopper pads have abnormal material batches, which may crack or fall off after long-term use. In extreme cases, this could cause the brake lights to remain on when the brake pedal is not pressed, sending incorrect signals to following vehicles. BYD authorized dealers will replace the improved stopper pads free of charge; vehicles that have already had them replaced do not need to be processed again. 召回原因是部分车辆的制动踏板限位垫存在材料批次异常，长期使用后可能开裂或脱落，极端情况下会使制动踏板未踩下时制动灯常亮，向后车传递错误信号。比亚迪授权经销商将免费更换改善后的限位垫；此前已经更换的车辆无需重复处理。
