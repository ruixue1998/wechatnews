我国首次载人登月乘组计划由 3 人组成，2 人着陆月球

On October 31, Wang Xiang, deputy chief designer of China's manned space engineering project and chief designer of the lunar exploration project, introduced at the 7th Manned Space Academic Conference that China's first manned lunar exploration mission crew is planned to consist of 3 people, with 2 of them landing on the lunar surface. 10 月 31 日，中国载人航天工程副总设计师兼月球探测工程总设计师王翔在第七届载人航天学术大会上介绍，我国首次载人月球探测任务乘组计划由 3 人组成，其中 2 人着陆月球表面。

According to an on-site report by a Global Times reporter, China plans to achieve a Chinese landing on the moon before 2030, adopting a "two launches, one moon landing" lunar orbit rendezvous scheme: first, a Long March 10 carrier rocket will send a lander to lunar orbit, then a manned spacecraft will be launched to rendezvous and dock with the lander. After astronauts enter the lander, they will separate from the manned spacecraft and land on the lunar surface to carry out scientific exploration. 据《环球时报》记者现场报道，我国计划在 2030 年前实现中国人登陆月球，采用「两次发射，一次登月」的环月轨道对接方案：先用一枚长征十号运载火箭将着陆器送往月球轨道，再发射载人飞船，与着陆器交会对接。航天员进入着陆器后，与载人飞船分离并着陆月面，开展科学探测。

After completing the lunar surface mission, the astronauts will ascend in the lander, rendezvous and dock with the manned spacecraft again in lunar orbit, and then return to Earth aboard the manned spacecraft. 月面任务完成后，航天员将乘着陆器上升，在月球轨道与载人飞船再次交会对接，随后乘载人飞船返回地球。

TCL 华星 t8 产线设备进场，计划明年底量产印刷 OLED

On October 31, TCL CSOT held the equipment move-in ceremony for its t8 project in Guangzhou. The first core process equipment entered the site, marking the transition of the production line from factory construction to equipment installation and debugging. This G8.6 printed OLED production line is planned for mass production in the fourth quarter of 2027. TCL 华星 10 月 31 日在广州举行 t8 项目设备搬入仪式，首台核心工艺设备进场，产线由厂房建设转入设备安装与调试阶段。这条 G8.6 印刷 OLED 产线计划于 2027 年第四季度量产。

According to TCL CSOT's project press release, t8 has a total investment of approximately 29.5 billion yuan, with a designed monthly processing capacity of about 22,500 glass substrates. Next, production line installation, power system joint debugging, and process verification will be carried out. 据 TCL 华星项目新闻稿，t8 总投资约 295 亿元，设计月加工玻璃基板能力约 2.25 万片，主要面向平板、笔记本电脑和显示器，也可延伸至柔性、折叠显示屏。接下来将开展产线安装、动力系统联调和工艺验证。

AI 控制机械臂完成活鼠脑内注射，兽医负责开颅

Biotechnology company Exin Therapeutics integrated an AI agent into experimental equipment, allowing it to select gene therapies and control a robotic arm to perform intracerebral injections for an epileptic model mouse. Founder Gabriel Ocana Santero told WIRED that veterinarians first perform craniotomy, then the AI selects the therapy, brain region, and injection coordinates from 19 options in a table, and controls the injection volume and speed. 生物技术公司 Exin Therapeutics 将 AI 智能体接入实验设备，让它选择基因疗法并控制机械臂，为一只癫痫模型小鼠完成脑内注射。创始人 Gabriel Ocana Santero 向 WIRED 介绍，兽医先完成开颅，AI 再从表格中的 19 个选项选择疗法、脑区和注射坐标，并控制注射量与速度。

The team initially tested Claude Opus 5.5, which completed the experimental planning but refused to insert the needle into the mouse's brain; operations only continued after switching to Claude Sonnet 5. The team required human approval for injection plans, set up an emergency button to cut off the equipment's motor, and prohibited the agent from altering the equipment's underlying code. 团队最初测试 Claude Opus 5.5，它完成了实验规划，但拒绝将针头伸入小鼠大脑；换用 Claude Sonnet 5 后才继续操作。团队要求注射方案先由人类批准，并设置可切断设备电机的紧急按钮，同时禁止智能体改动设备底层代码。

During the process, the equipment's coordinate system malfunctioned. The agent identified an error in the injection position calculation but could not modify the code due to the aforementioned restrictions, so the team used another Opus 5 agent to fix it. The entire experiment took slightly over an hour, while conventional similar operations take about 45 minutes. Santero stated that the mouse recovered well, and the team is awaiting the gene therapy's effect before evaluating its impact on seizures. 过程中，设备坐标系统出现故障。智能体识别出注射位置计算有误，却因上述限制无法修改代码，团队另用一个 Opus 5 智能体修复。整场实验耗时略超一小时，常规同类操作约需 45 分钟。Santero 称小鼠恢复良好，团队正等待基因疗法起效，再评估对癫痫发作的影响。

西湖大学成立世界模型研究中心，计划三年内构建千亿参数模型

On October 31, the Westlake World Model Research Center was established at Westlake University, with Yu Kaicheng, Assistant Professor and PhD supervisor at the School of Engineering, serving as its director. This university-level interdisciplinary research platform will be tasked with building a general world model. Yu Kaicheng stated in an exclusive interview with Chao News that they plan to achieve a scale of 100 billion parameters within three years. 10 月 31 日，西湖世界模型研究中心在西湖大学成立，由工学院助理教授、博士生导师于开丞担任主任。这个校级交叉科研平台将以构建通用世界模型为使命，于开丞在接受潮新闻专访时表示，规划在三年内做到 1000 亿参数规模。
