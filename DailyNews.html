蚂蚁灵波朱兴：机器人规模落地仍受能力、成本和数据约束

Zhu Xing, CEO of Ant Lingbo, said in an interview with APPSO that when robots enter real-world scenarios such as pharmacies and nursing homes, they first encounter dual limitations of model capabilities and hardware reliability. Long tasks, anomaly handling, and cross-scenario generalization in open environments are still unstable; a process learned by a robot in one pharmacy often needs to be re-adapted when moved to another. 蚂蚁灵波 CEO 朱兴在接受 APPSO 采访时说，当前机器人进入药房、养老院等真实场景，首先遇到的是模型能力和硬件可靠性的双重限制。开放环境里的长任务、异常处理和跨场景泛化仍不稳定，机器人在一家药房学会的流程，换到另一家往往还要重新适配。

He calls data the core bottleneck of Physical AI: laboratory-collected data is relatively structured, while real business data is like 'coarse grains,' noisy and inconsistent in format. Lingbo improves the usability of customized data from about 15% to over 90% through cleaning and automatic labeling. Zhu Xing believes that the more reliable commercialization path at this stage is to first solve specific problems in closed scenarios where customers are willing to pay for testing, and then gradually expand the scope of tasks. 他把数据称为 Physical AI 的核心瓶颈：实验室采集的数据较规整，真实业务数据却像「粗粮」，噪声大、格式不一。灵波通过清洗和自动标注，将定制数据的可用率从约 15% 提高到 90% 以上。朱兴认为，现阶段更可靠的商业化路径是先在客户愿意付费测试的封闭场景中解决具体问题，再逐步扩大任务范围。

Zhu Xing cited the pilot project at Guo Da Pharmacy as an example: robots need to walk in a passage about 80 centimeters wide, identify medicines, and handle temporary obstructions; any single error would interrupt the task. Customers pay for the system to work stably long-term; a single successful demonstration is not enough to support a purchasing decision. 朱兴以国大药房的试点为例：机器人要在约 80 厘米宽的通道内行走、识别药品并处理临时阻挡，任何一环失误都会中断任务。客户付费要求系统长期稳定工作；单次演示成功不足以支持采购决定。

Regarding general-purpose versus specialized routes, he does not see small models and foundation models as an either/or choice. Small models are suitable for cost and latency-sensitive deterministic tasks, while foundation models are responsible for understanding new environments and complex instructions; implementation teams still need to perform engineering orchestration between the two and continuously feed failed samples back into the data system. 对于通用与专用路线，他没有把小模型和基础模型看成二选一。小模型适合成本和时延敏感的确定性任务，基础模型负责理解新环境与复杂指令；落地团队仍要在两者之间做工程编排，并把失败样本持续回流到数据体系。

周鸿祎：不会再投资新能源汽车，机会已转向 AI 与具身智能

Zhou Hongyi, founder of 360 Group, stated on September 15 during the International Summit on BeiDou Scale Application that he 'has learned his lesson' and will no longer invest in new energy vehicles, currently focusing more on opportunities in AI and embodied intelligence. 360 集团创始人周鸿祎 9 月 15 日在北斗规模应用国际峰会期间表示，自己「已经吃过一次亏」，不会再投资新能源汽车，当前更看重 AI 和具身智能领域的机会。

Having learned my lesson once, I definitely won't invest again. The opportunities in artificial intelligence and embodied intelligence might be greater now. 已经吃过一次亏，肯定不会再投资。如今人工智能、具身智能的机会可能更大。

Zhou Hongyi invested in Nezha Auto in 2021, later transferring some equity and ceasing further investment. Nezha Auto delivered over 150,000 new vehicles in 2022, but since then, due to changes in market and operating conditions, production lines in multiple locations have been suspended, and issues of unpaid wages and supplier debts have emerged. 周鸿祎曾在 2021 年投资哪吒汽车，后来转让部分股权并停止追加投资。哪吒汽车在 2022 年交付超过 15 万辆新车，此后因市场和经营状况变化，多地生产线停产，并出现欠薪和供应商欠款问题。

When discussing AI entrepreneurship, Zhou Hongyi proposed 'silicon-based leadership': in the future, individuals will manage multiple Agents simultaneously, and entrepreneurs must not only be able to operate Agents but also build them and help enterprises establish knowledge bases that can be called by intelligent agents. 谈到 AI 创业时，周鸿祎提出「硅基领导力」：未来个人会同时管理多个 Agent，创业者既要会操作 Agent，也要能搭建 Agent，并帮助企业建立可供智能体调用的知识库。

In his keynote speech at the summit, he also applied this judgment to BeiDou application security, advocating for building a security foundation using a 'model-governs-model' approach to address the increased risks from models and intelligent agents as BeiDou services expand. 在峰会主旨演讲中，他还把这一判断落到北斗应用安全上，主张用「以模治模」的方式建设安全底座，以应对北斗服务规模扩大后增加的模型与智能体风险。

苹果推出 Reference Image，从相机传感器开始验证照片真实性

Apple yesterday announced the technical details of Apple Reference Image. This is an optional mode on the main cameras of iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max: a custom sensor signs the raw pixel data before imaging processing, and then Private Cloud Compute generates a reference image with a secure timestamp. Viewers can compare it with subsequent edited versions to determine if the photo still reflects the scene at the time of shooting. 苹果昨天公布 Apple Reference Image 的技术细节。这是 iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max 主摄上的可选模式：定制传感器会在成像处理前为原始像素数据签名，再由 Private Cloud Compute 生成带安全时间戳的参考图像。查看者可以把它与后续编辑版对照，判断照片是否仍反映拍摄当时的画面。

Apple believes that C2PA-like solutions only attach provenance credentials after shooting, and viewers may not be able to detect if a link in the editing chain is compromised. Reference Image also separates real-name identity from photos, supporting the revocation of abnormal credentials without exposing the photographer's identity. 苹果认为，C2PA 类方案在拍摄后才附加溯源凭证，编辑链路中的某个环节被攻破后，查看者可能无法察觉。Reference Image 还将实名身份与照片分离，支持在不暴露拍摄者身份的情况下撤销异常凭证。

This feature initially only supports the main cameras of two Pro models; ultra-wide and telephoto cameras cannot yet generate similar verification data. Reference images need to be generated via Private Cloud Compute, and this process will not be enabled by default for regular photos. 该功能首发仅支持两款 Pro 机型的主摄，超广角和长焦相机尚不能生成同类验证数据。参考图需要通过 Private Cloud Compute 生成，普通照片不会默认启用这套流程。
