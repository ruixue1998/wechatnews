陶哲轩等 25 位菲尔兹奖得主联名发文：警惕 AI 将数学难题异化为评测基准

Terence Tao, Peter Scholze, Hugo Duminil-Copin, Maryna Viazovska, and 21 other Fields Medalists jointly issued a statement yesterday, led by Terence Tao, titled "Severe Misalignment of AI Goals in Mathematics," warning that AI companies' practice of using the conquest of famous mathematical problems as a benchmark for model capability could have a destructive impact on mathematical research and the academic community. 陶哲轩、彼得·舒尔茨、雨果·迪米尼科潘、玛丽娜·维亚佐夫斯卡等 25 位菲尔兹奖得主昨日共同发表声明，并由陶哲轩牵头联合发表《数学领域中 AI 的严重目标错位》一文，警示 AI 公司将攻克著名数学难题作为模型能力衡量基准的做法，可能对数学研究与学术共同体带来破坏性影响。

The statement points out that large language models have recently made rapid progress in solving several open mathematical problems, but there is a disconnect between the goals of AI companies and the core demands of the mathematical community. 声明指出，大语言模型近期在解决多个数学开放问题上取得快速进展，但 AI 公司的目标与数学界的核心诉求存在脱节。

The core value of mathematical research lies in understanding why propositions hold, developing new concepts and tools, and transmitting knowledge, rather than merely producing conclusions on "whether a proposition is true." If models output proofs much faster than humans can understand them, it could lead to an accumulation of conclusions that are difficult to digest, explain, and teach, impacting the training of mathematicians and the system of knowledge transmission. 数学研究的核心价值在于理解命题为何成立、发展新概念与工具并传承知识，而非单纯生产「命题是否为真」的结论。如果模型输出证明的速度远快于人类理解速度，可能导致大量难以被消化、解释和教学的结论堆积，冲击数学家培养与知识传承体系。

Furthermore, some AI-driven mathematical results are published hastily, facing academic norm issues such as a lack of rigorous organization, insufficient existing citations, and disputes over authorship and attribution. The statement does not deny the auxiliary value of AI but explicitly opposes simplifying major mathematical conjectures into a score competition on model leaderboards. 此外，部分由 AI 推动的数学成果发布仓促，面临缺乏严谨梳理、既有引用不充分以及署名和归属权争议等学术规范问题。声明并未否定 AI 的辅助价值，但明确反对将重大数学猜想简化为模型排行榜的分数竞争。

The release of this joint statement is closely related to recent technological developments. On September 8, OpenAI announced that the computational capabilities of an undisclosed internal model had solved the existence and smoothness problem of the Navier-Stokes equations, one of the Millennium Prize Problems, and released a 166-page paper. The result is currently awaiting rigorous verification by the global mathematical community. 该联合声明的发布背景与近期技术动向密切相关。9 月 8 日，OpenAI 宣布其一款未公开内部模型的计算能力已解答千禧年大奖难题之一的纳维斯托克斯方程存在性与光滑性问题，并发布了 166 页的论文，目前该结果正等待全球数学界严格核验。

OpenAI Chief Research Officer Mark Chen and researcher Sébastien Bubeck revealed that the research consumed millions of dollars in computational costs, with the computational investment being approximately 1000 times that of previous mathematical achievements. OpenAI 首席研究官 Mark Chen 与研究员 Sébastien Bubeck 透露，该研究消耗了数百万美元计算成本，计算投入约为此前数学成果的 1000 倍。

苹果 CEO 回应折叠屏为何「迟到」：不赶时间做半成品

Apple CEO John Ternus and Senior Vice President of Worldwide Marketing Greg Joswiak discussed the development philosophy behind iPhone Duo in an exclusive interview with TechRadar, and responded to why Apple is only entering the foldable phone market after years of development. 苹果 CEO 约翰·特努斯与全球营销高级副总裁格雷格·乔斯维亚克在接受 TechRadar 专访时谈及 iPhone Duo 的研发思路，并回应苹果为何在折叠屏手机发展多年后才入场。

Ternus stated that Apple's standard for judging timing has always been whether the product meets the company's requirements for "quality, durability, and design"; Joswiak frankly said that previous foldable products from other manufacturers "were not particularly inspiring." 特努斯表示，苹果判断时机的标准始终是产品是否达到公司对「品质、耐用性和设计」的要求；乔斯维亚克则直言，此前其他厂商的折叠屏产品「并没有特别令人受到启发」。

Our history has never been about rushing to market with a half-finished product. 我们的历史从来都不是为了赶时间，就拿一个半成品进入市场。

In Apple's view, some existing foldable screens, when unfolded, look like "two phones awkwardly stitched together." Therefore, the iPhone Duo ultimately adopted a wider 1:1.4 aspect ratio and redesigned iOS 27 around this form factor. 在苹果看来，现有部分折叠屏展开后像「两台手机生硬地拼在一起」，因此 iPhone Duo 最终采用更宽的 1:1.4 比例，并围绕这一形态重新设计 iOS 27。

Both internal and external screens adopt the same aspect ratio logic, and the control area has been moved to the right side, ensuring that the main operations remain near the right thumb whether the device is folded or unfolded. Apple states that the core of this design is to maintain a continuous user experience between the two screens. 内外屏采用相同的宽高比逻辑，控制区域也被移至右侧，让设备无论折叠还是展开，主要操作都保持在右手拇指附近。苹果称，这套设计的核心是让两块屏幕之间的使用体验保持连续。

Creases are also a key problem Apple aimed to solve. The innermost layer of the iPhone Duo's internal screen uses polymer material and incorporates specially developed nano-texture processing; high-strength glass is also added above and below the screen, working with a hinge composed of over 100 parts to keep the screen flat. Ternus said that when he first saw this solution, his reaction was, "Yes, that's it." 折痕也是苹果重点解决的问题。iPhone Duo 内屏最表层采用聚合物材料，并加入专门开发的纳米纹理处理；屏幕上下还加入高强度玻璃，与超过 100 个零件组成的铰链共同保持屏幕平整。特努斯称，第一次看到这套方案时，他的反应就是「对，就是它」。

对于 iPhone 首次支持 Apple Pencil，特努斯也表示， he never considered "iPhones don't need a stylus" to be a real rule . Apple believes that the Duo's unfolded 7.6-inch screen provides a larger canvas, thus creating new use cases for the Pencil. 对于 iPhone 首次支持 Apple Pencil，特努斯也表示， 他从未将「iPhone 不需要手写笔」视为一条真正的规则 。苹果认为，Duo 展开后的 7.6 英寸屏幕提供了更大的画布，Pencil 因此有了新的使用场景。

Joswiak also revealed that the project used a "very funny" internal codename and implemented extra confidentiality measures, but outsiders had previously guessed its screen ratio through leaks. 乔斯维亚克还透露，这个项目曾使用一个「很好笑」的内部代号，并采取了额外保密措施，不过外界此前仍通过泄密猜中了它的屏幕比例。

微软计划到 2032 年把数据中心容量扩至 38 GW 以上

According to Bloomberg, Microsoft is planning to increase its global data center capacity from the current approximately 12 GW to over 38 GW by 2032. The statistics include capacity built by Microsoft itself and long-term leased capacity, but do not include computing power temporarily rented from some new cloud service providers. 据彭博社报道，微软正规划把全球数据中心容量从目前约 12 GW 提升到 2032 年的 38 GW 以上。统计包括微软自建和长期租赁的容量，不包含向部分新型云服务商临时租用的算力。

Sources say that the recent shortage of computing power has forced Microsoft to decline some AI and cloud businesses. The expansion plan may still be adjusted based on demand, power supply, and engineering progress, but it provides Microsoft's current internal measure of computing power demand for the next six years. 知情人士称，近年的算力短缺已经迫使微软拒绝部分 AI 与云业务。扩建规划仍可能随需求、供电和工程进度调整，但它给出了微软目前对未来六年算力需求的内部尺度。

九部门发布新能源汽车「十五五」规划，2030 年自动驾驶汽车将规模应用

Nine departments, including the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, released the '15th Five-Year Plan for the Development of the Intelligent Connected New Energy Vehicle Industry.' The plan proposes that by 2030, the proportion of new energy passenger vehicles and commercial vehicles in their respective total new car sales in China will reach 70% and 40% respectively, and vehicles with autonomous driving capabilities will achieve large-scale application. 工业和信息化部等九部门发布《智能网联新能源汽车产业发展「十五五」规划》。规划提出，到 2030 年，国内新能源乘用车和商用车占各自新车总销量的比例分别达到 70% 和 40%，具备自动驾驶功能的汽车实现规模应用。

Regarding energy efficiency targets, the average fuel consumption of passenger vehicles will be reduced to approximately 3.3 liters per 100 kilometers, and the average electricity consumption of pure electric passenger vehicles will be reduced to approximately 11.5 kilowatt-hours per 100 kilometers; highly autonomous driving will be achieved on expressways, urban expressways, and some urban roads. The plan also deploys 5 aspects, 17 tasks, 4 major projects, and 3 key actions, including key technology breakthroughs, safety systems, 'AI + Automotive,' and application promotion. 能效目标方面，乘用车平均燃料消耗量降至每百公里约 3.3 升，纯电动乘用车平均电耗降至每百公里约 11.5 千瓦时；高速公路、城市快速路和部分城市道路将实现高度自动驾驶。规划同时部署 5 方面 17 项任务、4 个重大工程和 3 个重点行动，包括关键技术攻关、安全体系、「人工智能 + 汽车」及应用推广。

These results further shift Oracle's growth focus towards cloud infrastructure.
