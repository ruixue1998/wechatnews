曝苹果进军智能家居，拟于 10 月 13 日推出家庭中枢

According to a Bloomberg report by Mark Gurman on September 30, Apple plans to launch a smart home product line on October 13, centered around a home control hub codenamed J490, while also updating the HomePod mini and Apple TV. The new products will showcase the capabilities of the new Siri AI, and Apple declined to comment on this. 据彭博社 Mark Gurman 9 月 30 日报道，苹果计划于 10 月 13 日推出智能家居产品线，核心是代号 J490 的家庭控制中枢，同时更新 HomePod mini 和 Apple TV。新品将用于展示新版 Siri AI 的能力，苹果对此拒绝置评。

The home hub is reportedly equipped with an approximately 6-inch square screen that can be placed on a countertop; another version, codenamed J491, is for wall mounting. The device identifies different family members via voice or facial recognition, automatically displaying corresponding contacts, messages, calendars, and app layouts, while guest mode hides personal information. The reliability of facial recognition is lower than iPhone's Face ID, and users can request secondary authentication via iPhone; if recognition fails, the phone can also assist. 家庭中枢据报配备约 6 英寸方形屏幕，可放置在台面；另一款代号 J491 的版本用于壁挂。设备通过声音或面部识别不同家庭成员，自动显示对应的联系人、消息、日历与应用布局，访客模式会隐藏个人信息。面部识别的可靠性低于 iPhone 的 Face ID，用户可要求通过 iPhone 进行二次认证，识别失败时也可由手机辅助。

The hub supports controlling connected devices such as thermostats and door locks, and can also be used for FaceTime, indoor intercom, music playback, and iCloud photo display. The device has no battery and requires continuous USB-C power; the desktop version's screen can be manually tilted back and forth, and the base has built-in microphones and speakers. 中枢支持控制温控器、门锁等联网设备，还可用于 FaceTime、室内对讲、音乐播放及 iCloud 照片展示。设备没有电池，需要持续接入 USB-C 电源；桌面版本的屏幕可手动前后倾斜，底座内置麦克风与扬声器。

The HomePod mini and Apple TV will retain their existing appearance, adopting faster processors to support the Siri AI model. The HomePod mini is reportedly adding green and pink colors. Apple is also developing an upgraded hub with a larger screen and a robotic arm, but this product is not among the first devices planned for this release. HomePod mini 与 Apple TV 将延续现有外观，换用更快的处理器以支持 Siri AI 模型。HomePod mini 据报新增绿色、粉色。苹果还在开发配备更大屏幕和机械臂的升级中枢，但该产品不属于此次计划发布的首批设备。

iPhone 18 Pro 中国首销 3 天销量超上代首周，苹果周份额达 33%

Counterpoint Research released its weekly tracking data for Chinese smartphones on September 30: The iPhone 18 Pro series went on sale on September 18, with sales in the first 3 days being 12% higher than the first week of sales for the iPhone 17 Pro series. These two comparison periods have different lengths and should not be interpreted as a year-on-year comparison of two full first weeks. Apple topped the weekly sales chart in the Chinese market during the 38th week, from September 14 to 20, with a 33% share. Counterpoint Research 9 月 30 日公布中国智能手机周度追踪数据：iPhone 18 Pro 系列于 9 月 18 日开售，上市首 3 天销量比 iPhone 17 Pro 系列上市首周高 12%。这两个比较时段长度不同，不能理解为两个完整首周的同比。苹果在 9 月 14 日至 20 日的第 38 周登上中国市场周销量榜首，份额为 33%。

Ivan Lam, Senior Analyst at Counterpoint, believes that the delayed launch of the iPhone 18 base model may have caused some consumers to switch to the Pro series; the price increases of some Chinese brand flagship models have also narrowed the price gap between them and the iPhone Pro series. The agency also pointed out that domestic mobile phone weekly sales have continued to decline by double digits year-on-year since July, and with new products launching in September, the agency expects the market to have an opportunity for month-on-month recovery. Counterpoint 高级分析师 Ivan Lam 认为，iPhone 18 基础版延后上市，可能使部分消费者转向 Pro 系列；部分中国品牌旗舰机型涨价，也缩小了它们与 iPhone Pro 系列的价格差距。该机构同时指出，国内手机周销量自 7 月起持续同比双位数下降，9 月新品陆续上市，机构预计市场有机会环比恢复。

Google 发布 Gemini 4 Argon，先向网络防御者开放测试

Google released its next-generation flagship model, Gemini 4 Argon, on September 30. The first batch is open to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind program, and will gradually expand to developers, enterprises, and consumers, with priority given to paid API clients and Google AI Ultra subscribers. Google 9 月 30 日发布新一代旗舰模型 Gemini 4 Argon，首批通过 Fairwind 计划向受信任的网络防御者开放，后续将逐步扩展至开发者、企业和消费者，付费 API 客户及 Google AI Ultra 订阅者优先。

Argon is designed for professional tasks such as software engineering, legal, and finance, as well as cybersecurity defense. Google has increased its output limit from the previous 64,000 tokens to 1 million tokens, supporting longer inference and task execution. Argon 面向软件工程、法律与金融等专业任务，以及网络安全防御。谷歌将其输出上限从此前的 6.4 万 token 提高到 100 万 token，支持更长的推理与任务执行。

Google states that its internal engineers have used it to debug code, migrate large codebases, and design algorithms; in the real-world long-task software engineering test DeepSWE v1.1, the model scored 77.9%. Google 称，内部工程师已用它调试代码、迁移大型代码库及设计算法；在真实长任务软件工程测试 DeepSWE v1.1 中，模型得分为 77.9%。

However, according to sources cited by Bloomberg, while Argon performed outstandingly in various benchmark tests, it frequently encountered obstacles in real-world business code, especially in front-end design that determines app appearance and interaction. 不过，据彭博社援引知情人士的说法，Argon 在各类基准测试中表现突出，但在真实业务代码中频频受阻，尤其是决定 App 外观和交互的前端设计。

Some employees believe that Anthropic's Fable and OpenAI's Astra are progressing faster, while others believe the new version has caught up with leading labs. 部分员工认为 Anthropic 的 Fable 和 OpenAI 的 Astra 进步更快，也有员工认为新版本已追上头部实验室。

A Google spokesperson responded, stating that the claim "Gemini 4 performs poorly in areas like programming" is inaccurate. DeepMind head Koray Kavukcuoglu previously said that Google will always be at the forefront. Google 发言人回应称，「Gemini 4 在编程等领域表现不佳」的说法并不准确。DeepMind 负责人 Koray Kavukcuoglu 此前表示，Google 将永远站在最前沿。

The initial price after the API launch is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input being 95% cheaper than regular input; after the promotional period, input and output prices will be $4 and $20 respectively. API 上线后的初期价格为每百万输入 token 2 美元、输出 token 10 美元，缓存输入比普通输入便宜 95%；优惠期结束后，输入和输出价格分别为 4 美元、20 美元。

机构预计 iPhone Duo 年内销量约 600 万部

Bloomberg reported on September 30 that Counterpoint has for the first time provided a sales forecast for Apple's foldable phone, iPhone Duo: approximately 6 million units are expected to be sold in 2026. The device is expected to launch in October, leaving only one quarter for sales this year. 彭博社 9 月 30 日报道，Counterpoint 首次给出苹果折叠屏手机 iPhone Duo 的销量预测：2026 年预计售出约 600 万部。该机预计于 10 月上市，留给今年销售的时间只有一个季度。

Counterpoint analyst Ivan Lam stated that actual sales depend on how quickly Apple can ramp up production. The report lists production progress as a key variable, so this figure is an institutional forecast, not Apple's orders or actual shipments. Counterpoint 分析师 Ivan Lam 表示，实际销量取决于苹果提升产量的速度。报道将生产进度列为关键变量，因此这一数字是机构预测，尚非苹果订单或实际出货量。

IDC estimates that global foldable phone shipments will reach 22.9 million units this year, a year-on-year increase of approximately 13%; the agency stated that without the entry of iPhone Duo, this market might have declined this year. The estimates from the two agencies are for Apple's new device and the entire foldable category, respectively, and cannot be directly used to calculate market share with the same standard. IDC 预计，今年全球折叠屏手机出货量将达到 2290 万部，同比增长约 13%；该机构称，若没有 iPhone Duo 入市，今年这一市场原本可能下滑。两家机构的估算分别针对苹果新机和整个折叠屏品类，不能直接当作同一口径的市场份额计算。

DeepSeek 开源昇腾平台基础组件，覆盖算子、计算与跨卡通信

DeepSeek announced on September 30 the open-sourcing of infrastructure components for Huawei's Ascend computing platform, including the TileLang high-level language compilation tool, computing libraries, and distributed communication libraries. These components correspond to the tools previously used on the Nvidia platform, allowing developers to write operators, perform matrix computations, and achieve cross-device communication on the Ascend platform. DeepSeek 9 月 30 日宣布，开源面向华为昇腾算力平台的基础设施组件，包括 TileLang 高级语言编译工具、计算库和分布式通信库。这批组件对应其此前在英伟达平台使用的工具，供开发者在昇腾平台编写算子、完成矩阵计算和跨设备通信。

DeepSeek stated that the Ascend version of TileLang encapsulates underlying Ascend C instructions; the TileLang operators used in its training already have corresponding implementations on the Ascend platform. DeepGEMM, DeepEP, TileKernels, FlashMLA, and DeepSelect, opened concurrently, cover matrix operations, cross-device communication, general vector and memory access, sparse attention, and data filtering, respectively. DeepSeek 称，TileLang 昇腾版本封装了 Ascend C 底层指令；其训练中使用的 TileLang 算子，已在昇腾平台有对应实现。同期开放的 DeepGEMM、DeepEP、TileKernels、FlashMLA 和 DeepSelect，分别覆盖矩阵运算、跨设备通信、常规向量与访存、稀疏注意力和数据筛选。

DeepSeek also disclosed that the Huawei team participated in computing and communication optimization on the Ascend platform, and both sides are advancing a 128-card super-node solution based on Ascend 950. DeepSeek 还披露，华为团队参与了昇腾平台上的计算和通信优化，双方正推进基于昇腾 950 的 128 卡超节点方案。

Anthropic IPO 材料曝光，近半收入来自亚马逊与Google 云渠道

According to a Reuters report on September 29, Anthropic's filing materials prepared for its IPO show that approximately 47% of its revenue in 2025 will come from Amazon and Google's cloud marketplace channels. Both companies are simultaneously investors in Anthropic, computing power providers, and competitors with their own AI products. 据路透社 9 月 29 日报道，Anthropic 为 IPO 准备的申报材料显示，2025 年约 47% 的收入来自亚马逊和谷歌的云市场渠道。两家公司同时是 Anthropic 的投资者、算力供应商，也是拥有自研 AI 产品的竞争者。

The report states that Anthropic's revenue in 2025 will be close to $4.6 billion, with operating losses exceeding $8 billion. Through contracts sold via the aforementioned cloud channels, Anthropic recognizes revenue on a gross basis, and distribution fees paid to partners are recorded under sales, marketing, and partner expenses; this portion of fees is approximately $351 million. Anthropic stated that it is the principal in these transactions and that this accounting treatment follows established accounting principles. 报道称，Anthropic 2025 年收入接近 46 亿美元，经营亏损超过 80 亿美元。通过上述云渠道销售的合同，Anthropic 按总额确认收入，将向合作伙伴支付的分销费用计入销售、营销与合作伙伴支出；这部分费用约为 3.51 亿美元。Anthropic 表示，自己是相关交易的主要责任方，该处理方式遵循既定会计原则。

The proportion of revenue from cloud marketplace channels has risen from 11% in 2023 and 32% in 2024 to 47% in 2025. These channels allow Anthropic to reach existing customers of cloud service providers, but the filing materials also warn that partners are aware of its pricing and customer information, and changes in their relationship could affect the business. Reuters also stated that in 2025, two unnamed customers each contributed 12% of revenue, and many large customers do not have long-term contract constraints, allowing them to reduce or cease spending. 云市场渠道占收入的比例，已从 2023 年的 11%、2024 年的 32% 升至 2025 年的 47%。渠道能让 Anthropic 接触云服务商的既有客户，但申报材料也提示：合作伙伴了解其定价及客户信息，双方关系变化可能影响业务。路透还称，2025 年两名未具名客户各贡献 12% 的收入，许多大客户没有长期合同约束，可减少或停止支出。

集邦：第四季度 DRAM 合约价预计再涨 10%—15%，消费端仍承压

TrendForce released its memory market research on September 30, predicting that general-purpose DRAM contract prices will increase by 10%-15% quarter-on-quarter in Q4 this year, and NAND Flash contract prices will rise by 15%-20%. Memory manufacturers continue to shift advanced processes and capacity towards server applications, while consumer electronics demand remains weak, still facing supply and cost pressures. 集邦咨询 9 月 30 日发布存储器市场研究，预计今年第四季度一般型 DRAM 合约价环比上涨 10%—15%，NAND Flash 合约价上涨 15%—20%。存储器厂商持续把先进制程和产能转向服务器应用，消费电子需求偏弱，仍面临供应和成本压力。

The report points out that low-priced inventory for mobile phone brands is gradually depleting, and mobile DRAM quotas remain tight; some PC brands have begun to reduce the SSD capacity of mainstream models to lower overall machine costs. Laptop manufacturers are also concerned about a further tightening of DRAM supply in 2027 and are maintaining active procurement. 报告指出，手机品牌的低价库存逐步用尽，移动 DRAM 配额依然吃紧；部分 PC 品牌为降低整机成本，开始调低主流机型的 SSD 容量。笔电厂商同时担心 2027 年 DRAM 供应进一步收紧，维持积极采购。

Enterprise SSDs are the only category in the report expected to see an expanded increase in Q4. Cloud service providers are increasing investment in AI inference infrastructure, and the real-time retrieval and caching demands brought by agents are driving the procurement of high-capacity QLC SSDs, with much of the new supply already locked in. The aforementioned increases are TrendForce's predictions for the component contract market, not a uniform price increase for mobile phones, computers, or retail hard drives. 企业级 SSD 是报告中唯一预计第四季度涨幅扩大的品类。云服务商加码 AI 推理基础设施，智能体带来的实时检索与缓存需求推动高容量 QLC SSD 采购，而新增供应多已被提前锁定。上述涨幅均为集邦对元件合约市场的预测，并非手机、电脑或零售硬盘的统一涨价幅度。

快手成立「企业 AI 生产力」组织，于越转任可灵 CEO

Kuaishou announced on September 30 the establishment of a cross-business unit virtual organization called "Enterprise AI Productivity" to unify the construction of general agents, enterprise context systems, and internal information systems. This organization is composed of the Smart Office Platform Department and the AI Productivity Center, led by Wang Xu, head of the Smart Office Platform Department. 快手 9 月 30 日宣布成立跨事业部虚拟组织「企业 AI 生产力」，统一建设通用 Agent、企业上下文体系与内部信息系统。该组织由智能办公平台部和 AI 生产力中心组成，由智能办公平台部负责人王诩牵头。

According to reports from National Business Daily, the new organization is responsible for both the stable operation of basic office systems and the overall planning of AI infrastructure, breaking down barriers between data and systems. Kuaishou had previously launched internal tools such as the general productivity intelligent agent MyFlicker and the data analysis intelligent agent Data Agent; as of June this year, the proportion of employees using various self-developed AI Agents exceeded 92%, and the AI code contribution rate of R&D technical personnel reached 60%. These percentages are based on company disclosures. 据每日经济新闻报道，新组织既负责基础办公系统稳定运行，也统筹 AI 基础设施，打通数据与系统之间的壁垒。快手此前已推出通用生产力智能体 MyFlicker、数据分析智能体 Data Agent 等内部工具；截至今年 6 月，使用各类自研 AI Agent 的员工占比超过 92%，研发技术人员的 AI 代码贡献率达到 60%。这些比例来自公司披露。

On the same day, Kuaishou adjusted the management division of Keling AI: Founder and CEO Cheng Yixiao will concurrently serve as the head of the Community Science Line, while former head Yu Yue will transition to become a director and CEO of Keling AI company, focusing on the operational management of Keling AI. Cheng Yixiao stated in an all-hands letter that Keling remains a core part of Kuaishou's AI strategy and will continue to collaborate with other businesses. 同日，快手调整可灵 AI 的管理分工：创始人兼 CEO 程一笑将兼任社区科学线负责人，原负责人于越转任可灵 AI 公司董事兼 CEO，专注于可灵 AI 的经营管理。程一笑在全员信中表示，可灵仍是快手 AI 战略的核心布局，将继续与其他业务协同。

The new version increases the CLTC pure electric range from the current models' 210 kilometers to 300 kilometers, with a combined range of 1300 kilometers. 奇瑞 iCAR V27 长续航版于 9 月 30 日上市，推出两款四驱车型：猎鹰 500 售 18.98 万元，猎鹰 700 售 20.38 万元。新版本将 CLTC 纯电续航从在售车型的 210 公里提升至 300 公里，综合续航为 1300 公里。

The vehicle is equipped with a 51.2kWh extended-range dedicated battery, using CATL lithium iron phosphate cells. The official 30%-80% charging time is 17 minutes, and it supports 6kW external discharge. The front seats feature zero-gravity functionality, electric leg rests, and massage functions, and the roof is equipped with a dual panoramic sunroof. 车辆搭载 51.2kWh 增程专用电池，采用宁德时代磷酸铁锂电芯，官方给出的 30%—80% 充电时间为 17 分钟，并支持 6kW 外放电。前排增加零重力座椅、电动腿托和按摩功能，车顶配备双天幕。

Horizon HSD V2.1, released on the same day, will be pushed to iCAR V27 owners in batches starting October 8. The update includes all-scenario reverse parking Beta, custom parking space remote entry/exit, physical key remote parking, and the integration of trajectory reversal with parking assist functions. The reverse parking function is still part of assisted driving and is in the Beta stage; the release time differs from when owners actually receive the upgrade. 同日发布的地平线 HSD V2.1 将于 10 月 8 日起向 iCAR V27 车主分批推送。更新加入全场景倒车 Beta、自定义车位遥控泊入泊出、实体钥匙遥控泊车，以及循迹倒车与泊车辅助衔接等功能。倒车功能仍属于辅助驾驶，且处于 Beta 阶段；发布时间与车主实际收到升级的时间不同。

B 站开源 Index-Translate，支持 150 种语言并针对社区热梗优化

On September 30, Bilibili open-sourced the Index-Translate family of multilingual translation models. Text translation supports 150 languages, offering 2B, 9B, and 35B-A3B preview models, built upon Qwen3.5. The official team lists community context, format constraints, and long-text consistency as key optimization priorities. B 站 9 月 30 日开源 Index-Translate 多语言翻译模型家族，文本翻译支持 150 种语言，提供 2B、9B 及 35B-A3B 预览版模型，基于 Qwen3.5 构建。官方把社区语境、格式约束和长文本一致性列为优化重点。

In the examples provided by the project, 「狒瘾犯了」 (literally 'baboon addiction struck') was translated according to the player context of Final Fantasy XIV as 'wanting to play FFXIV,' rather than being literally understood as an addiction to baboons. The model also supports preserving JSON, tag, and topic formats, reducing issues of structural damage during translation. These demonstrations are from the project's examples. 项目给出的例子中，「狒瘾犯了」被按《最终幻想 XIV》的玩家语境翻译为想玩 FFXIV，而没有照字面理解成对狒狒上瘾。模型也支持保留 JSON、标签和话题格式，减少翻译时破坏结构的问题。这些展示来自项目方示例。

The same model family also includes subtitle and speech translation, syllable-length-constrained translation, and long document translation. The 150 languages correspond to text models, while speech and document models each have their own language ranges; larger parameter versions are currently still marked as preview versions. 同一模型家族还包括字幕与语音翻译、受音节长度约束的翻译，以及长文档翻译。150 种语言对应文本模型，语音和文档模型各有自己的语言范围；较大参数版本目前仍标为预览版。

Ling-3.1-flash 开放体验，强化长任务与代码执行能力

On September 30, Ant Group's Bailing announced an upgrade to Ling-3.1-flash, focusing on code writing, tool invocation, and multi-step tasks. The model adopts a hybrid attention architecture with a total of 560B parameters and 25B active parameters, and enhances its sustained execution capability in complex tasks through reinforcement learning training. 蚂蚁百灵 9 月 30 日宣布升级 Ling-3.1-flash，重点面向代码编写、工具调用和多步骤任务。模型采用混合注意力架构，总参数为 560B，激活参数为 25B，并通过强化学习训练提升复杂任务中的持续执行能力。

Bailing showcased cases where the model ran programming tasks for extended periods: writing a Lua native compiler from scratch using C++, working continuously for about 17 hours, and ultimately passing 178 out of 182 tests. The team also used it to rewrite Pyright, and after all 2279 tests passed, measured an increase in running speed. These results are from Bailing's own demonstrations and tests. 百灵展示了模型长时间运行编程任务的案例：从头用 C++ 编写 Lua 原生编译器，连续工作约 17 小时，最终通过 182 项测试中的 178 项；团队还用它改写 Pyright，并在 2279 项测试全部通过后测得运行速度提高。这些结果来自百灵自己的演示与测试。

Ling-3.1-flash has launched a two-week free trial on the experience platform, offering 256K context during the free period. Bailing plans to introduce paid services, open 1M context, and open-source the model after the free trial; the open-sourcing mentioned in the current announcement is part of a subsequent plan. Ling-3.1-flash 已在体验平台开启为期两周的免费试用，免费期提供 256K 上下文。百灵计划在免费期结束后推出付费服务、开放 1M 上下文并开源模型；当前公告中的开源属于后续计划。
