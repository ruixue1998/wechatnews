OpenAI 重构模型规范，将「忠于人类意图」列为首项准则

Recently, OpenAI updated its Model Spec, reorganizing the principles of model behavior around five values: faithfulness to human intent, harm reduction, honesty and transparency, safeguarding human autonomy, and cautious care. The specification is aimed at GPT models powering products like ChatGPT and APIs, publicly outlining how the company intends to shape model behavior. 近日，OpenAI 更新 Model Spec，以五项价值重新组织模型行为原则：忠于人类意图、减少伤害、诚实透明、保障人的自主选择，以及审慎关怀。规范面向驱动 ChatGPT 和 API 等产品的 GPT 模型，公开说明公司希望如何塑造模型行为。

The new version places what users and developers want to achieve at its core, while retaining instruction hierarchies and safety boundaries. For tasks where the goal is unclear, the model should understand intent based on context; for facts, evidence, uncertainties, capabilities, and actual actions taken, it should provide truthful explanations, allowing people to evaluate its work. 新版把用户和开发者想完成什么放在核心位置，同时保留指令层级与安全边界。对于目标尚不明确的任务，模型应结合上下文理解意图；对事实、证据、不确定性、能力和实际采取的行动，则应如实说明，让人能够评估其工作。

The specification also specifically discusses the tension between immediate instructions and long-term goals: models can prompt overlooked consequences and help users think, but they cannot treat their predictions of choices users will make in the future as decisions users have already made. OpenAI states that online models have not yet fully met the specification requirements, and the company will continue to adjust training and systems. 规范还专门讨论了即时指令与长期目标之间的张力：模型可以提示被忽略的后果、帮助用户思考，但不能把自己预测用户将来会作出的选择，视为用户已经作出的决定。OpenAI 表示，线上模型尚未完全达到规范要求，公司将继续调整训练与系统。
