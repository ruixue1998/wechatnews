库克谈 Apple Watch 未来：帮助用户掌握健康信息

On October 12, during an interview with iFanr in Beijing, Cook was asked about the future development of Apple Watch in the next decade. Cook stated that Apple Watch aims to be everyone's "personal guardian" for health, helping users live a healthier day. 10 月 12 日，在北京接受爱范儿采访时，库克被问及 Apple Watch 在未来十年会如何发展。库克表示，Apple Watch 要成为每个人健康的「个人守护者」，帮助用户过上更健康的一天。

Cook cited sleep apnea as an example: many patients are unaware they have this problem, and Apple hopes to alert them so they can seek the help they need. 库克以睡眠呼吸暂停为例：许多患者并不知道自己存在这一问题，苹果希望提醒他们，让他们能够寻求所需的帮助。

Cook stated that Apple hopes to put information that users previously couldn't access into their hands, helping them sleep better, exercise more, stand more, burn more calories; and when needed, go to the hospital or seek a doctor's help. 库克表示，苹果希望把用户原本无法获得的信息交到他们手中，帮助他们睡得更好、多运动、多站立、消耗更多热量；在需要时，去医院或寻求医生帮助。

三季度全球手机出货下降 20%，苹果份额升至 22%

Counterpoint Research released preliminary statistics showing that global smartphone shipments in Q3 2026 decreased by 20% year-on-year, falling to their lowest third-quarter level since 2012. Persistent memory shortages, along with rising DRAM and NAND costs, have constrained manufacturers' production and supply capabilities. Counterpoint Research 公布初步统计，2026 年第三季度全球智能手机出货量同比下降 20%，降至 2012 年以来最低的第三季度水平。内存持续短缺，以及 DRAM、NAND 成本上涨，压缩了厂商的生产与供货能力。

Samsung's shipments decreased by 3% year-on-year, but its market share rose to 23%, maintaining its number one position; Apple's share reached 22%, exceeding 20% for the first time in a third quarter, though its shipments still declined year-on-year. Counterpoint attributed part of Apple's decline to the absence of the base model iPhone 18 in the quarter, with channel distribution scale being smaller than the previous year's complete iPhone 17 series. 三星出货量同比下降 3%，但市场份额升至 23%，继续排名第一；苹果份额达到 22%，首次在第三季度超过 20%，其出货量仍同比下滑。Counterpoint 将苹果的下滑部分归因于当季缺少基础款 iPhone 18，渠道铺货规模小于前一年完整的 iPhone 17 系列。

Xiaomi's shipments decreased by over 30%, with its share falling back to 10%; OPPO and vivo both saw shipments decline by over 20%, with shares of 9% and 8% respectively. The statistics use a sell-in methodology (shipments from manufacturers to channels), where OPPO includes OnePlus and realme. 小米出货量降幅超过 30%，份额回落至 10%；OPPO、vivo 出货量均下降超过 20%，份额分别为 9% 和 8%。统计采用厂商向渠道出货的 sell-in 口径，其中 OPPO 包含一加和 realme。

The agency pointed out that under memory cost pressure, manufacturers are raising prices and reducing low-profit models and markets, while consumers are extending their phone replacement cycles and increasingly considering refurbished devices. High-end demand remains relatively strong, while price-sensitive markets face greater pressure. 机构指出，厂商在内存成本压力下提高价格、收缩低利润机型与市场，消费者则延长换机周期、更多考虑翻新设备。高端需求相对坚挺，价格敏感市场承受更大压力。

人脸也能「明码标价」：授权平台报价 500 至 10000 元，不少人上传后一单未接

Nandu N Video released an investigation on October 12. Reporters tested face authorization platforms such as Xinzhao AIGC, Mirafans Jingjie, and Yuanxiang Xinsheng, finding that authorization quotes vary by use, ranging from 500 yuan to 10,000 yuan. 南都 N 视频 10 月 12 日发布调查，记者实测新爪 AIGC、Mirafans 镜界、元相新生等人脸授权平台发现，授权报价按用途划分，从 500 元至 10000 元不等。

Buyers can use authorized images for advertisements, film and television dramas, games, and other scenarios. Some platforms allow uploaders to individually select whether to accept themes such as "death scenes," "intimate emotional scenes," and "passionate scenes." 买家可将获授权形象用于广告、影视剧、游戏等场景，部分平台允许上传者逐项选择是否接受「死亡戏」「情感亲密戏」「激情戏」等题材。

Xinzhao AIGC requires buyers to pay and submit authorization information, which is then confirmed by the authorizing party within 24 hours. Only after approval are authorization documents generated and materials made available. The platform responded on October 8 that authorization is only for a single project and does not automatically extend to sequels, spin-offs, or derivative works, nor does existing authorization include model training. 新爪 AIGC 要求买家付款并提交授权信息后，由授权方在 24 小时内确认，通过后才生成授权文件、开放素材。平台 10 月 8 日回应称，授权仅针对单个项目，不会自动延伸到续作、番外或衍生作品，现有授权也不包含模型训练。

The platform promotes continuous income generation, but Nandu observed in user groups that many people reported not receiving orders after uploading their faces. Liu Xiao, founder of Yuanxiang Xinsheng, stated that the demand from production companies is currently not high, and the platform cannot guarantee every user will get an order; their process requires the production company to purchase specific project authorization, and then the individual confirms acceptance. A certificate of entry into the database does not equate to the production company having obtained usage permission. 平台宣传持续创收，但南都在用户群中看到，不少人反映上传人脸后一直未接到订单。元相新生创始人刘啸表示，制片方需求量目前不多，平台无法保证每个用户都有单；其流程要求制作方购买具体项目授权，再由本人确认是否接受，入库证书并不等于制作方已取得使用许可。

The investigation pointed out that unauthorized use, impersonation, reuse, or out-of-scope use may still occur at various stages of material upload, content generation, production, and distribution; authorization records, material versions, and project-by-project confirmation can provide a basis for accountability, but whether the actual usage chain is complete remains a problem for the platform. 调查指出，素材上传、内容生成、制作和发行各环节仍可能发生盗用、冒用、复用或超范围使用；授权记录、素材版本和逐项目确认能留下追责依据，实际使用链路是否完整仍是平台面临的问题。

9 月乘用车零售同比下降 28%，新能源渗透率升至 67%

The China Passenger Car Association announced on October 12 that national passenger car retail sales in September were 1.618 million units, a year-on-year decrease of 28% and a month-on-month increase of 5%. Cumulative retail sales from January to September were 13.334 million units, a year-on-year decrease of 21.7%. 乘联分会 10 月 12 日公布，9 月全国乘用车市场零售 161.8 万辆，同比下降 28%，环比增长 5%。1 至 9 月累计零售 1333.4 万辆，同比下降 21.7%。

Domestic new energy passenger vehicle retail sales in September decreased by 16.7% year-on-year, but the retail penetration rate rose to a record high of 67%. Retail sales of conventional fuel vehicles decreased by 43% year-on-year; among them, ordinary hybrid vehicles increased by 25% year-on-year. The increase in new energy vehicle proportion occurred simultaneously with an overall sales decline, with fuel vehicles experiencing a larger contraction. 国内新能源乘用车 9 月零售同比下降 16.7%，但零售渗透率升至 67%，创历史新高。常规燃油车零售同比下降 43%；其中普通混合动力车同比增长 25%。新能源占比提升与整体销量下降同时发生，燃油车收缩幅度更大。

The brand structure continues to shift towards independent brands: in September, independent brands retailed 1.13 million vehicles, accounting for 69.8% of the domestic retail market; mainstream joint venture brands retailed 310,000 vehicles, and luxury cars retailed 170,000 vehicles. The report points out that high oil prices have a significant impact on fuel vehicles, and dealers are more cautious in purchasing goods. Most dealers believe that the peak season effect of "Golden September and Silver October" this year is weaker than in previous years. 品牌结构继续向自主品牌倾斜：9 月自主品牌零售 113 万辆，占国内零售市场的 69.8%；主流合资品牌零售 31 万辆，豪华车零售 17 万辆。报告指出，高油价对燃油车的冲击明显，经销商进货更谨慎，多数经销商认为今年「金九银十」的旺季效应弱于往年。

大众 CEO 呼吁加强合作，应对中国车企竞争

On the eve of the Paris Motor Show, Volkswagen Group CEO Oliver Blume called on France and Germany to strengthen cooperation to enhance the European automotive industry's ability to face Chinese competition. At the "For Europe" event on October 11, he urged companies to improve competitiveness and also called on policymakers to create suitable conditions for European industries. 在巴黎车展前夕，大众汽车集团 CEO 奥博穆呼吁法国与德国加强合作，提升欧洲汽车业面对中国竞争的能力。10 月 11 日的「For Europe」活动上，他要求企业提高竞争力，也要求政策制定者为欧洲产业创造合适的条件。

According to a live report by The Guardian, the pressures listed by Blume include high energy costs, inflation suppressing consumer demand, the need to accelerate development, and competition from Chinese car manufacturers. He supports the EU's proposed "Made in Europe" rules, advocating for companies that invest and conduct R&D in Europe to be rewarded, and emphasizing that companies entering the European market should also create jobs and value locally. 据《卫报》现场报道，奥博穆列出的压力包括能源成本高、通胀压低消费需求、开发速度需要加快，以及中国车企的竞争。他支持欧盟拟议的「欧洲制造」规则，主张让在欧洲投资和研发的企业得到回报，并强调进入欧洲市场的企业也应在当地创造就业与价值。

Data released by Volkswagen on October 12 also showed pressure in China: 405,800 vehicles were delivered in China in the third quarter, a year-on-year decrease of 38.5%; global deliveries during the same period were 1.8853 million vehicles, a year-on-year decrease of 14.3%. 大众 10 月 12 日公布的数据也显示了在华压力：第三季度在中国交付 40.58 万辆，同比下降 38.5%；同期全球交付 188.53 万辆，同比下降 14.3%。

字节海外短剧应用 PineDrama 累计下载破亿

According to an original report by Investment Community citing Sensor Tower data, as of September 2026, ByteDance's overseas short-drama app PineDrama has accumulated over 100 million downloads, with approximately 7 million daily active users, becoming one of the fastest-growing products in the overseas short-drama market. 据投资界原创报道援引 Sensor Tower 数据，截至 2026 年 9 月，字节跳动旗下海外短剧应用 PineDrama 累计下载量突破 1 亿，日活跃用户约 700 万，成为海外短剧市场中增长较快的产品之一。

PineDrama primarily targets markets such as Brazil, the United States, and Indonesia, supporting TikTok account login. It adopts a free-to-watch, ad-monetization model: users jump from TikTok short-drama clips to the standalone app, watch ads, and then continue watching the series, similar to the business model of domestic Hongguo short dramas. PineDrama 主要面向巴西、美国和印尼等市场，支持 TikTok 账号登录。它采用免费观看、广告变现的模式：用户从 TikTok 的短剧片段跳转到独立应用，观看广告后继续追剧，与国内红果短剧的商业路径相近。

The report points out that Brazil and Indonesia collectively contributed about 82% of new downloads, and PineDrama has also built a production network, recruited actors, and produced local stories there. Sensor Tower's Q2 market report previously listed it as an app with prominent download growth; this 100 million milestone reflects cumulative installations, not equivalent to daily active users or paying users. 报道指出，巴西和印尼合计贡献了约 82% 的新增下载，PineDrama 也在当地搭建制片网络、招募演员并制作本土故事。Sensor Tower 的第二季度市场报告此前已将其列为下载增长突出的应用；此次破亿数据体现的是累计安装量，不等同于日活或付费用户数。

Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney 在 X 上表示，公司正在进行重大转型，调配开发者、重整工作方式，以加快 AI 辅助开发。他称，目标是让开发者制作更多、更好的内容，并将相关能力带给使用 Unreal 的团队。

Epic 传播负责人 Jake Jones 向彭博社回应，公司正在采用「AI 加速开发」，开发者仍负责创意方向。Sweeney 在同一帖子的回复中进一步解释，他看重的是缩短从产生想法到试玩测试的时间；他认为，游戏系统迟迟无法进入试玩，是近年部分大型游戏失败的原因。

无问芯穹完成 23 亿元 C 轮融资，累计融资 43 亿元

Wuwenshinqiong announced on October 12 the completion of its Series C funding round of 2.3 billion yuan, bringing its total accumulated funding since establishment to 4.3 billion yuan. The company was founded in 2023, originating from technological achievements of Tsinghua University and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with its business focusing on AI-native infrastructure. 无问芯穹 10 月 12 日宣布完成 23 亿元 C 轮融资，成立以来累计融资额达到 43 亿元。公司成立于 2023 年，由清华大学与上海交通大学的科技成果转化而来，业务聚焦 AI 原生基础设施。

The solution is planned for demonstration in the NVIDIA partner exhibition area at OCP Global Summit 2026. 纳微半导体宣布，与英伟达合作开发一款 24kW 直流供电板，可将 800V 直流电直接转换为 12V，面向英伟达 MGX 生态及 AI 数据中心。该方案计划在 OCP Global Summit 2026 的英伟达合作伙伴展区展示。

The new power board uses GaNFast gallium nitride devices, eliminating the original 50V to 12V intermediate bus conversion step through single-stage conversion, and then delivering 12V power to the voltage regulator modules that supply the processor. 新供电板采用 GaNFast 氮化镓器件，通过单级转换省去原有 50V 至 12V 的中间母线转换环节，再将 12V 电力送至为处理器供电的电压调节模块。

Navitas states a full-load target efficiency of 97% and a power density exceeding 2000W per cubic inch. The power board adopts a 400mm × 60mm form factor, allowing integration onto the computing motherboard and deployment close to the processor, reducing power path losses. This is a new version following their previous 20kW, 800V to 6V solution. 纳微给出的满载目标效率为 97%，功率密度超过每立方英寸 2000W。供电板采用 400mm × 60mm 形态，可集成在计算主板上、靠近处理器部署，减少供电路径损耗。这是双方此前 20kW、800V 至 6V 方案之后的新版本。

千问介绍 QwenGyre：让数小时的 Agent 任务参与强化学习

On October 12, the Qwen team introduced QwenGyre, an online reinforcement learning framework for ultra-long-range agent tasks, hoping to make tasks lasting several hours, such as generating code repositories, reproducing systems, and refactoring code, serve as training material for model learning. The related paper was submitted to arXiv on September 27. 千问团队 10 月 12 日介绍了面向超长程智能体任务的在线强化学习框架 QwenGyre，希望让生成代码仓库、复现系统和重构代码等持续数小时的任务，成为模型学习的训练材料。相关论文已于 9 月 27 日提交至 arXiv。

Such tasks may involve hundreds of rounds of interdependent model-environment interactions, with highly variable task completion times, leading to idle GPUs while waiting for all tasks to finish; historical compression, sub-agents, and retries generate branched trajectories, which can lead to redundant computations. 这类任务可能包含数百轮相互依赖的模型与环境交互，任务结束时间差异很大，等待全部任务完成会让 GPU 闲置；历史压缩、子智能体和重试产生的分叉轨迹，又会带来重复计算。

QwenGyre divides GPUs into switchable computing units. Without interrupting ongoing tasks and while preserving workspace and tool states, idle computing power is gradually shifted to training. Trajectory processing retains the true context when each output segment is generated, scores verifiable partial results, and removes redundant paths. QwenGyre 将 GPU 划分为可切换用途的计算单元。在不打断正在执行的任务、保留工作区与工具状态的情况下，逐步将空闲算力转入训练。轨迹处理则保留每段输出生成时的真实上下文，对可检验的部分成果评分，并去除重复路径。

The team conducted experiments on Qwen3.8-2.4T, processing approximately 700,000 tokens cumulatively per execution. After 48 training steps, the NL2RepoBench pass rate increased from 52.48% to 58.54%; under the same GPU budget, training time decreased from 134.55 hours for the Async solution to 75.42 hours, a reduction of approximately 44%. Cumulative tokens include input, output, and repeatedly read historical context from each round, not the length of a single context window. 团队在 Qwen3.8-2.4T 上进行实验，每次执行累计处理约 70 万 token。经过 48 步训练，NL2RepoBench 通过率从 52.48% 升至 58.54%；在相同 GPU 预算下，训练耗时从 Async 方案的 134.55 小时降至 75.42 小时，缩短约 44%。累计 token 包括各轮输入、输出和重复读取的历史上下文，并非单次上下文窗口长度。
