GPT-6 Sol 与 Luna 发布，API 价格降低 50%

On September 22, OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, bringing some of GPT-6 Astra's advancements in professional work, factual accuracy, programming, and computer operation to two faster, lower-priced models. The official statement claims that compared to GPT-5.6's promotional price, the API prices for both new models have been reduced by 50%. OpenAI 9 月 22 日发布 GPT-6 Sol 与 GPT-6 Luna，将 GPT-6 Astra 在专业工作、事实准确性、编程和电脑操作方面的部分进展下放到更快、价格更低的两款模型。官方称，相比 GPT-5.6 的促销价，两款新模型的 API 价格都下调 50%。

For GPT-6 Sol, the input and output prices are $2 and $10 per million tokens, respectively; for GPT-6 Luna, they are $0.1 and $0.5. OpenAI's internal tests show that GPT-6 Sol's factual errors are about half of its predecessor; in DeepSWE 1.1, Sol achieved a top score of 68.8%, and Luna achieved 66.6%. GPT-6 Sol 每 100 万 Token 输入和输出价格分别为 2 美元和 10 美元；GPT-6 Luna 分别为 0.1 美元和 0.5 美元。OpenAI 内部测试显示，GPT-6 Sol 的事实性错误约为前代的一半；在 DeepSWE 1.1 中，Sol 的最高成绩为 68.8%，Luna 为 66.6%。

GPT-6 Sol and Luna are now available to ChatGPT Work and Codex users with Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu subscriptions; Free and Go users can use Luna in the desktop application. The API model names are `gpt-6-sol` and `gpt-6-luna` respectively. GPT-6 Sol 和 Luna 已向 Plus、Pro、Business、Enterprise 和 Edu 用户的 ChatGPT Work 与 Codex 开放；Free 和 Go 用户可在桌面应用使用 Luna。API 模型名分别为 `gpt-6-sol` 和 `gpt-6-luna`。

Claude Opus 5.5 发布，典型任务运行成本降低 40%

On September 22, Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in the Claude 5.5 family. The official statement claims that the new model achieves the level of Claude Fable 5.1 in most tasks, and under normal settings, the cost of processing typical workloads is 40% lower than Opus 5, while the output speed is more than 30% faster. Anthropic 9 月 22 日发布 Claude Opus 5.5，这是 Claude 5.5 家族的首款模型。官方称，新模型在多数工作中达到 Claude Fable 5.1 的水平，常规设置下处理典型工作负载的成本比 Opus 5 低 40%，输出速度则快 30% 以上。

For Opus 5.5, the input, output, and cache read prices are $4, $20, and $0.2 per million tokens, respectively. In cases disclosed by Anthropic, early testers used it to complete 680,000 lines of code migration in less than a day; in a web page loading optimization test, the model succeeded 39 out of 40 attempts. Opus 5.5 每 100 万 Token 的输入、输出和缓存读取价格分别为 4 美元、20 美元和 0.2 美元。在 Anthropic 披露的案例中，早期测试者用它在不到一天内完成 68 万行代码迁移；在一项网页加载优化测试中，模型在 40 次尝试中成功 39 次。

This is Anthropic's first release after publicly calling for "slowing down the progress of frontier models." Before its launch, the model underwent evaluations by external organizations such as Frontier Design and METR, and adopted biosafety and cybersecurity protections similar to Claude Fable 5.1. Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 will be released in the coming weeks. 这是 Anthropic 公开呼吁「放慢前沿模型进度」后的首次发布。模型上线前接受了 Frontier Design 和 METR 等外部机构的评估，同时采用与 Claude Fable 5.1 类似的生物安全与网络安全防护。Claude Sonnet 5.5 和 Haiku 5.5 将在未来几周推出。

特努斯：Siri AI 不应代替人际关系

Apple CEO John Ternus stated in an interview with Canal+ program Clique that Siri AI "is not designed to be your friend." Facing market competition from AI companion products, he believes Apple does not want AI to replace human relationships, as human relationships are the foundation for learning, growth, and happiness. 苹果 CEO John Ternus 在接受 Canal+ 节目 Clique 采访时表示，Siri AI 「并非被设计成你的朋友」。面对 AI 伴侣产品的市场竞争，他认为苹果不希望 AI 取代人与人的关系，因为人际关系是学习、成长和获得快乐的基础。

He also discussed the development trade-offs for iPhone Duo. Apple determined the unfolding ratio based on a large-screen experience similar to the iPad, rather than using the common form of two spliced phone screens. The reliability of the hinge and screen was a major engineering challenge; for example, the team needed to simultaneously address the unfolding angle, screen continuity, and coordination with software animations. 他还谈到 iPhone Duo 的开发取舍。苹果从类似 iPad 的大屏体验出发确定展开比例，没有沿用常见的两块手机屏幕拼接形态。铰链和屏幕的可靠性是主要工程问题；例如，团队需要同时处理展开角度、屏幕连续性与软件动画的配合。

Ternus specifically demonstrated the animation that spans both screens when unfolding the phone. He views this small detail as a product goal beyond engineering: users should immediately understand how the interface is continuous when unfolding the device, while also retaining a sense of ease. Ternus 特别展示了展开手机时跨越两侧屏幕的动画。他把这个小细节视为工程之外的产品目标：用户在展开设备时能立刻理解界面如何连续，同时保留一点轻松感。

Regarding the direction after taking over as CEO, Ternus said he would not make radical changes to Apple. When Tim Cook and Apple's board asked if he was willing to take over, he described himself as "thankfully sitting down at the time." His stated operational boundary is to continue placing products, innovation, and users at the center, not to package his new term as a complete change of direction. 对于接任 CEO 后的方向，Ternus 称不会对苹果做激进改造。Tim Cook 和苹果董事会询问他是否愿意接任时，他形容自己「幸好当时是坐着的」。他给出的经营边界是继续把产品、创新和用户放在中心，不把新任期包装成路线翻转。

美国联邦航空管理局开始用 AI 调度航班

The U.S. Federal Aviation Administration has begun using an air traffic management platform called SMART, which unifies 200 data streams, including weather, routes, real-time traffic, and controller schedules, into a single interface, using AI to assist in generating capacity, congestion, and flight scheduling recommendations. 美国联邦航空管理局已开始使用名为 SMART 的空中交通管理平台，将天气、航路、实时流量和管制员排班等 200 条数据流统一到一套界面中，用 AI 辅助生成容量、拥堵与航班排程建议。

The U.S. Department of Transportation states that the system's goal is to reduce information gaps between multiple existing independent tools, enabling controllers to more quickly re-route traffic during weather or communication failures in national airspace. The platform provides recommendations, but operational decisions are still made by air traffic controllers. 美国交通部称，这套系统的目标是减少现有多个独立工具之间的信息断层，使管制人员在全国空域发生天气或通信故障时更快重新安排流量。平台提供建议，运行决策仍由空中交通管制人员作出。
