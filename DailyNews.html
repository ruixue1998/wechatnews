我们跟库克聊了聊 iPhone Duo

On October 11, Tim Cook stated during a discussion with APPSO in Guangzhou about iPhone Duo's app adaptation that developers should fully utilize the larger, more immersive screen, explore different usage methods, and consider various user holding angles. 10 月 11 日，蒂姆·库克在广州与 APPSO 交流 iPhone Duo 的应用适配时表示，开发者应充分利用更大、更具沉浸感的屏幕，探索不同使用方式，并考虑用户不同的握持角度。

APPSO observed on-site that the Guangzhou food map created by Xmind for iPhone Duo changes with the device's opening and closing form. The Xmind team introduced that they completed the adaptation in half a month and designed a "half-screen display" outline mode for the hinge, allowing mind maps and outlines to collaborate in split-screen; Cook also affirmed this adaptation. APPSO 在现场看到，Xmind 为 iPhone Duo 制作的广州美食地图会随设备开合形态变化。Xmind 团队介绍，他们用半个月完成了适配，并针对铰链设计了「半屏展示」大纲模式，让思维导图与大纲分屏协同；库克也对这一适配给予肯定。

APPSO also observed that during the use of some applications, the system adds spacing on both sides of the hinge to ensure that the interactive area avoids creases. The layout changes brought about by the folding form became the focus of this on-site discussion. APPSO 还观察到，部分应用在使用过程中，系统会在铰链两侧增加间距，确保交互区域避开折痕。折叠形态带来的布局变化，成为这次现场交流的重点。

On the same day, Guangdong Governor Meng Fanli met with Cook and his delegation in Guangzhou. 苹果 COO 探访郑州工厂，披露 iPhone Duo 屏幕与芯片制造工艺

On October 11, Apple COO Sabih Khan visited Foxconn's Zhengzhou Science Park and Yuto Tech's Xuchang factory. Media materials provided by Apple disclosed parts of the manufacturing process for iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro series, from screens and cameras to chip packaging and packaging boxes. 10 月 11 日，苹果首席运营官 Sabih Khan 参观富士康郑州科技园及裕同科技许昌工厂。苹果提供的媒体材料披露了 iPhone Duo、iPhone 18 Pro 系列从屏幕、摄像头到芯片封装、包装盒的部分制造流程。

At Foxconn, Apple and its suppliers created custom equipment for the iPhone Duo hinge area: confocal lasers scan the microscopic topography of the screen substrate layer by layer, machine learning algorithms instantly generate filling solutions, and then micro-scale 3D printing forms up to 25 layers of photosensitive polymer to build a flat support base. 在富士康，苹果与供应商为 iPhone Duo 铰链区域打造了定制设备：共聚焦激光逐层扫描屏幕基底的微观形貌，机器学习算法即时生成填充方案，再以微米级 3D 打印成型多达 25 层光敏聚合物，构建平整的支撑基底。

The iPhone 18 Pro series' mechanical variable aperture camera reads upstream optical calibration data and completes precise assembly through dual active alignment stations. The chip process directly sputters an electromagnetic shielding layer onto the surface of the SoC package, replacing traditional metal shielding covers; Apple states that this reduces module thickness by 20% and improves heat dissipation efficiency. iPhone 18 Pro 系列的机械可变光圈摄像头，通过双主动对准工站读取上游光学标定数据并完成精密装配。芯片工艺则将电磁屏蔽层直接溅镀在 SoC 封装表面，取代传统金属屏蔽罩；苹果称，这使模组厚度缩减 20%，并提升散热效能。

Yuto's Xuchang factory showcased the inline printing, automatic assembly, and quality inspection of iPhone 18 Pro series packaging boxes. Water-based coatings replace plastic film, reducing plastic by over 3,000 tons annually according to the materials released; jointly developed intelligent optical inspection equipment can capture 64 images in 1.9 seconds to inspect the appearance of the packaging boxes. 裕同许昌工厂展示了 iPhone 18 Pro 系列包装盒的联线印刷、自动组装和质检。水性涂层取代塑料覆膜，按材料公布的口径，每年减塑超过 3000 吨；联合研发的智能光学检测设备可在 1.9 秒内采集 64 张图像，检查包装盒外观。

Apple also introduced newly launched manufacturing AI application courses, which have been opened to over 30 factories in China, including online videos and offline practical training. An industrial engineer in the materials created an SOP comparison tool during the course, which can mark changes and generate summaries after uploading new and old operating procedures. 苹果还介绍了新推出的制造业 AI 应用课程，已向中国 30 多家工厂开放，包含线上视频和线下实操。材料中的一名工业工程师在课程中做出 SOP 比对工具，上传新旧两份作业程序后即可标出修改并生成总结。

中汽协倡议摒弃「重形式、轻安全」的非理性创新

On October 11, the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers issued an initiative, proposing that automobile companies should abandon irrational innovation that "emphasizes form over safety," prioritize safety assessments throughout the entire product innovation process, and improve the R&D testing and verification system. 中国汽车工业协会 10 月 11 日发布倡议，提出汽车企业应摒弃「重形式、轻安全」的非理性创新，将安全评估前置到产品创新全过程，并完善研发测试验证体系。

The CAAM pointed out that some designs that unilaterally pursue innovation gimmicks, ignore safety bottom lines, and insufficient R&D testing can bring hidden dangers to product safety and industrial development. Its initiatives include standardizing innovative designs, strengthening full-scenario verification such as comprehensive vehicle reliability and adaptability to complex environments, and ensuring product safety through sufficient testing. 中汽协指出，部分片面追求创新噱头、忽视安全底线的设计，以及研发测试不充分等问题，会给产品安全和产业发展带来隐患。其倡议包括规范创新设计、加强车辆综合可靠性和复杂环境适应性等全场景验证，以及用充分测试保障产品安全。

This initiative responds to the draft notice on the management of innovative design and R&D test verification for automotive products, jointly drafted and released by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, and the State Administration for Market Regulation on October 10. The document focuses on the management of unreasonable innovative design and test verification and is currently still in the public comment phase. 这项倡议回应了工业和信息化部会同公安部、生态环境部、市场监管总局于 10 月 10 日起草发布的汽车产品创新设计和研发测试验证管理通知征求意见稿。文件聚焦不合理创新设计与测试验证管理，目前仍处于征求意见阶段。

💡 纳德拉：给 AI 配上「紧急刹车」，企业不能外包控制责任

On October 10, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella published a lengthy article on X, calling for companies to establish an 'emergency brake' for powerful AI systems: authorized individuals should always be able to pause or shut down a model while it is performing tasks. Companies cannot solely rely on the security assurances of model providers; they must assume from the outset that models may be compromised and be prepared for isolation and control. 10 月 10 日，微软 CEO 萨提亚·纳德拉在 X 发表长文，呼吁企业为强大的 AI 系统建立「紧急刹车」：获得授权的人应始终能在模型执行任务期间暂停或关闭它。企业不能仅依赖模型提供商的安全保证，必须从一开始就假定模型可能遭到入侵，并做好隔离与控制。

Nadella pointed out that while the behavior of traditional software can be traced back to specific code paths, current model behavior is difficult to map to a particular piece of training data or a set of weight configurations. Yet, companies have allowed these systems to access sensitive data and perform critical tasks on their behalf, thus requiring a re-evaluation of trust architectures. He emphasized that treating models as internal risks does not mean deeming them malicious, but rather acknowledging that any powerful entity with access to critical systems can make mistakes or be compromised. 纳德拉指出，传统软件的行为可以追溯到具体代码路径，但当下的模型行为很难对应到某条训练数据或某组权重配置。企业却已让这些系统接触敏感数据、代为执行关键任务，因此必须重新审视信任架构。他强调，把模型当作内部风险，并非认定它们恶意，而是任何能接触重要系统的强大主体都可能犯错或被攻破。

He proposed that models, the execution frameworks that orchestrate their work, and the scope of permissible actions should be separated from each other, and permissions and protective mechanisms must be placed outside the model. Even using another model for adversarial testing might only involve one opaque model supervising another opaque model, which still cannot independently prove system reliability. 他提出，模型、编排其工作的执行框架和允许采取行动的范围应彼此分离，权限与防护机制必须置于模型之外。即便用另一个模型做对抗测试，也可能只是让一个不透明模型监督另一个不透明模型，仍不能独立证明系统可靠。

Specific measures include: 具体措施包括：

  • Do not allow a single model to be the sole dependency for important outcomes, nor let it validate its own work independently. 不让单一模型成为重要结果的唯一依赖，也不让它独自验证自己的工作。
  • Leave tamper-proof, human-readable records for every important action, and continuously test for failures, attacks, and edge cases. 为每项重要行动留下防篡改、可供人阅读的记录，并持续测试失败、攻击和边界情况。
  • Companies should independently determine access permissions and scope of action, retaining mechanisms for independent auditing, manual mid-task shutdowns, and timely disclosure of security incidents. 由企业独立决定访问权限和行动范围，保留独立审计、人工中途停机及及时披露安全事故的机制。
