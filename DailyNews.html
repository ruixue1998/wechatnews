中国空间站把铷原子云冷却到 9.7 皮开尔文

Peking University's School of Electronics announced on November 6 that the experimental team in China's Tiangong space station's Mengtian module has cooled a rubidium atomic cloud to an equivalent temperature of 9.7 picokelvins, setting a new record for artificial ultracold quantum gases. The results have been published in Science. 北京大学电子学院 11 月 6 日公布，中国空间站梦天舱的实验团队将铷原子云的等效温度降至 9.7 皮开尔文，刷新了人造低温量子气体的纪录。成果已发表于《科学》。

In a microgravity environment, the team used two sets of crossed laser beams with different focusing degrees and potential well depths to cool atoms step by step. After releasing the atomic cloud, researchers measured its equivalent temperature based on its diffusion speed and observed it for 7 seconds. Previously, the lowest equivalent temperature in similar experiments was 38 picokelvins, with a free diffusion time of 2 seconds. 团队在微重力环境下，使用两组聚焦程度和势阱深度不同的交叉激光束逐级冷却原子。释放原子云后，研究人员根据它扩散的速度测得等效温度，并观测了 7 秒。此前同类实验的最低等效温度为 38 皮开尔文，自由扩散时间为 2 秒。

博世吴永桥：汽车不该拿来订咖啡订机票

According to Yicai, Wu Yongqiao, President of Bosch Smart Driving and Control Systems China, said at a car forum in Wuzhen that he does not agree with the judgment that cars will become a 'third space'. Bosch has worked on more than 100 smart cockpit projects for global clients, some of which integrated ordering coffee, restaurants, flight tickets, and hotels into the car's infotainment system, but very few people actually used them. 据第一财经报道，博世智能驾驶与控制系统事业部中国区总裁吴永桥在乌镇的汽车夜话上说，他不认同汽车将成为「第三空间」的判断。博世为全球客户做过 100 多个智能座舱项目，其中一些把订咖啡、订餐厅、订机票和酒店搬进车机，实际使用的人很少。

Wu Yongqiao believes that even if autonomous driving develops to L3 or L4, people in the car, with free time, are more likely to use their mobile phones to book tickets and hotels because mobile phone software services and algorithms iterate faster. He suggests that car infotainment systems should focus on functions directly related to car use, such as navigation, vehicle control, and energy replenishment. 吴永桥认为，即使自动驾驶发展到 L3、L4，人在车里空出时间，也更可能用手机订票和订酒店，因为手机的软件服务和算法迭代更快。他建议车机把精力放在导航、车辆控制和补能等与用车直接相关的功能上。

💡 Airbnb CEO：AI 助手带来的订单很少，旅行规划仍需要多人参与

Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky stated on the Q3 earnings call that Meta Muse and Instinct brought very little traffic to Airbnb, even fewer actual bookings, and there was no significant growth in traffic over the past four weeks. Airbnb 首席执行官布莱恩·切斯基在第三季度财报电话会上表示，Meta Muse 和 Instinct 给 Airbnb 带来的访问量很小，实际订单更少，过去四周的流量也没有明显增长。

He views these assistants as entry points similar to search engines, offering an opportunity for more people to discover Airbnb. 他把这类助手视为类似搜索引擎的入口，有机会让更多人发现 Airbnb。

Chesky uses shopping at a pharmacy versus shopping at IKEA to distinguish between two types of needs: booking a flight ticket can be a single purchase, while planning a trip requires simultaneously deciding where to stay, what to do, and how to get there.
