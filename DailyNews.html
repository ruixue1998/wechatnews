DeepSeek 开放 App 工具接口，开发者可接入搜索、文件和语音

On November 13, DeepSeek opened a set of application APIs, integrating tools such as internet search, file input, and link reading used by its official app and web interface into existing dialogue interfaces. After developers enable the corresponding tools in the V4.1-Flash Messages, Responses, or Chat Completions interfaces, the server will process the tool calls and return the results to the dialogue context. DeepSeek 11 月 13 日开放一组应用 API，把官方 App 和网页端使用的联网搜索、文件输入、链接阅读等工具接入现有对话接口。开发者在 V4.1-Flash 的 Messages、Responses 或 Chat Completions 接口启用相应工具后，服务端会处理工具调用，并把结果放回对话上下文。

In normal mode, you can search web pages or images by question and include citations in the answer. In thinking mode, you can continue to search and open links in multi-step reasoning. When uploading PDF, Office documents, or images, the interface can extract text or parse mixed text and image content, then hand it over to the model for answering. 普通模式可按问题搜索网页或图片，并在回答中附上引用。思考模式可以在多步推理中继续搜索、打开链接。上传 PDF、Office 文档或图片时，接口可提取文本或解析图文混排内容，再交给模型回答。

Web search and scraping, image text recognition, speech recognition, and speech synthesis can also be called separately. DeepSeek also open-sourced a static chat page to demonstrate attachments, voice, and link reading, and supports importing DeepSeek historical conversations. 网页搜索和抓取、图片文字识别、语音识别及语音合成也可单独调用。DeepSeek 同时开源了一个静态聊天页面，用来演示附件、语音和链接阅读，并支持导入 DeepSeek 历史对话。

OpenAI 撤销独立机器人团队，研究人员并入通用模型团队

OpenAI confirmed to Fortune that the company has disbanded its independent robotics team. OpenAI 向《财富》证实，公司已撤销独立机器人团队。机器人研究人员将并入由首席研究官马克·陈领导的研究部门，机器人应用和硬件团队则转入总裁格雷格·布罗克曼负责的应用部门。

OpenAI 表示，研究人员年初曾计划沿着 Sora 等视频生成模型的路线推进机器人研究，随后发现 GPT-6 Astra 等通用模型可以直接用于执行部分机器人任务。公司将机器人研究并入更广泛的模型研发工作，硬件和应用继续由另一部门负责。

两部门公布新型储能方案，提出到 2030 年平均储能约 3 小时

The National Development and Reform Commission and the National Energy Administration publicly released the "15th Five-Year Plan for New Energy Storage Implementation" on November 13, proposing that by 2030, the national installed capacity of new energy storage will reach 300 million kilowatts, with an average storage duration of approximately 3 hours. Among these, the target for grid-side independent new energy storage capable of ensuring supply during peak electricity consumption is approximately 140 million kilowatts. 国家发展改革委、国家能源局 11 月 13 日公开《「十五五」新型储能实施方案》，提出到 2030 年全国新型储能装机达到 3 亿千瓦，平均储能时长约 3 小时。其中，可在用电高峰承担保供任务的电网侧独立新型储能规模目标为约 1.4 亿千瓦。

The plan proposes establishing a capacity pricing mechanism for grid-side independent new energy storage under the premise of list management. After the continuous operation of the spot electricity market, a reliable capacity compensation mechanism will be gradually established. For the demand side, industrial parks and 5G communication base stations are encouraged to configure energy storage according to actual needs. 方案提出，在实行清单管理的前提下，建立电网侧独立新型储能的容量电价机制。电力现货市场连续运行后，有序建立可靠容量补偿机制。用电侧则鼓励产业园区和 5G 通信基站等按实际需求配置储能。

💡 灵光产品经理谈个人 AI 助手：团队开始数用户愿意托管多少件事

Ant Group's AI application, Lingguang, launched its 2.0 version in late October. Lingguang product manager Siji recently told "LatePost" that the team considers "hosting volume" as the core metric for personal AI assistants, counting the number of ongoing tasks users entrust to the application. 蚂蚁集团旗下 AI 应用灵光在 10 月底上线 2.0 版。灵光产品经理寺二近日接受《晚点》采访时说，团队把「托管量」作为衡量个人 AI 助手的核心指标，统计用户交给应用持续跟进的事项。

Siji gave an example: ordering a cup of coffee is a single task, but continuously monitoring flight prices is a hosted affair. The application runs continuously in the cloud, actively reminding the user when prices change or a decision is needed. The team also observes whether users return after a reminder is sent and if they are willing to provide more information to let the assistant continue with the task. 寺二举例，点一杯咖啡是一次任务，持续盯着机票价格才是一项托管事务。应用在云端持续运行，在价格变动或需要用户作决定时主动提醒。团队也会看提醒发出后用户是否回来，以及用户是否愿意继续补充情况，让助手把事情往下办。

Siji said that current hosting is more focused on monitoring and reminding. To enable the assistant to perform actual operations further, data and interfaces across different internet platforms still need to be integrated. 寺二说，目前的托管更多集中在监测和提醒。要让助手进一步完成实际操作，还要打通不同互联网平台的数据和接口。
