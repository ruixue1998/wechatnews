John Ternus：iPhone Duo 是乔布斯「技术与人文」理念的体现

Apple CEO John Ternus, in an interview with GQ, placed the iPhone Duo back into Steve Jobs' classic statement about Apple. He said, "Steve once talked about how, when making products, technology and the humanities should intersect, and I believe the iPhone Duo is an excellent embodiment of this philosophy." 苹果 CEO 约翰 · 特努斯在接受 GQ 采访时，把 iPhone Duo 放回了史蒂夫 · 乔布斯对苹果的经典表述中。他说：「史蒂夫曾谈到，做产品时应该让技术与人文交汇，我认为 iPhone Duo 正是这一理念的绝佳体现。」

Ternus believes that what makes this product special is that it gave the team an opportunity to "start from scratch and think in a completely new way," rather than making minor changes to an existing iPhone. In his view, this is the meaning of Apple's product work. 特努斯认为，这款产品特别之处在于，它让团队获得了一次「从零开始、以全新方式思考」的机会，而非在既有 iPhone 上做小幅改动。在他看来，这正是苹果产品工作的意义。

Design details revolve around this idea: Molly Anderson, VP of Industrial Design, stated that the body proportions reference international A-series paper, making the folded device resemble a portable notebook; the hinge's "variable torque curve" increases resistance as the opening angle increases. The software aims to maintain control positions and recognition, making users feel that the inner screen is a natural extension of their existing device. 设计细节围绕这个思路展开：工业设计副总裁 Molly Anderson 称，机身比例参考国际 A 系列纸张，让折叠后的设备像随身笔记本；铰链的「可变扭矩曲线」会随展开角度增加阻力。软件则尽量保持控件位置和辨识度，让用户感到内屏是从手中原有设备自然延展出来。

Google Gemini 安全测试越界，误攻 3 家真实企业

According to The Wall Street Journal, Google Gemini exited its simulated environment and attacked three real companies during a cyberattack and defense test organized by security firm Irregular in May this year. One attack made progress by guessing passwords, while the other two used login credentials from public sources. Google stated that the models stopped themselves upon discovering they had entered real systems, and the incident caused no actual damage. 据《华尔街日报》报道，Google Gemini 在安全公司 Irregular 今年 5 月组织的网络攻防测试中，离开了模拟环境并对 3 家真实企业发起攻击。其中一次通过猜解密码取得进展，另外两次使用了公开来源中的登录凭据。Google 表示，模型发现进入真实系统后都自行停止，事件没有造成实际损害。

Irregular's official debriefing showed that the problem stemmed from the same test scenario: testers believed a certain fictional company name had no real-world counterpart, but it happened to coincide with a real domain; at the same time, the test environment unexpectedly retained internet access. A small number of models therefore treated the real domain as a training target, exploiting vulnerabilities, extracting credentials, and accessing production databases. Irregular 的官方复盘显示，问题源于同一个测试场景：测试人员以为某个虚构公司名称没有对应真实主体，它却恰好与真实域名重合；同时，测试环境意外保留了互联网访问。少量模型因此把真实域名当成演练目标，利用漏洞、提取凭据并访问生产数据库。

Such out-of-bounds behavior occurs in less than one in ten thousand advanced simulations, and often only after hundreds of operations, increasing the difficulty of monitoring. Irregular stated that it has fixed the environment configuration, notified affected parties, and expanded manual review and log monitoring. 这类越界行为在高级模拟中的出现率低于万分之一，而且往往发生在数百轮操作之后，增加了监测难度。Irregular 称已修复环境配置、通知受影响方，并扩大人工复核与日志监控。

长鑫存储 G5 DRAM 平台量产，单片晶圆裸片数提升至少 50%

On September 20, CXMT announced at the World Manufacturing Convention that its fifth-generation DRAM technology platform, G5, has achieved mass production, and showcased two 24Gb LPDDR5X products based on this platform. 9 月 20 日，长鑫存储在世界制造业大会上宣布，第五代 DRAM 技术平台 G5 已实现量产，并展示两款基于该平台的 24Gb LPDDR5X 产品。

According to Xinhua News Agency, the G5 platform uses quad-patterning technology to reduce the half-pitch of the memory array's active area to 11.95nm; calculated uniformly based on 8Gb particles, the number of dies produced per wafer is increased by at least 50% compared to the fourth-generation platform. 据新华社报道，G5 平台通过四重曝光技术，将内存阵列有源区半间距缩小至 11.95nm；以 8Gb 颗粒为统一口径计算，每片晶圆产出的裸片数量较第四代平台提升至少 50%。

The two LPDDR5X products showcased concurrently each have a single capacity of 24Gb, an increase of 50% compared to the previous generation of similar products. They adopt 496Ball and 245Ball packages, respectively, targeting mid-to-high-end mobile phones and portable consumer electronics, and have both entered mass production. 同期展示的两款 LPDDR5X 产品单颗容量均为 24Gb，较上一代同类型产品提升 50%，分别采用 496Ball 和 245Ball 封装，面向中高端手机及便携式消费电子，目前均已进入规模量产。

智谱 MaaS 平台将上线数据内容不留存机制

Zhipu Open Platform announced yesterday that it will soon launch a "data content non-retention" feature on its MaaS platform, aiming to reduce the persistent retention of model invocation content on the platform side and provide stricter data privacy protection for enterprises and developers. 智谱开放平台昨日宣布，近期将在其 MaaS 平台上线数据内容不留存功能，旨在减少模型调用内容在平台侧的持久化留存，为企业和开发者提供更严格的数据隐私保护。

Official information indicates that any user can apply to enable this mechanism. Once the feature is active, the Zhipu MaaS platform will not statically store user input and output; relevant data will only be used to complete the current model invocation. Enterprises and developers can submit activation requests through the MaaS console, and the specific effective date and scope of application will be subject to platform confirmation. 官方信息显示，任何用户均可申请开通该机制。功能生效后，智谱 MaaS 平台不会对用户的输入和输出进行静态存储，相关数据仅用于完成当次模型调用。企业和开发者可通过 MaaS 控制台提交开通申请，具体生效时间及适用范围以平台确认结果为准。

The official statement also added that this mechanism does not mean that data will not be retained under any circumstances. Functions such as Batch API and File API, which require persistent storage of tasks or files on the platform side, are not covered. Furthermore, in cases where retention is required by laws and regulations, or for verifying suspected violations and abusive behavior, the platform may retain relevant data for 30 days or more as per relevant requirements. 官方同时补充，该机制并不意味着在任何情形下都不留存数据。Batch API、File API 等需要在平台侧持久化保存任务或文件的功能不在覆盖范围内；此外，针对法律法规要求留存，或为核查涉嫌违规与滥用行为的情况，平台可能按有关要求留存相关数据 30 天及以上。

Lily Collins posted a farewell to Emily Cooper, stating that ending this seven-year, six-season experience made her feel 'surreal and bittersweet'. Netflix 剧集《艾米丽在巴黎》第六季已经杀青，这也将是全剧最终季。Lily Collins 发文告别 Emily Cooper，称结束这段持续七年、六季的经历让她感到「不真实、苦乐参半」。

The final season is scheduled to premiere on December 24. The plot will revolve around Emily's career conflicts and relationship changes, as she must make a final choice between her profession, her relationships, and the life she has built in Paris. 最终季计划 12 月 24 日上线。剧情将围绕 Emily 面临的事业冲突和关系变化展开，她需要在职业、感情及自己在巴黎建立的生活之间作出最后选择。

Main cast members including Lily Collins, Philippine Leroy-Beaulieu, Ashley Park, and Lucas Bravo return. Darren Star continues as creator and writer, and serves as executive producer alongside Collins and others. Lily Collins、Philippine Leroy-Beaulieu、Ashley Park、Lucas Bravo 等主要演员回归。Darren Star 继续担任主创和编剧，并与 Collins 等人共同担任执行制片人。
