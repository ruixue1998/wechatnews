国行 Apple 智能开启测试，Siri、写作工具与视觉智能接入千问

Early this morning, Apple pushed the first developer beta of iOS 27.3, and the "Apple Intelligence and Siri" experience began to open in mainland China. 今天凌晨，苹果推送 iOS 27.3 首个开发者测试版，国行「Apple 智能与 Siri」开始开放体验。

The first batch of features listed on Apple's Chinese official website includes writing tools, real-time translation, photo erasure, smart emoji creation, and notification summaries. 苹果中文官网列出的首批功能包括写作工具、实时翻译、照片消除、智绘表情及通知摘要。

Qianwen has been integrated into Siri, Visual Intelligence, and writing tools, allowing users to ask questions, generate text, or process images within the system. 千问被整合进 Siri、视觉智能和写作工具，用户可在系统内提问、生成文本或处理图像。

According to iFanr's actual testing, Visual Intelligence's image search also integrates Baidu search. Siri now features a halo around the screen's edge and supports double-tapping the bottom of the screen to switch to text input. 据爱范儿实测，视觉智能的以图搜物还接入了百度搜索。Siri 换上环绕屏幕边缘的光晕，并支持双击屏幕底部切换为文字输入。

In Siri, basic Q&A can be completed directly. Taking "I haven't been sleeping well lately" as an example, Siri will call on Qianwen to provide general sleep advice. 在 Siri 中，基础问答可直接完成。以「最近总是睡不好」为例，Siri 会调用千问给出通用睡眠建议。

For more personalized solutions, users can click the Qianwen Logo to jump to the Qianwen App, and conversation information will be automatically synchronized. It is understood that Qianwen can formulate improvement plans based on users' previous sleep records, exercise status, and living habits. 如需更个性化的方案，用户可点击千问 Logo 跳转至千问 App，对话信息会自动同步。据了解，千问可结合用户此前的睡眠记录、运动情况和生活习惯制定改善方案。

Real-time translation covers messages, phone calls, and FaceTime, with phone calls and FaceTime limited to one-on-one conversations. AirPods translation requires the headphones to be updated to the latest firmware and paired with an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone. 实时翻译覆盖信息、电话和 FaceTime，其中电话与 FaceTime 限一对一通话。AirPods 翻译需适配耳机更新至最新固件，并与启用 Apple 智能的 iPhone 配对。

The first batch of features is applicable to the iPhone 15 Pro series, the entire iPhone 16 series and subsequent models, as well as iPhone Air, iPhone Duo. Siri and device language must be set to Simplified Chinese or English. 首批功能适用于 iPhone 15 Pro 系列、iPhone 16 全系列及后续机型，以及 iPhone Air、iPhone Duo，Siri 和设备语言须设为简体中文或英语。

It is worth noting that this domestic update provides the first batch of Apple Intelligence features, and the new Siri AI version previewed at WWDC 2026 is not included. 值得注意的是，此次国行更新提供的是首批 Apple 智能功能，WWDC 2026 预告的新版 Siri AI 未包含在内。

苹果天气出现数据异常，多地下周五「跌至」零下 32 度

Swipe left to view more content 向左滑动查看更多内容

Early this morning, Apple's built-in Weather App showed abnormal forecast data in several Chinese cities, incorrectly displaying next Friday's (November 13) lowest temperature as around -32℃. 今天凌晨，苹果自带天气 App 在国内多个城市出现预报数据异常，将下周五（11 月 13 日）的最低气温错误显示为 -32℃ 左右。

Affected areas cover multiple cities including Beijing, Shanghai, Nanjing, Wuhan, Hefei, Fuzhou, Xi'an, and Kunming, with extreme low temperatures displayed on the page generally concentrated in the -29℃ to -33℃ range. 受影响的区域覆盖北京、上海、南京、武汉、合肥、福州、西安、昆明等多个城市，页面显示的极端低温普遍集中在 -29℃ 至 -33℃ 区间。

For example, the Kunming area page showed that the lowest temperature of the day would plummet to -32℃ between 19:00 and 20:00, while the highest daytime temperature still showed 17℃ to 24℃, with a single-day diurnal temperature range of nearly 50℃. At the same time, weather forecasts for Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Nanning, Haikou, and other areas in Guangdong, Guangxi, and Hainan appeared normal, without similar data. 例如昆明地区页面显示当日最低气温将在 19:00 至 20:00 骤降至 -32℃，而白天最高气温仍显示为 17℃ 至 24℃，单日昼夜温差接近 50℃。与此同时，广州、深圳、南宁、海口等两广及海南地区的天气预报显示正常，并未出现类似数据。

The National Meteorological Center and local meteorological departments have not issued cold wave warnings recently, nor have mainstream numerical forecast models at home and abroad predicted a drastic drop in temperature around November 13. 中央气象台及各地气象部门近期并未发布寒潮预警，国内外主流数值预报模式亦未预测 11 月 13 日前后会有剧烈降温天气。

A meteorological blogger analyzed that the abnormal display's time node coincided with the numerical model forecast node at 20:00 Beijing time (12:00 UTC), speculating that it was caused by algorithmic interpolation and amplification after a deviation occurred in the prediction data for that time. 有气象博主分析指出，异常显示的时间节点恰好处于北京时间 20:00（世界协调时 12:00）的数值模式预报节点，推测系该时次的预测数据出现偏差后经算法插值放大所致。

Apple announced that with the iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS 27.3 beta releases, a new Siri AI developer preview is available, allowing all developers to integrate app content and actions into the new Siri via App Intents. 苹果宣布，随 iOS、iPadOS、macOS 和 visionOS 27.3 测试版开放新的 Siri AI 开发者预览，所有开发者均可通过 App Intents，将应用中的内容与操作接入新版 Siri。

Once integrated, Siri AI can perform in-app actions based on personal context, screen content, and natural language requests, such as checking delivery, making reservations, or placing orders. Apple also introduced "skills," allowing developers to combine multiple actions into a complete experience and enabling Siri AI to retrieve entities from Spotlight's semantic index. 接入后，Siri AI 能结合个人上下文、屏幕内容和自然语言请求执行应用内动作，例如查配送、订位或下单。苹果还引入「技能」，允许开发者把多个操作组合为一次完整体验，并让 Siri AI 检索 Spotlight 语义索引中的实体。

15 Apple apps, including Calendar, Files, Finder, Mail, Maps, Messages, and Photos, have begun offering related capabilities, such as finding coffee shops visited last week or archiving files not opened for over a year. Apple stated that these APIs will first be available for developers to explore, with user delivery starting in a few months and App Store submissions opening next spring. 日历、文件、访达、邮件、地图、信息、照片等 15 款苹果应用已开始提供相关能力，例如寻找上周去过的咖啡店、归档超过一年未打开的文件。苹果称，这批接口将先供开发者探索，几个月后开始面向用户交付，明年春季开放 App Store 提交。

GPT 爆改 Qwen 权重，团队测试数学成绩提升约 6 倍

MIT CSAIL Ph.D. student Rikiya Takehi and collaborators unveiled a "weight space programming" experiment: allowing GPT-6 Astra to directly read and modify the weights of Qwen3.5-0.8B without using gradient descent or training loops. In the team's MATH-500 test, the modified model's average accuracy increased from 7.9% to 48.1%, approximately 6 times the original. MIT CSAIL 博士生 Rikiya Takehi 及合作者公布「权重空间编程」实验：让 GPT-6 Astra 直接读取、修改 Qwen3.5-0.8B 的权重，不使用梯度下降或训练循环。在团队的 MATH-500 测试中，修改后模型的平均准确率由 7.9% 升至 48.1%，约为原来的 6 倍。

The experiment provided Astra with a Python environment for weight manipulation and math practice materials without test set problems. The best-performing round allowed up to 50 forward calls for the model to check generated results and internal states; the final edits involved approximately 0.016% of the weights. The team stated that Astra identified and weakened directions that caused the small model to fall into repetitive reasoning, focusing on modifying parts of the MLP output columns in layers 15 and 19. 实验向 Astra 提供权重操作用的 Python 环境，以及不含测试集题目的数学练习材料。效果最好的这一轮允许最多 50 次前向调用，供模型检查生成结果和内部状态；最终编辑涉及约 0.016% 的权重。团队称，Astra 找到并削弱了使小模型陷入重复推理的方向，集中修改第 15 层和第 19 层的部分 MLP 输出列。

In the same round of testing, GSM8K accuracy increased from 13.8% to 67.1%, and IFEval strict instruction following accuracy increased from 21.0% to 62.3%. The team has publicly released experiment prompts, code, evaluations, and weight differentials; the authors later also shared the reconstructed model and an online demo. 同一轮测试中，GSM8K 准确率由 13.8% 升至 67.1%，IFEval 严格指令遵循准确率由 21.0% 升至 62.3%。团队已公开实验提示、代码、评测和权重差分；作者随后还分享了重建后的模型及在线演示。

OpenAI 用 Astra 一周设计语音 Codex 设备，并与马自达合作研究

OpenAI announced a hardware design experiment: two team members used GPT-6 Astra to complete the design of a device capable of voice interaction with Codex in less than a week of spare time, and a contract manufacturer subsequently produced a small batch of prototypes in less than a month. OpenAI 公布一项硬件设计实验：两名团队成员利用 GPT-6 Astra，在不到一周的业余项目时间内完成一台可与 Codex 语音交互的设备设计，代工商随后用不到一个月制作出小批原型。

The model participated in the design of the casing and internal structure, component selection, PCB layout and wiring, as well as interface, firmware, and Codex software integration. After entering the manufacturing phase, the team still conducted multiple rounds of review and iteration to address production limitations, tolerances, and assembly details not fully considered by the model. 模型参与了外壳和内部结构设计、元件选择、PCB 布局与连线，以及界面、固件和 Codex 软件集成。进入制造阶段后，团队仍进行了多轮审查与迭代，解决模型未充分考虑的生产限制、公差与装配细节。

The company also announced research collaboration with Mazda, allowing the model to assist aerodynamic and crash safety engineers in designing, simulating, and evaluating automotive solutions. Collaboration with Chip Ganassi Racing is exploring the conversion of 3D scans of existing parts into editable CAD models and 2D manufacturing drawings, with outputs checked by team engineers for accuracy and manufacturability. 公司同时宣布与马自达开展研究合作，让模型辅助空气动力学和碰撞安全工程师设计、模拟及评估汽车方案。与 Chip Ganassi Racing 的合作则在探索将现有零件的 3D 扫描转换为可编辑 CAD 模型和二维制造图纸，输出由车队工程师检查准确性与可制造性。

On BenchCAD 2.0, which tests 3D part reconstruction, assembly, and circuit board connection recognition, OpenAI disclosed that a newer internal model achieved an average score of 0.875, while GPT-6 Astra's highest inference tier scored 0.594. 在测试三维零件重建、装配和电路板连接识别的 BenchCAD 2.0 上，OpenAI 披露一款较新的内部模型平均得分为 0.875，GPT-6 Astra 最高推理档为 0.594。

曝 DeepSeek V5 拟用昇腾预训练

"STAR Market Daily" cited multiple industry sources stating that DeepSeek's next-generation large model V5 plans to be pre-trained based on Huawei's Ascend chips. Previously, Ascend chips primarily handled inference tasks for DeepSeek models. 《科创板日报》援引多名产业链人士消息称，DeepSeek 下一代大模型 V5 计划基于华为昇腾芯片进行预训练。此前，昇腾芯片主要承担 DeepSeek 模型的推理任务。

The report summarized that DeepSeek open-sourced infrastructure components for Ascend on September 30, including the TileLang compilation tool, computing libraries like DeepGEMM and FlashMLA, and the DeepEP distributed communication library, corresponding to its previous components for the Nvidia platform. 报道梳理，DeepSeek 于 9 月 30 日开源了面向昇腾的基础设施组件，包括 TileLang 编译工具、DeepGEMM 和 FlashMLA 等计算库，以及 DeepEP 分布式通信库，与此前面向英伟达平台的组件对应。

Huawei, in turn, provides jointly defined supernode and networking solutions, and opens up underlying programming interfaces and instruction support. 华为则提供联合定义的超节点及组网方案，并开放底层编程接口和指令支持。

On the training side, teams like Shenzhen Hetao College had previously completed full-parameter post-training of DeepSeek-V4-Pro relying on the Ascend 910C cluster; the reported plan for V5 involves foundational pre-training for the next-generation model. 在训练侧，深圳河套学院等团队此前已依托昇腾 910C 集群完成 DeepSeek-V4-Pro 的全参数后训练；V5 传出的计划则涉及下一代模型的基础预训练。

盗用 AI 平台算力，上海一男子获刑三年

Shanghai's first case involving the use of AI to forge signatures and misappropriate large model computing power was recently sentenced in the first instance. The Paper learned from the Xuhui District Procuratorate on November 2 that the defendant, Zong, was sentenced to three years in prison, suspended for three years, and fined 40,000 yuan for the crime of illegally obtaining computer information system data. 上海首例利用 AI 伪造签名、盗用大模型算力案近日一审宣判。澎湃新闻 11 月 2 日从徐汇区检察院获悉，被告人宗某某因犯非法获取计算机信息系统数据罪，被判有期徒刑三年、缓刑三年，并处罚金 4 万元。

According to the procuratorial authorities, Zong used AI to write automated registration scripts and forge electronic signatures, bypassing the platform's registration and call restrictions, and built his own relay website to sell image and video generation services at low prices. By the time of the incident, he had illegally called platform interfaces over 290,000 times, with illegal gains exceeding 34,000 yuan. 据检察机关介绍，宗某某利用 AI 编写自动化注册脚本、伪造电子签名，绕过平台的注册和调用限制，自建中转网站低价出售图片、视频生成服务。至案发，他非法调用平台接口 29 万余次，违法所得 3.4 万余元。
