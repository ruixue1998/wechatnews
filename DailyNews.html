报告：中国以外头部平台三季度新短剧，三分之二由 AI 生成

The proportion of AI-generated short dramas in new overseas platform releases continues to rise. According to Owl & Co.'s latest report obtained by The Hollywood Reporter, approximately two-thirds of the vertical short dramas released by 20 top platforms outside of China in Q3 2026 were AI-generated; this proportion was 3% in Q1 and 28% in Q2. AI 短剧在海外平台的新作中占比继续上升。据《好莱坞报道者》获得的 Owl & Co. 最新报告，2026 年第三季度，中国以外 20 家头部平台发布的竖屏短剧中，约三分之二由 AI 生成；这一比例在第一季度为 3%，第二季度为 28%。

Among paid short drama apps, ranked by viewing minutes, only one live-action work was in the top five, and two were in the top twenty. AI works listed in the report include ReelShort's "The Great and Powerful Genie," and DramaBox's "Deny Me, Dragon King" and "Married to the Hidden Dragon King." 在付费短剧应用中，按观看分钟数排名，前五名只有一部真人作品，前二十名中则有两部。报告列出的 AI 作品包括 ReelShort 的《The Great and Powerful Genie》，以及 DramaBox 的《Deny Me, Dragon King》和《Married to the Hidden Dragon King》。

The apps covered in the report collectively received approximately 63 billion views and 2 billion hours of watch time in Q3, with TikTok's free app PineDrama contributing over half of the views. Among paid apps, DramaBox accounted for 36% of views, and ReelShort for 29%. 报告覆盖的应用第三季度共获得约 630 亿次观看、20 亿小时观看时长，TikTok 的免费应用 PineDrama 贡献了超过一半的观看次数。付费应用中，DramaBox 的观看次数占比为 36%，ReelShort 为 29%。

Live-action works still have clues to their continued viewing advantage. The report found that ReelShort live-action works have a higher probability of remaining in the top 10% of viewership 30 days after launch than AI works; the number of live-action short dramas is also growing along with AI works. 真人作品仍有持续观看的优势线索。报告发现，ReelShort 真人作品在上线 30 天后继续留在观看量前 10% 的概率高于 AI 作品；真人短剧的数量也在与 AI 作品一起增长。

车灯国标征求意见：拟限 L3 及以上车辆安装 ADS 标志灯

On October 20, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology publicly solicited opinions on five mandatory national standards, including the "Installation Requirements for External Lighting and Light Signaling Devices for Motor Vehicles and Trailers," with a feedback deadline of December 19. 工信部 10 月 20 日就《汽车及挂车外部照明和光信号装置的安装要求》等五项强制性国家标准公开征求意见，反馈截止日期为 12 月 19 日。

Specifically, the ADS autonomous driving system indicator light is proposed to adopt a dedicated blue-green light color; vehicles without L3 level and above autonomous driving systems are prohibited from installing this indicator light. This distinguishes between L2 combined driving assistance and L3 conditional autonomous driving in terms of indicator light usage conditions. 具体来看，ADS 自动驾驶系统标志灯拟采用专用蓝绿色光色；不具备 L3 级及以上自动驾驶系统的车辆，禁止安装该标志灯。L2 组合驾驶辅助与 L3 有条件自动驾驶，在标志灯使用条件上由此作出区分。

The draft also proposes that, except for the illuminated logo function, all other functions of the lamps cannot display text; lamps within the specified range cannot be operated by gestures, nor can they be turned off by voice. When using voice to turn on lamps, the vehicle must indicate the position of the manual switch. 草案还拟规定，除发光徽标功能外，灯具各功能不能显示文字；不得通过手势操作规定范围内的灯具，也不得通过语音关闭这些灯具。使用语音开启灯具时，车辆须指示手动开关的位置。

This standard is currently still in the public consultation phase. The drafting instructions suggest implementation from January 1, 2028, and provide transitional arrangements for models that have already received type approval. 这份标准目前仍处于征求意见阶段。编制说明建议自 2028 年 1 月 1 日起实施，并为已获型式批准的车型设置过渡安排。

工信部：防范未经充分测试的汽车进入市场，拟强化创新设计准入要求

At a press conference held by the State Council Information Office on October 20, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology stated that some automobile companies have problems such as blindly launching new products and insufficient testing and verification. It will prevent unreasonable innovative designs and products that have not undergone sufficient R&D testing and verification from entering the market through product access and subsequent supervision. 工信部在 10 月 20 日国新办新闻发布会上表示，部分汽车企业存在跟风推出新产品、测试验证不充分等问题，将通过产品准入和后续监督，防范不合理创新设计、未经充分研发测试验证的产品进入市场。

According to CCTV News, a management notice currently soliciting opinions from four departments proposes to include innovative designs such as hidden door handles, electric suction doors, virtual controls, and environmental adaptability test parameters into access and production consistency management; products that are not declared as required or are insufficiently tested will not be granted access. 据央视新闻报道，四部门正在征求意见的管理通知，拟将隐藏式门把手、电吸门、虚拟操纵件等创新设计及环境适应性测试参数纳入准入和生产一致性管理；未按要求申报、测试不充分的产品不予准入。

Safety assessments cover more than 20 designs, including oddly shaped steering wheels and virtual controls. The draft requires electric suction doors to have an anti-pinch function, and "zero-gravity" seats, rotating seats, seats that fold into beds, and pop-up cabins cannot be used while driving. 安全评估覆盖异形方向盘、虚拟操纵件等 20 余项设计。草案要求电吸门具备防夹功能，行驶中不能使用「零重力」座椅、旋转座椅、座椅放倒成床及升顶座舱等功能。

In terms of R&D testing, it is proposed to require reliability verification of no less than 30,000 kilometers, environmental adaptability verification of no less than 1 year, and to include "two winters and one summer" tests; the cycle life and durability of power batteries must be actually tested by vehicle manufacturers and supplier reports cannot be directly relied upon. The relevant notice is still in the public consultation and drafting stage and should not be considered as all these requirements having been fully implemented. 研发测试方面，拟要求可靠性验证不少于 3 万公里，环境适应性验证不少于 1 年，并包含「两冬一夏」试验；动力电池循环寿命、耐久性等需由车企实测，不能直接采信供应商报告。相关通知仍处征求意见和编制阶段，不能视为这些要求已经全部实施。

Reuters also previously reported that Anthropic disclosed at its investor day that third-quarter revenue grew 45% quarter-on-quarter to $16.8 billion and was profitable on an adjusted basis. 路透社另据此前报道表示，Anthropic 在投资者日披露三季度营收环比增长 45%，至 168 亿美元，并在经调整口径下盈利。

两人都认为，按主观体验来理解意识，当下 AI 大概率并不具备意识。Seth 强调，智能与疼痛体验是两回事：聊天机器人可以模仿人类的表达、识别模式和进行推理，并不因此拥有承受痛苦的能力。

他们对不确定性的看法有所不同。Seth 认为现有 AI 拥有意识的可能性微乎其微；Sebo 则认为，意识理论尚无定论，应给不同主流理论一定权重，不能把可能性直接归零，但这也不等于当前 AI 很可能已经有意识。

两人仍主张友善交互。Seth 的理由是，残酷对待大模型或无生命玩偶，都可能钝化人的道德感，而不能据此反推 AI 有情感。Sebo 将其视为一种成本较低的预防策略，并认为尊重式对话有时也能改善模型输出。

When memory is enabled, new messages after locking can be used for the memory of other locked conversations but will not update regular memory; information already remembered before locking will not be removed. OpenAI 强调，对话锁不会创建端到端加密或独立加密保险库，也不改变保留期限及「为所有人改进模型」设置。启用记忆时，上锁后的新消息可用于其他锁定对话的记忆，但不会更新常规记忆；上锁前已经记住的信息不会因此移除。

If you choose to reset after forgetting your PIN, locked conversations, related memories, and files protected by the old PIN will be permanently deleted. This feature covers Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu versions; the medical version is not yet supported. 忘记 PIN 后若选择重置，会永久删除受旧 PIN 保护的锁定对话、相关记忆与文件。该功能覆盖 Free、Go、Plus、Pro、Business、Enterprise 和 Edu，医疗版暂不支持。

Memory Tensor has released and open-sourced the MemT 1.0 series of memory-native foundation models, including Flash and Nano, with total parameters of 288B and 36B respectively, and active parameters of 17B and 3B respectively. Model weights and inference code are available via Hugging Face and GitHub. 记忆张量发布并开源记忆原生基础模型 MemT 1.0 系列，包含 Flash 和 Nano 两款模型，总参数分别为 288B、36B，激活参数分别为 17B、3B。模型权重与推理代码已通过 Hugging Face、GitHub 提供。

MemT 1.0 incorporates a native memory module into the Transformer, allowing memory attention to run in parallel with original attention. When generating a response, the model reads existing memories; after an interaction round, it writes the input and response into an independent dynamic memory state for subsequent tasks. During online interaction, the model backbone and hyper-memory weights remain fixed, while the memory state of each interaction trajectory changes. MemT 1.0 在 Transformer 中加入原生记忆模块，让记忆注意力与原有注意力并行运行。生成回答时，模型读取已有记忆；一轮交互结束后，再把输入与回答写入独立的动态记忆状态，供后续任务使用。在线交互期间，模型主干及超记忆权重保持固定，变化的是每条交互轨迹的记忆状态。

The team also pointed out that fixed-capacity memory requires a trade-off between retaining early details and accepting new information.
