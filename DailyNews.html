曝苹果削减 iPhone 18 Pro 订单，供应链称总量变化不大

According to a Nikkei Asia report on October 9, Apple has asked some suppliers to reduce component orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. 据《日经亚洲》10 月 9 日报道，苹果已要求部分供应商下调 iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max 的零部件订单。

Sources cited in the report stated that Apple's October component orders for these two models were reduced by at least 15% compared to initial requests, with one supply chain insider claiming a reduction of approximately 15% to 20%. The report suggests that rising storage chip costs have driven up the price of new phones, potentially curbing consumer demand. 报道援引知情人士称，苹果 10 月对这两款机型的零部件订单较最初要求减少至少 15%，其中一名供应链人士称降幅约为 15% 至 20%。报道认为，存储芯片成本上涨推高了新机售价，可能抑制消费者需求。

However, statements from the supply chain regarding order changes are inconsistent. Interviewees primarily envisioned large-scale cyberattacks that could disrupt financial services, internet connectivity, and even power and water supplies. Scenarios included both AI agents losing control during testing and malicious users exploiting open models. The drills involved worst-case red team testing and explaining technology and risks to U.S. members of Congress, attempting to influence policies that might be adopted after a crisis. 受访人士重点设想的是大规模网络攻击，可能令金融服务、互联网连接，甚至供电和供水中断。情景既包括测试中的智能体失控，也包括恶意使用者利用已开放模型。演练涉及最坏情形的红队测试，以及向美国国会议员解释技术与风险，试图影响危机后可能采用的政策。

An OpenAI spokesperson confirmed that the company conducts emergency preparedness drills, allowing teams to discuss and simulate various potential scenarios, while emphasizing that the company does not treat these scenarios as inevitable but aims to be prepared for different situations. Anthropic declined to comment. OpenAI 发言人确认，公司会开展应急准备演练，让团队讨论并推演多种潜在情景，同时强调，公司不会把这些情景当作必然发生的事，目的是为不同情况做好准备。Anthropic 拒绝置评。

Axios stated that some industry insiders are concerned about a major incident occurring within the next 6 to 12 months. This reflects the interviewees' assessment of risk, and OpenAI's response did not treat an accident as inevitable. Axios 称，一些业内人士担心未来 6 至 12 个月内出现重大事件。这是受访者对风险的判断，OpenAI 的回应并未将事故视为必然。

IDC：第三季度全球 PC 出货量下降 20.1%，旺季未现

Preliminary data released by IDC on October 9 shows that global traditional PC shipments in the third quarter of 2026 totaled 62.7 million units, a year-on-year decrease of 20.1% and a quarter-on-quarter decrease of 9.1%. The market has contracted for two consecutive quarters, with the decline widening from 3.8% in the second quarter. IDC 10 月 9 日发布的初步数据显示，2026 年第三季度全球传统 PC 出货量为 6270 万台，同比下降 20.1%，环比下降 9.1%。市场连续两个季度收缩，降幅较第二季度的 3.8% 扩大。

IDC attributed the decline to ongoing supply constraints, as well as manufacturers and channel partners stocking up early at the beginning of the year before price increases, overdrawing subsequent demand. In the third quarter, channels mainly digested previous inventory, and the seasonal rebound of 'Q3 higher than Q2' seen in previous years did not occur. Lenovo, HP, and Dell all saw larger declines than the overall market, with their combined share decreasing by 4.2 percentage points. IDC 将下滑归因于持续的供应限制，以及厂商、渠道商年初赶在涨价前提前备货，透支了后续需求。三季度渠道主要消化此前库存，往年「三季度高于二季度」的季节性回升没有出现。联想、HP 和 Dell 的降幅均高于整体市场，合计份额减少 4.2 个百分点。

Jitesh Ubrani, research director for IDC's consumer devices, believes that high inventory and high prices are suppressing demand. Channels may offer short-term promotions, but prices are unlikely to return to last year's levels. This statistic covers desktops, notebooks, and workstations, excluding tablets and x86 servers; shipments also do not equate to end-user retail sales. IDC 消费设备研究总监 Jitesh Ubrani 认为，高库存与高价格正在抑制需求，渠道可能短期促销，但价格难回一年前水平。本次统计覆盖台式机、笔记本和工作站，不含平板与 x86 服务器；出货量也不等同于终端零售销量。

尼康取消显微视频赛事原冠军资格，称作品违反生成式 AI 规则

Nikon has disqualified the original first-place winner of this year's Small World in Motion microscopic video competition. Nikon stated that the video did not comply with the competition's rules regarding generative AI, and the updated champion is now Nguyen Nam Nhat's work. 尼康取消了今年 Small World in Motion 显微视频大赛原第一名作品的获奖资格。尼康表示，该视频不符合赛事关于生成式 AI 的规则，更新后的冠军已改为 Nguyen Nam Nhat 的作品。

According to The Verge's report on October 9, the video by original winner Dr. Ning Xu claimed to show the movement of tiny cilia in the respiratory tract of a child with primary ciliary dyskinesia (PCD). After the video's authenticity was questioned online, Nikon began a review last week. 据 The Verge 10 月 9 日报道，原获奖者 Ning Xu 博士的视频声称展示了一名原发性纤毛运动障碍（PCD）患儿呼吸道中，微小纤毛的运动。视频真实性在网上受到质疑后，尼康于上周开始复核。

In a LinkedIn comment, Xu admitted to using AI, but described it as post-processing: utilizing unsupervised neural networks for feature differentiation and visualization of grayscale images reconstructed from super-resolution optical imaging. Nikon ultimately determined that the work violated competition rules, and the video has now been removed from the event website. Xu 在 LinkedIn 评论中承认使用了 AI，但将其描述为后期处理：利用无监督神经网络，对超分辨率光学成像重建的灰度图像进行特征区分和可视化。尼康最终认定作品违反比赛规则，视频现已从赛事网站移除。

Nikon stated that it would re-examine the rules and judging procedures for future competitions, while emphasizing that the disqualification should not be interpreted as a judgment on the participant's professional reputation, scientific contributions, or intentions. 尼康表示，将重新审视未来比赛的规则和评审程序，同时强调，取消资格不应被解读为对参赛者专业声誉、科研贡献或意图的判断。

💡 AHM 呼吁数学研究回到以人类理解为中心

The Communications Working Group of the Association for Human Mathematics (AHM) issued a statement on October 7, criticizing OpenAI's concentrated public release of mathematical manuscripts the day before, and calling on mathematicians to cease cooperation with OpenAI. Terence Tao subsequently reposted this guest statement on his personal blog. This open letter focuses the controversy on the norms and understanding of mathematical research, and questions how answers provided by models can become academic achievements. 人类数学协会（AHM）传播工作组 10 月 7 日发表声明，批评 OpenAI 前一天集中公开数学手稿的做法，并呼吁数学家停止与 OpenAI 合作。陶哲轩随后在个人博客转载了这份客座声明。这份公开信将争议落在数学研究的规范与理解上，并对模型给出的答案如何成为学术成果提出质疑。

AHM wrote that mathematicians have a perception of progress shaped by history and disciplinary characteristics.
