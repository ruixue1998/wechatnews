Key business and cash flow performance are as follows: 主要业务和现金流表现如下：

  • Automotive business revenue was $20.56 billion, a year-on-year decrease of 3%; deliveries were 486,532 vehicles, a year-on-year decrease of 2%. 汽车业务营收 205.6 亿美元，同比下降 3%；交付 486532 辆，同比下降 2%。
  • Energy business revenue was $2.881 billion, a year-on-year decrease of 16%; service and other business revenue was $4.605 billion, a year-on-year increase of 33%. 能源业务营收 28.81 亿美元，同比下降 16%；服务及其他业务营收 46.05 亿美元，同比增长 33%。

王兴兴：物理 AI 已有突破苗头，难点在模型与机器人对齐

At the 2026 World Physical AI Conference and Unitree Partner Ecosystem Conference held on October 22, Wang Xingxing, founder of Unitree Robotics, stated that physical AI is showing signs of being 'broken through,' and related breakthroughs may appear in the next 1-2 years or 2-3 years. 在 10 月 22 日举行的 2026 世界物理 AI 大会暨宇树合作伙伴生态大会上，宇树科技创始人王兴兴表示，物理 AI 已经看到被「击穿」的苗头，相关突破可能在未来 1—2 年或 2—3 年出现。

According to a report by *Science and Technology Innovation Board Daily*, he believes that almost everyone's attention to and belief in AI are still insufficient, including himself. 据《科创板日报》报道，他认为，几乎所有人对 AI 的重视程度和信仰都还不够，其中也包括他自己。

Regarding the basis for progress, Wang Xingxing stated in his keynote speech that for top large model companies, in a sense, internal self-evolution of models has largely been achieved: models write programs and collect data themselves, with humans only needing to provide appropriate guidance and participation. 谈及进展的依据，王兴兴在主旨演讲中称，对于顶尖大模型公司，某种意义上，内部已经基本实现模型的自进化：模型自己写程序、采集数据，人只需要适当引导和参与。

He also distinguished between the time for technological breakthroughs and widespread application. Wang Xingxing stated that it takes 5-10 years for a technological breakthrough to spread to everyone's use, so people's perception of these advancements is not yet profound. 他同时区分了技术突破与普遍应用的时间。王兴兴表示，从技术突破扩散到每个人都能使用，需要 5—10 年，因此人们对这些进展的感受还不那么深刻。

In his view, the most critical challenge for embodied AI to cross the 'ChatGPT moment' is the alignment of AI model input/output with real robots. As for the specific deployment order, he judges that commercial service scenarios will be faster than home and industrial sectors. 在他看来，具身智能迈过「ChatGPT 时刻」的最核心挑战，是 AI 模型的输入输出与真实机器人的对齐。至于具体落地顺序，他判断商业服务场景会比家庭、工业领域更快。

A live report from Yicai also documented an internal test: Wang Xingxing stated that by deploying GPT-6 onto a robot, without specifically programming it for brush washing or color changing, the robot could observe the adjacent paint palette, clean its brush independently, and continue painting. He believes that large models can already solve some planning problems, but fine assembly and complex object interaction still present numerous engineering challenges. 第一财经的现场报道还记录了一项内部测试：王兴兴称，把 GPT-6 部署到机器人上，在没有为洗笔、换色专门编写程序的情况下，机器人能观察旁边的颜料盘，自行清洗画笔并继续绘画。他认为，大模型已能解决部分规划问题，但精细装配和复杂物体交互仍有大量工程难点。

Unitree is also internally researching a robot self-evolution process: allowing the model to collect papers, write code, train first in simulation, then test in a real environment, and provide feedback to the model after scoring by AI or humans, continuing to rewrite code and solutions. At the end of his speech, he said he was 'really afraid of wasting every day,' as industry opportunities are abundant and competition is fierce. 宇树也在内部预研机器人自进化流程：让模型搜集论文、写代码，先在仿真中训练、再到真实环境测试，由 AI 或人打分后反馈给模型，继续改写代码与方案。他在演讲结尾说，自己「真的很害怕浪费每一天」，行业机会很多，竞争也很激烈。
