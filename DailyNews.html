四部门拟禁全隐藏式车门把手，要求车企如实宣传使用边界

On October 10, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and three other departments released a draft notice for public comment on the management of innovative design and R&D testing and verification for automotive products. It proposes to prohibit fully concealed door handles and requires car manufacturers to conduct marketing promotions truthfully and objectively, accurately explaining the usage boundaries, restrictions, and test verification status of innovative designs. 10 月 10 日，工业和信息化部等四部门发布汽车产品创新设计和研发测试验证管理通知征求意见稿，拟禁止配备全隐藏式门把手，并要求车企真实、客观开展营销宣传，如实说明创新设计的使用边界、限制条件和测试验证情况。

The draft notice addresses issues such as blindly launching new products and insufficient testing and verification, putting forward requirements: emergency controls involving safety should retain physical controls and prominent markings; door handles must have mechanical redundancy design and hand operating space, and their operability in extreme environments and emergencies must be verified. Functions such as "zero gravity" seats, swivel seats, or seats folding into beds are not allowed while driving. 征求意见稿针对跟风推出新产品、测试验证不充分等问题提出要求：涉及安全的应急操纵件应保留实体操纵件和醒目标识；门把手须具备机械冗余设计和手部操作空间，并验证极端环境及紧急情况下的可操作性。行车时不可使用「零重力」座椅、旋转座椅或座椅放倒成床等功能。

According to the proposed arrangement, starting from January 1, 2027, newly declared products involving innovative designs must supplement technical parameters and complete corresponding tests; products that have already received approval should submit supplementary parameters and verification materials before July 1, 2027. The environmental adaptability verification period shall not be less than 1 year, and reliability verification tests shall not be less than 30,000 kilometers. 按拟议安排，2027 年 1 月 1 日起，涉及创新设计的新申报产品需补充技术参数并完成相应测试；已取得公告的产品应在 2027 年 7 月 1 日前补报参数及验证材料。环境适应性验证周期不少于 1 年，可靠性验证试验不低于 3 万公里。

Regarding promotion, car manufacturers must not engage in false, exaggerated, or misleading advertising, must not use scenarios that violate road traffic regulations to demonstrate product functions, nor must they induce drivers to neglect their safe driving responsibilities. The above requirements are currently in the public consultation phase. 宣传方面，车企不得作虚假、夸大或引人误解的宣传，不得使用违反道路交通法规的场景展示产品功能，也不得诱导驾驶人忽视安全驾驶责任。上述要求目前处于公开征求意见阶段。

长鑫披露 4F² 内存架构进展，首款服务器产品预计年底推出

Cao Kanyu, President of CXMT, disclosed at the 4th Integrated Chip and Chiplet Conference on October 10 that CXMT expects to launch a new generation of DDR5 RDIMM server memory products using a 4F² architecture by the end of the year. According to Yicai and 21st Century Business Herald, this technology involves adjustments in both storage cell and manufacturing processes. 长鑫存储总裁曹堪宇在 10 月 10 日的第四届集成芯片和芯粒大会上披露，长鑫预计年底推出采用 4F² 架构的新一代 DDR5 RDIMM 服务器内存产品。据第一财经、21 世纪经济报道，这项技术在存储单元和制造工艺两方面作出调整。

Currently, mainstream DRAM storage cells use a 6F² architecture, where F represents the minimum feature size. 4F² reduces the cell area to four times the square of the minimum feature size, increasing storage density per unit area. CXMT uses self-developed VSG (Vertical Surrounding Gate) transistors, arranging them vertically to enhance gate control, improve leakage, and mitigate short-channel effects. 目前 DRAM 主流存储单元采用 6F² 架构，F 代表最小特征尺寸。4F² 将单元面积缩小至四倍最小特征尺寸的平方，提高单位面积的存储密度。长鑫采用自研 VSG 垂直包围沟道晶体管，让晶体管竖立布置，以增强栅极控制能力，改善漏电与短沟道效应。

Another adjustment is to separate the manufacturing processes of the DRAM storage array and the CMOS peripheral logic circuits, and then interconnect them through wafer-level hybrid bonding, allowing for separate optimization of logic performance and storage density. Cao Kanyu stated that CXMT has over 300 related public patent families. 另一项调整是将 DRAM 存储阵列与 CMOS 外围逻辑电路的制造工艺分开，再通过晶圆级混合键合实现互联，使逻辑性能和存储密度可以分别优化。曹堪宇称，长鑫相关公开专利族已超过 300 件。

The first-generation product chosen is DDR5 RDIMM for servers, which requires meeting continuous operation, stability, and compatibility requirements. This disclosure covers research and development progress and product plans for the end of the year. 首代产品选择服务器用 DDR5 RDIMM，这类内存需要满足持续运行、稳定性和兼容性要求。此次披露的是研发进展与年底产品计划。

💡 东芝人力负责人：AI 改变初级岗位，面试更看重解决问题的过程

How do junior recruiters gain experience after AI starts screening resumes? Jason Richard Desentz, Chief Human Resources Officer for Toshiba Americas, stated in an article published in Business Insider that junior positions will remain, but job content and recruitment standards are changing.
