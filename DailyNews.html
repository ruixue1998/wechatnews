苹果发布 M6 MacBook Pro 和 iMac

Apple has released MacBook Pro and iMac models equipped with the M6 chip, with starting prices in mainland China of 15,999 yuan and 12,499 yuan, respectively. 苹果发布搭载 M6 芯片的 MacBook Pro 和 iMac，国行起售价 分别为 15999 元、12499 元。

The M6 features a 12-core CPU and a 12-core GPU, an increase of 2 cores each compared to the M5. Each GPU core includes a neural accelerator and is equipped with a dual 16-core neural engine. Apple states that compared to the M5, graphics performance is boosted by up to 30%, and on-device AI workload performance is improved by up to 25%. M6 配备 12 核 CPU 和 12 核 GPU，相比 M5 各增加 2 核，GPU 每个核心均带有神经网络加速器，并配备双 16 核神经网络引擎。苹果称，相比 M5，图形性能最高提升 30%，端侧 AI 工作负载性能最高提升 25%。

The MacBook Pro comes standard with 16GB of unified memory, with an option for up to 32GB, and a memory bandwidth of 170GB/s. MacBook Pro 标配 16GB 统一内存，最高可选 32GB，内存带宽为 170GB/s。

The iMac's design continues the 24-inch all-in-one form factor, offering seven color options: blue, green, pink, yellow, purple, orange, and silver. It features a 4.5K Retina display; it can be equipped with up to 4 Thunderbolt 4 ports and supports up to 2 external 6K displays. iMac 外观则延续 24 英寸一体机设计，提供蓝、绿、粉、黄、紫、橙、银七种配色，配备 4.5K 视网膜显示屏；最高配备 4 个雷雳 4 端口，并支持最多 2 台 6K 外接显示器。

iPhone 18 Pro 上市三周在华销量增 29%，美国与日本下滑

Counterpoint Research's weekly sales tracker, released on October 27, shows that global sales of the iPhone 18 Pro series in its first three weeks on the market increased by 3.1% compared to the same period for the previous generation. The main growth came from the Chinese market, where sales increased by 29% year-on-year, while the US and Japanese markets both saw declines. Counterpoint Research 10 月 27 日公布的周度销量追踪显示，iPhone 18 Pro 系列上市前三周的全球销量较上一代同期增长 3.1%，主要增量来自中国市场：在华销量同比增长 29%，美国、日本市场则均出现下滑。

The agency believes that the Chinese market benefited from improved supply in the initial launch period and price increases for competing Android flagship models. The iPhone 18 Pro Max accounted for 56% of this generation's Pro series sales, slightly lower than the previous generation's 57%. 有线电视与 IPTV 新规：不得播放开机广告，首页须设免费专区

The National Radio and Television Administration announced on October 27 the "Regulations on the Administration of Cable Radio and Television Operation Services" and the "Interim Regulations on the Administration of Interactive Network Television Operation Services". Both regulations will come into effect on November 27. 国家广播电视总局 10 月 27 日公布《有线广播电视运营服务管理规定》和《交互式网络电视运营服务管理暂行规定》，两份规章均将于 11 月 27 日施行。

The new regulations require relevant operation service providers to prioritize live broadcasting, enable full-screen live broadcasting upon startup, set up channel review entry points, and prohibit playing startup advertisements. Users should be able to conveniently choose to enter live broadcasting or the TV interactive homepage upon startup; smart TVs must also support prioritizing live channels. 新规要求相关运营服务提供者坚持直播优先，实现开机看全屏直播、设置频道回看入口，不得播放开机广告。用户应能通过便捷入口，自行选择开机进入直播或电视交互主页；智能电视机也须支持优先呈现直播频道。

In terms of fees and operation, the homepages of cable TV and IPTV must set up a free zone, primarily recommending high-quality free content, streamlining paid columns, and clearly indicating pricing information; the remote control should be able to switch live sources with one key, and terminals must continuously shorten startup times. 在收费和操作方面，有线电视与 IPTV 首页须设立免费专区，重点推荐优质免费内容，精简收费栏目并明示收费信息；遥控器应能一键切换直播信源，终端还须持续缩短开机时长。

IDC：中国手机 Q3 出货下滑 15.2%，折叠屏连续四季度下降

IDC's quarterly tracking report released on October 27 shows that China's smartphone market shipments in the third quarter of this year were approximately 58.06 million units, a year-on-year decrease of 15.2%, further expanding from the approximately 4% decline in the first half of the year. IDC 10 月 27 日发布的季度跟踪报告显示，今年第三季度中国智能手机市场出货量约 5806 万台，同比下降 15.2%，降幅较上半年约 4% 进一步扩大。

IDC summarized the market as "declining volume, rising prices": the rising costs of storage and other components are passed on to terminals, manufacturers adjust configurations and raise prices, while consumers extend their replacement cycles. Huawei's market share rose to 21.1%, with shipments maintaining double-digit year-on-year growth; Honor is the only leading mainstream manufacturer to achieve positive month-on-month shipment growth. IDC 将市场概括为「量跌价升」：存储及其他元器件成本上涨传导至终端，厂商调整配置、提高定价，消费者则延长换机周期。华为市场份额升至 21.1%，出货量保持同比两位数增长；荣耀是头部主流厂商中唯一实现出货量环比正增长的品牌。

The adjustment for foldable phones is more pronounced, with approximately 1.77 million units shipped in the Chinese market in the third quarter, a year-on-year decrease of 32.7%, marking four consecutive quarters of decline. Huawei's share in this market exceeds 70%, followed by Samsung and Xiaomi. IDC expects that with Apple's entry and more brands launching new wide-foldable products, the domestic foldable phone market will rebound starting next quarter. 折叠屏的调整更明显，第三季度中国市场出货约 177 万台，同比下降 32.7%，已连续四个季度下滑。华为在该市场的份额超过 70%，三星与小米分列其后。IDC 预计，随着苹果入局、更多品牌推出阔折叠新品，国内折叠屏市场将从下一季度起回暖。

邓煜：AI 公司应公开证明思路，让用户决定数据是否用于训练

Mathematician Deng Yu spoke at a forum jointly organized by "The Intellectual" and "Science & Culture" on October 25, stating that AI has greatly promoted mathematical research, but companies providing models also need to address issues of academic norms, sources of ideas, and user rights. 数学家邓煜在 10 月 25 日由《知识分子》《赛先生》联合举办的论坛演讲中谈到，AI 对数学研究有很大促进作用，但提供模型的公司同样需要面对学术规范、思想来源和用户权益问题。

He believes that if AI is treated as a researcher publishing research results, it should explain how major proofs were obtained and clarify whose ideas were adopted. Mathematicians need to answer these questions when reporting results, and AI companies should also disclose corresponding thought chains, not just refuse on the grounds of preventing distillation. 他认为，如果把 AI 当作发表研究成果的研究者，就应解释重大证明是如何得到的，说明其中沿用了哪些人的思路。数学家报告成果时需要回答这些问题，AI 公司也应公开相应思维链，不能仅以防止蒸馏为由拒绝。

From a commercial perspective, Deng Yu questioned: if mathematicians pay to use tools and input research ideas, and then the model provider uses these ideas to train products and publish results, how should the user's contribution be acknowledged? He advocates that users should have the choice of whether to allow input data to participate in training. 从商业关系看，邓煜质疑：数学家付费使用工具、输入研究思路后，模型提供方再利用这些思路训练产品并发布成果，用户的贡献应如何得到承认？他主张，是否允许输入数据参与训练，应由用户自行选择。

He also emphasized that mathematicians should not refuse to use AI, but rather use it as a tool to advance research, with humans posing questions and guiding direction. His proposed solutions include establishing a traceability mechanism for ideas and contributions, allowing results to indicate who initially proposed a certain idea. 他同时强调，数学家不应拒绝使用 AI，而应把它作为推进研究的工具，由人提出问题、把握方向。他提出的方案包括建立思路和贡献的追溯机制，让成果能够说明某个想法最初由谁提出。

研究：豆包引用抖音的权重升至 31.8%

NewRank Service's AI source research shows that among its monitored samples, Doubao's citation weight for Douyin content increased from 10.79% in May this year to 31.8% in August, nearly one-third. 新榜服务发布的 AI 信源研究显示，在其监测样本中，豆包对抖音内容的引用权重由今年 5 月的 10.79% 升至 8 月的 31.8%，接近三分之一。

These results are derived from NewRank Zhihui's monitoring and statistics, reflecting the citation distribution in the covered questions and content samples. 新榜进一步分析视频标题与引用次数的关系，发现标题与问题的语义相关性、是否包含品牌和专业术语，比单纯增加标题长度或堆砌规格数字更有解释力。这些结果来自新榜智汇的监测与统计，反映所覆盖问题和内容样本中的引用分布。
