腾讯 WorkBuddy 上线微信小程序生成和发布能力

On September 24, Tencent launched the WeChat mini-program publishing capability for WorkBuddy. After users describe their requirements in "code development" mode, they can generate mini-programs with capabilities such as cloud database, file storage, and WeChat authorized login, and preview, test, and initiate publishing on the same workbench. Tencent stated that it can be used by upgrading to version 5.6.1 or higher. 腾讯 9 月 24 日为 WorkBuddy 上线微信小程序发布能力。用户在「代码开发」模式中描述需求后，可以生成带云数据库、文件存储及微信授权登录等能力的小程序，并在同一工作台预览、测试和发起发布。腾讯表示，升级至 5.6.1 或更高版本即可使用。

When publishing, WorkBuddy accesses the user-authorized mini-program account as a WeChat third-party service provider, assisting in uploading code, generating a trial version, submitting for review, and publishing the official version. Users with existing mini-program accounts can scan a QR code to bind and test the trial version on a real device first; those without an account can create a 14-day valid trial version, but the trial version cannot be directly converted to an official version. 发布时，WorkBuddy 以微信第三方服务商身份接入用户授权的小程序账号，协助上传代码、生成体验版、提交审核和发布正式版。已有小程序账号的用户可以扫码绑定，先在真机测试体验版；没有账号可创建有效期 14 天的试用版，但试用版无法直接转为正式版。

If the application needs to save data, upload files, or user login, WorkBuddy will prompt to enable cloud services and integrate corresponding capabilities after user confirmation. The official version still needs to pass WeChat's official review. This update extends the previously supported web application generation to the construction and launch process of WeChat mini-programs. 若应用需要保存数据、上传文件或用户登录，WorkBuddy 会提示开通云服务，并在用户确认后接入相应能力。正式版仍需经过微信官方审核。此次更新把此前已支持的网页应用生成，延伸到了微信小程序的构建与上线流程。

Google 下周将把 TPU 送入轨道，测试太空运行条件

Yesterday, Google announced the first orbital test plan for Project Suncatcher: collaborating with satellite company Planet to send a prototype satellite equipped with TPUs on SpaceX's Transporter-18 rideshare mission. Google stated that the launch is scheduled for next week, and the mission's focus is to collect operational data after the chips experience launch vibrations, space radiation, and temperature changes. 昨天，Google 公布 Project Suncatcher 的首次轨道测试计划：与卫星公司 Planet 合作，把搭载 TPU 的原型卫星送上 SpaceX 的 Transporter-18 拼车任务。谷歌称发射安排在下周，任务重点是收集芯片经历发射振动、太空辐射与温度变化后的运行数据。

Google has already conducted vibration, proton beam radiation, and thermal vacuum tests on the ground. Its preliminary results show that the Trillium TPU withstood a total ionizing radiation dose exceeding the level required to simulate a five-year space mission; true heat dissipation and long-term stability still await orbital data validation. Computing devices on satellites cannot dissipate heat through air circulation, so the team is testing a combination of heat pipes and radiators. 谷歌已在地面做过振动、质子束辐射和热真空测试。其公布的初步结果显示，Trillium TPU 承受的总电离辐射剂量超过模拟五年太空任务所需的水平；真正的散热与长期稳定性仍待轨道数据验证。卫星上的计算设备无法靠空气流动散热，团队正在测试热管和辐射器组合。

Project Suncatcher was announced last year, aiming to study the feasibility of solar-powered satellite constellations carrying AI computations. Google plans to test high-speed laser connections between two more satellites in 2027. Project Suncatcher 于去年公布，目标是研究太阳能供电的卫星群承载 AI 计算的可行性。谷歌计划在 2027 年再测试两颗卫星之间的激光高速连接。

💡 Jeffrey Ding：AI 领先要看普及应用，而非模型首发

The New Yorker published an article by Zhang Che on September 24, interviewing George Washington University political scholar Jeffrey Ding, discussing the concept of "first-mover advantage" in US-China AI competition. Ding pointed out that when the United States became a leading economy in the four industries of steel, electricity, chemicals, and automobiles, less than one-third of the key innovations were pioneered by its companies; the extent to which a technology integrates into businesses, public institutions, and daily processes determines economic returns more than the time of its initial release. 《纽约客》9 月 24 日刊发张澈的文章，采访乔治华盛顿大学政治学者 Jeffrey Ding，讨论中美 AI 竞争中的「先发」观念。Ding 指出，美国在钢铁、电力、化工和汽车四个行业成为领先经济体时，其企业首创的关键创新不到三分之一；一项技术进入企业、公共机构和日常流程的程度，比首发时间更能决定经济收益。

He cited the development of electricity as an example: British researchers were the first to make important inventions, and the United States subsequently built power generation and supply networks more quickly. Ding said in the interview that if one only compares who has the most advanced models, it would overlook the productivity changes generated after AI diffuses throughout the entire economy. 他以电力发展为例：英国研究者率先取得重要发明，美国随后更快建设发电和供电网络。Ding 在访谈中说，若只比较谁拥有最先进模型，会忽略 AI 扩散到整个经济体之后产生的生产率变化。

Regarding AI safety, Ding drew an analogy with the slowdown in nuclear power plant construction after the 1979 Three Mile Island nuclear accident in the United States. He believes that technology needs to win public trust to become widespread, and prioritizing the reliability of AI systems does not conflict with expanding adoption. 对 AI 安全，Ding 以 1979 年美国三里岛核事故之后核电建设放缓作类比。他认为技术要赢得公众信任才能普及，优先提高 AI 系统的可靠性，与扩大采用并不冲突。

He advocates for the United States to invest more resources in community colleges, vocational schools, and state universities to cultivate talent who can apply AI in local banks and municipal departments. Ding believes that China's "AI+" policy emphasizes factories, hospitals, and administrative services; the United States still has an advantage in cloud computing investment and adoption. He also suggested that American companies should acknowledge the reality that China's open-weight models have entered the toolchains of local developers and enterprises. 他主张美国把更多资源投向社区学院、职业学校和州立大学，培养会把 AI 用进地方银行和市政部门的人才。Ding 认为，中国的「人工智能＋」政策强调工厂、医院和行政服务；美国在云计算投入与采用上仍有优势。他也提出，美国企业应正视中国开放权重模型已进入本土开发者和企业工具链的现实。
