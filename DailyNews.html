我国算力总规模达 310 万 PFLOPS，全国数标委成立具身智能数据标准起草组

According to CCTV News, the National Data Administration disclosed that China's total computing power has reached 3.1 million PFLOPS, ranking second in the world. This data was disclosed during the second "Standard Week" event of the National Data Standardization Technical Committee, held in Xiamen from November 9 to 13. 据央视新闻报道，国家数据局披露，我国算力总规模已达到 310 万 PFLOPS，位居世界第二。该数据在全国数据标准化技术委员会第二次「标准周」活动期间披露。活动于 11 月 9 日至 13 日在厦门举行。

At the opening ceremony on November 10, the National Data Standardization Technical Committee established a working group for embodied AI data standards, as well as four industry application standard drafting working groups for meteorology, health, power, and commerce. The Standard Week also arranged discussions on technical topics such as security technical requirements for high-quality datasets and data circulation security audit specifications. 11 月 10 日的开幕式上，全国数标委成立了具身智能数据标准起草工作组，以及气象、卫生健康、电力、商务四个行业应用标准起草工作组。标准周还安排了高质量数据集安全技术要求、数据流通安全审计规范等技术议题的研讨。

北京印发具身智能机器人三年行动方案，提出上市一对一服务

The Beijing Municipal Bureau of Economy and Information Technology issued the "Beijing Action Plan for High-Quality Development of the Embodied AI Robot Industry (2026-2028)" on November 11. The plan proposes establishing a one-on-one listing service mechanism for embodied AI robot enterprises and fostering innovative enterprises in areas such as complete machines, components, models, and data collection. 北京市经济和信息化局 11 月 11 日印发《北京市具身智能机器人产业高质量发展行动方案（2026—2028 年）》。方案提出建立面向具身智能机器人企业的上市一对一服务机制，并在整机、零部件、模型和数据采集等领域培育创新型企业。

Beijing proposes to cultivate no fewer than 5 internationally leading and 10 domestically leading humanoid robot complete machine enterprises by 2028. The plan also proposes to add a dedicated embodied AI computing cluster with a total scale exceeding 50,000 PFLOPS, aiming for the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region's complete machine production capacity to account for 25% of the national total and a key component matching rate exceeding 60%. 北京提出，到 2028 年力争培育不少于 5 家国际一流、10 家国内领先的人形机器人整机企业。方案还提出新增建设总规模超过 50,000 PFLOPS 的具身智能专用算力集群，并以京津冀区域整机产能占全国 25%、关键零部件配套率超过 60% 为目标。

OpenRouter 公布的数据显示，平台 10 月处理了 790 万亿 Token，比 9 月增长 37%。按工具调用频率、对话轮数等信号分类，智能体类 API 密钥占当月 Token 用量的 76%，一年前这一比例为 8%。
