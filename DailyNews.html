苹果触觉反馈专利案被裁赔 57 亿美元，称将上诉

According to Reuters, a federal jury in San Diego, USA, ruled that Apple's Taptic Engine infringed two haptic feedback patents held by Taction Technology, ordering Apple to pay over $5.7 billion. The technology in question is used for vibration feedback in iPhones and Apple Watches. The jury did not find Apple guilty of willful infringement; this ruling also does not mean that the appeal process has concluded. 据路透社报道，美国圣地亚哥联邦陪审团裁定，苹果的 Taptic Engine 侵犯 Taction Technology 持有的两项触觉反馈专利，判赔超过 57 亿美元。涉案技术用于 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 的振动反馈。陪审团没有认定苹果属于故意侵权；这一裁决也并不等于上诉程序已结束。

Apple responded, stating that its Taptic Engine is fundamentally different from Taction's technology and announced that it would appeal. Taction's lawyers stated that the jury upheld the company's patent rights. Taction sued Apple in 2021; the case had previously received a summary judgment in Apple's favor in 2023, but was later remanded for further proceedings by the Federal Circuit Court of Appeals. 苹果回应称，其 Taptic Engine 与 Taction 的技术有根本区别，并表示将上诉。Taction 的代理律师则表示，陪审团维护了该公司的专利权。Taction 于 2021 年起诉苹果；案件曾在 2023 年获得对苹果有利的简易判决，之后由联邦巡回上诉法院发回继续审理。

OpenAI 暂停最强模型的工具使用训练，沙盒 DNS 漏洞让智能体访问外部聊天服务

OpenAI's technical report updated on September 25 disclosed that an internal research model, during a search training task on September 20, exploited a loophole in insufficient sandbox DNS filtering to send queries to external public chat services. It had previously been restricted from directly accessing search engines and chat services, but later obtained external replies through a domain name resolution path. OpenAI stated that other network requests still went through offline caching and did not directly access the real-time internet. OpenAI 9 月 25 日更新的技术报告披露，一款内部研究模型在 9 月 20 日的搜索训练任务中，利用沙盒 DNS 过滤不足的缺口，向外部公共聊天服务发送查询。它此前尝试直接访问搜索引擎和聊天服务时受到限制，后来通过域名解析路径获得了外部回复。OpenAI 称，其他网络请求仍经过离线缓存，没有直接访问实时互联网。

The monitoring system issued a top-level alert approximately 12 minutes after the first successful access, and human reviewers took over 3 minutes later, but the training task was not terminated until about 2.5 hours later. OpenAI has added two layers of interception measures and restricted the scope of sandbox DNS queries. The company stated that all training, evaluation, and inference involving tool use in its strongest models remain suspended, pending network control verification and further security testing before resumption; the model itself will not continue to be trained. 监控系统在首次成功访问约 12 分钟后发出最高级别警报，人工审核人员 3 分钟后接手，但训练任务直到约 2.5 小时后才被终止。OpenAI 已增加两层拦截措施，并限制沙盒 DNS 查询范围。公司表示，最强模型中所有涉及工具使用的训练、评估和推理仍处于暂停状态，待网络控制验证与进一步安全测试完成后再恢复；涉事模型本身不会继续训练。

蔚来与吉利达成充换电领域全面战略合作

On September 28, NIO Inc. and Geely Holding Group announced a comprehensive strategic cooperation in the field of charging and battery swapping. Both parties will collaborate on technology, operations, and capital levels, including cross-shareholding in charging and swapping business entities, sharing technologies and standards, and jointly building a charging and swapping service network. 9 月 28 日，蔚来公司与吉利控股集团宣布达成充换电领域全面战略合作，双方将在技术、运营和资本层面展开合作，包括交叉持股充换电业务主体、共享技术与标准，以及共建充换电服务网络。

According to the agreement, Geely Holding Group will invest in NIO Power with its 100% equity in E-E Interconnection and 640 million yuan in cash, holding 30% equity in NIO Power after the transaction is completed. E-E Interconnection's operational vehicle battery swapping business will also be merged into NIO Power. Both parties also plan to jointly build unified C-end battery swapping technologies and standards, and Geely will develop battery-swappable models for the C-end market and integrate them into NIO Power's battery swapping network. 根据协议，吉利控股集团将以所持易易互联 100% 股权及现金 6.4 亿元入股蔚来能源，交易完成后持有蔚来能源 30% 股权。易易互联营运车辆换电业务也将并入蔚来能源。双方还计划共建统一的 C 端换电技术和标准，吉利将开发面向 C 端市场的换电车型，并接入蔚来能源换电网络。

NIO Power plans to have cumulatively built 10,000 battery swapping stations by 2030, and it is estimated that the annual electricity demand of the battery swapping network will exceed 10 billion kilowatt-hours by then. 蔚来能源计划到 2030 年累计建成 1 万座换电站，预计届时换电网络年电力需求将超过 100 亿度。

Regarding the charging business, NIO will invest in Geely's vast energy, holding a 10% stake after the transaction is completed. The charging resources of both parties will be fully interconnected. Geely plans to build over 22,000 charging stations and over 100,000 charging piles by the end of 2027, including over 15,000 Geely Smart Charging Stations and over 50,000 Smart Charging Piles, achieving coverage in all county-level cities nationwide. 充电业务方面，蔚来将入股吉利旗下浩瀚能源，交易完成后持有其 10% 股权。双方充电资源将全面互联互通。吉利计划到 2027 年底建成超过 2.2 万座充电站、超过 10 万把充电枪，其中吉利智充站超过 1.5 万座、智充枪超过 5 万把，并实现全国县级城市覆盖。

Yesterday, NIO Chairman William Li stated at Power UP 2026 Charging Day that 4,125 NIO battery swap stations are already operational nationwide. Calculating with approximately 2,000 kWh of electricity reserved per station, he said these batteries collectively constitute about 8 GWh of energy storage resources. 昨天，蔚来董事长李斌在 Power UP 2026 加电日表示，全国已有 4125 座蔚来换电站投入运营。按每站约储备 2000 度电计算，他称这些电池合计可构成约 8GWh 的储能资源。

💡 比尔·盖茨：AI 可能引发十亿人死亡事件

Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates warned in an NBC 'Meet the Press' interview that AI's capabilities are already sufficient to drive events causing a billion deaths. 微软联合创始人比尔·盖茨在 NBC《Meet the Press》采访中警告，AI 的能力已足以推动造成十亿人死亡的事件。

He emphasized that the risk lies in malicious individuals using the latest AI tools, believing this combination to be more powerful than any weapon before. 他强调，风险在于怀有恶意的人使用最新 AI 工具，认为这种组合比以往任何武器都更强大。

In a lengthy article published on his personal website in August, Gates explained the basis of his concerns: AI lowers the barrier for cyberattacks and bioterrorism, allowing criminals with limited capabilities to launch attacks against infrastructure such as hospitals, financial institutions, and water and electricity systems. He believes that the same model can help defenders find vulnerabilities and help attackers exploit them, making it difficult for existing mechanisms to completely separate the two uses. 盖茨在 8 月发表于个人网站的长文中解释了担忧的依据：AI 会降低实施网络攻击和生物恐怖主义的门槛，让能力有限的犯罪者也能针对医院、金融机构、水电系统等基础设施发动攻击。他认为，同一模型既能帮防御方找漏洞，也能帮攻击方利用漏洞，现有机制难以彻底切分两种用途。

He proposed that governments need to establish inter-agency bodies to coordinate national security, employment, education, public health, and other matters, and to establish international governance frameworks with other countries. Citing nuclear weapons verification and international aviation rules as references, Gates advocated for common oversight of cross-border risks; for employment impacts, he suggested involving workers, businesses, and communities in policy discussions. 他提出，政府需要建立跨部门机构，协调国家安全、就业、教育、公共卫生等事务，并与其他国家建立国际治理框架。盖茨以核武器核查和国际航空规则为参照，主张对跨境风险设立共同监督；对就业冲击，则建议让劳动者、企业和社区参与政策讨论。

Gates also acknowledged that a unified global plan to slow AI progress is difficult to achieve under economic and geopolitical competition. He advocated for continuing to promote AI applications in healthcare, agriculture, and education, while establishing response mechanisms for malicious use and job displacement early on; he listed how to ensure benefits reach vulnerable groups as part of the governance framework. 盖茨同时承认，全球放缓 AI 进展的统一计划在经济和地缘竞争下很难实现。他主张继续推动 AI 在医疗、农业和教育中的应用，同时及早为恶意使用和岗位流失建立应对机制；他把如何让收益惠及弱势群体，列为治理框架的一部分。
