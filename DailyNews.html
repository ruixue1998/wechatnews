曝特努斯要求加快 AI 研发，推动 Siri 主动处理任务

According to Bloomberg, Apple's new CEO John Ternus is pushing software head Craig Federighi to take a more aggressive approach to AI, hoping to further transform Siri into an AI agent capable of autonomously completing tasks. 据彭博社报道，苹果新任 CEO John Ternus 正推动软件负责人 Craig Federighi 在 AI 上采取更激进的路线，希望将 Siri 进一步改造为能自主完成任务的 AI 智能体。

Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman said that Ternus held closed-door meetings with Federighi in recent weeks, expressing dissatisfaction with the pace of Apple's AI progress and demanding an accelerated roadmap, not just necessary improvements to Siri. 彭博社记者 Mark Gurman 称，Ternus 近几周与 Federighi 举行了闭门会议，对苹果 AI 的推进速度不满，要求加快路线图，不要止步于对 Siri 的必要改进。

Ternus stated on last week's first earnings call that Siri currently primarily operates in a request-based mode, but its modern orchestrator-driven architecture leaves room for future expansion of capabilities. Ternus 在上周的首次财报电话会上表示，Siri 目前主要仍是基于请求的模式，但其基于编排器驱动的现代架构，为未来扩展能力留出了空间。

Regarding agent capabilities, he said it's a promising tool, but it's necessary to ensure users are clear about what permissions they have granted and to protect security and privacy. 对于智能体能力，他称这是有前景的工具，但需要确保用户清楚自己授予了哪些权限，并保护安全和隐私。

Gurman believes that Siri still has a significant gap compared to ChatGPT Dots, Meta Muse, and Grok, which can take over continuous tasks, remember across conversations, and proactively push information; Apple has also not yet launched features similar to "computer usage." He expects Apple might make Siri closer to an agent as early as next year. Gurman 认为，Siri 与 ChatGPT Dots、Meta Muse 和 Grok 等仍有明显差距，后者可接手持续性任务、跨对话记忆并主动推送信息；苹果也尚未推出类似「计算机使用」的功能。他预计苹果最早可能在明年让 Siri 更接近智能体。

苹果称超 10 万款应用已适配 iPhone Duo

Apple announced on November 6 that over 100,000 apps have been optimized for the foldable iPhone Duo, which has been on the market for about two weeks. According to MacRumors' verification with Apple, this number refers to apps built with iOS 27.1 SDK or higher, which can fill the full width of both inner and outer screens and move controls to the status bar. 苹果在 11 月 6 日宣布，上市约两周的折叠屏 iPhone Duo 已有超过 10 万款应用完成优化。据 MacRumors 向苹果求证，这一数字指基于 iOS 27.1 SDK 或更高版本构建的应用，可铺满内外屏全宽，并将控件移至状态栏。

Apple previously mentioned in a press release that Weibo, iQiyi, WPS Office, Amap, Xiaohongshu, Taobao, and Xmind have been optimized for the iPhone Duo. 苹果此前在新闻稿提到，微博、爱奇艺、WPS Office、高德地图、小红书、淘宝和 Xmind 已针对 iPhone Duo 做了优化。

However, a large number of apps have not yet been adapted. MacRumors listed unadapted apps including Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Steam, PayPal, and YouTube Music, with Apple's own Apple Store app also among them. 不过，仍有大量应用尚未适配。MacRumors 列出的未适配应用包括 Google 办公套件、Microsoft Teams、Steam、PayPal 和 YouTube Music， 苹果自家的 Apple Store 应用也在其中。

适配程度分为三档： 适配程度分为三档：

  • Apps built with iOS 27.1 SDK can fill both inner and outer screens; 用 iOS 27.1 SDK 构建的应用可铺满内外屏；
  • Apps built with iOS 27 SDK can fill most of the inner screen area but will avoid the right-side status bar; 用 iOS 27 SDK 构建的应用可填满内屏大部分区域，但会避开右侧状态栏；
  • Apps built with iOS 26 or earlier SDK can only run centered, with black bars on both sides. 用 iOS 26 或更早 SDK 构建的应用只能居中运行，两侧留有黑边。

Apple states that apps using standard SwiftUI and UIKit components can automatically adapt, and thousands of optimized apps are still being released daily. 苹果称，使用标准 SwiftUI 和 UIKit 组件的应用可自动适配，每天仍有数千款优化后的应用上架。

英伟达 1140 万美元货物被盗

According to the Los Angeles Times, Oakland, California police are investigating a major theft involving Nvidia cargo, with the stolen items being AI chips and computer hardware, valued at up to $11.4 million (approximately 76.1 million RMB). 据洛杉矶时报报道，美国加州奥克兰警方正在调查一起涉及英伟达货物的重大盗窃案， 被盗物品为 AI 芯片和计算机硬件，价值高达 1140 万美元（约合 7610 万元人民币）。

Reports indicate that six pallets of Nvidia cargo were originally scheduled to be shipped from Oakland to Fort Worth, Texas, but after being picked up by a truck on August 29, the truck subsequently went missing. 报道称，6 托盘英伟达货物原定从奥克兰运往得克萨斯州沃思堡，但在 8 月 29 日被一辆卡车提走后，卡车随后失联。

Police attempted to track the vehicle, eventually losing its trace in San Bernardino County. Oakland police confirmed that the theft was reported on September 13 and is currently under investigation, but declined to release further details. 警方曾尝试追踪该车辆，最终在圣贝纳迪诺县失去踪迹。奥克兰警方确认，该盗窃案已于 9 月 13 日报案，目前仍在调查中，但拒绝公布更多细节。

According to CCTV Finance, the truck made multiple stops along the way, with single stops lasting several hours. Experts say that the relevant chips sell for nearly twice the price outside the U.S. compared to domestically, and it's not ruled out that the stolen goods have been transported out of the country. 另据央视财经消息，这辆卡车途中曾多次停靠，单次停留长达数小时。专家称，相关芯片在美国境外的售价接近美国本土的两倍，不排除赃物已被转运出境。

CCTV Finance stated that this is not the first time Nvidia has experienced cargo theft. 央视财经称，这并非英伟达首次遭遇货物被盗。

马斯克：SpaceX 明年 12 月年化营收或超 5000 亿美元

Elon Musk stated at the Barron's Investment Conference on November 6 that, according to SpaceX's current plans, he personally estimates the company's annualized revenue could exceed $500 billion by December 2027. This is an annualized figure based on the revenue rate at that time, which Musk referred to as his own estimate. 马斯克在 11 月 6 日的巴伦投资大会上表示，按 SpaceX 目前的计划，他个人估计，公司到 2027 年 12 月的年化营收可能超过 5000 亿美元。这是按届时营收速度折算的年化规模，马斯克把它称为自己的估计。

Regarding the source of growth, Musk placed ground data centers and orbital computing power within the same planning framework. He believes that ground expansion is primarily constrained by power supply and chip availability, and data center construction cannot simply begin once chips are acquired. 谈到增长从何而来，马斯克把地面数据中心和轨道算力放在同一套规划里。他认为，地面扩张首先受制于供电和芯片供应，数据中心建设并非只要拿到芯片就能启动。

SpaceX hopes to reduce launch costs with reusable Starship vehicles, creating conditions for sending computing equipment into orbit. SpaceX 希望用可重复使用的星舰降低发射成本，为把计算设备送入轨道创造条件。

He also discussed Tesla's business in China. Taking the Model Y as an example, he said that it's not easy for this car, produced at the Shanghai factory, to achieve sales success in the Chinese market due to the strong competitiveness of Chinese car manufacturers. He also said he likes going to China and finds traveling there inspiring. 他还谈到特斯拉在中国的业务。以 Model Y 为例，他说上海工厂生产的这款车在中国市场取得销量成绩并不容易，原因是中国车企的竞争力很强。 他也说自己喜欢去中国，觉得在中国旅行让人受到启发。

Barron compared the revenue estimate with SpaceX's historical scale during the interview. The annualized revenue mentioned by both refers to the income rate at a specific point in time and cannot be directly compared with the company's full-year revenue from previous years on the same basis. 巴伦在访谈中将营收估计与 SpaceX 历史规模作比较。两人口中的年化营收是特定时点的收入速度，不能直接与公司过去完整年度的营收作同口径比较。

Regarding a more recent timeframe, Musk also predicted that fourth-quarter revenue growth would exceed 50%. 谈及更近的时间点，马斯克还预计第四季度营收增幅将超过五成。

成都一男子戴 AI 眼镜考驾照作弊，现场演示多选题全错

NASA 推进核动力月球车项目，目标 2028 年底发射

NASA will advance the development of the nuclear-powered lunar rover PROMISE, aiming for a launch by the end of 2028, with a planned 18-month mission on the lunar surface. Blue Origin will use its Mark 1 lander to deliver this six-wheeled rover to the Moon's south pole. NASA 将推进核动力月球车 PROMISE 的研制，目标在 2028 年底发射，计划在月面执行为期 18 个月的任务。蓝色起源将用 Mark 1 着陆器把这辆六轮月球车送到月球南极。

OLM, the Japanese animation studio behind "Pokémon" TV anime and films, showcased a coloring tool in Taipei. According to Tatsuo Shikura, director of OLM Digital, at the Taiwan Creative Content Fest, artists first manually complete 3 frames, then the system takes about 90 seconds to learn the work's lines and color scheme, and then about 15 seconds to process one frame. Increasing manual samples can improve accuracy. 制作《宝可梦》电视动画和电影的日本动画公司 OLM 在台北展示了一套上色工具。按 OLM Digital 董事四仓达夫在台湾创意内容大会上的介绍，画师先手工完成 3 帧，系统用约 90 秒学习这部作品的线条与配色，再用约 15 秒处理一帧。增加手工样本可以提高准确度。

Each work is trained only with its own footage. When encountering new characters or creatures, artists can draw a small number of samples first to allow the system to adapt to that character. Tatsuo Shikura estimates that during the proof-of-concept phase, this tool reduced coloring workload by about 30%, while the workload for personnel responsible for frame-by-frame inspection increased. 每部作品只用自己的画面训练。遇到新角色或生物，画师可以先画少量样本，让系统适应该角色。四仓达夫估计，在概念验证阶段，这套工具让上色工作量减少约三成，负责逐帧检查的人员工作量则有所增加。
