曝苹果调整 iOS 28 开发节奏，首阶段留出六周探索功能

According to Mark Gurman of Bloomberg, Apple has adjusted the development process for its next-generation operating system, iOS 28. The first development phase (M1) allocates six weeks for coding, exploration, and feature development, followed by only two weeks for integration. 据彭博社 Mark Gurman 报道，苹果已调整下一代操作系统 iOS 28 的开发流程，首个开发阶段（M1）留出六周用于编码、探索和功能开发，随后仅有两周用于整合。

The report states that Apple is currently deeply advancing the development of next year's new systems, with iOS 28 and iPadOS 28 codenamed Bell, and macOS 28 codenamed Poppy. 报道称，苹果目前已深入推进明年的新系统开发，其中 iOS 28 和 iPadOS 28 代号为 Bell，macOS 28 代号为 Poppy。

Conventionally, Apple's annual new system development is divided into three main phases: M1, M2, and M3. Each phase lasts eight weeks, comprising four weeks of coding and four weeks of integration, where integration refers to incorporating various features into the operating system. 按照惯例，苹果每年的新系统开发分为 M1、M2、M3 三个主要阶段，每个阶段持续八周，包含四周编码和四周整合，整合指的是将各项功能集成进操作系统。

In this M1 phase, Apple did not follow the usual two four-week arrangement. Instead, engineers spent six weeks on coding, exploration, and feature development, followed by two weeks for integration. The report mentioned that some employees expressed liking this change, believing it allows more time for conceptualization and experimentation before features are locked down. 此次 M1 阶段，苹果没有沿用两个四周的常规安排，而是让工程师先用六周进行编码、探索和功能开发，再用两周完成整合。报道提到，有员工表示喜欢这一变化，认为这样能在功能锁定前留出更多时间构思和尝试。

The report also mentioned that Apple's new CEO, John Ternus, is not satisfied with the company's AI progress and has held closed-door meetings with software head Craig Federighi in recent weeks, hoping to accelerate the AI roadmap. 报道同时提到，苹果新任 CEO John Ternus 对公司 AI 进展并不满意，近几周已与软件负责人 Craig Federighi 举行闭门会议，希望其加快 AI 路线图。

谷歌发布两套第八代 TPU 系统，分别面向长上下文和保密推理

Google announced TPU 8i Max and TPU 8tx on November 9, expanding the eighth-generation TPU lineup that debuted in April this year. Both systems utilize existing chips, with adjustments made to chip packaging and accompanying hardware. 谷歌 11 月 9 日公布 TPU 8i Max 和 TPU 8tx，扩充今年 4 月亮相的第八代 TPU 阵容。两套系统沿用现有芯片，分别调整了芯片封装和配套硬件。

  • TPU 8i Max packages two TPU 8i chips together. Google states that compared to TPU 8i, the new system's computing power, on-chip SRAM, and high-bandwidth memory bandwidth are all doubled, used for processing entire codebases or long-context tasks such as long videos. TPU 8i Max 把两枚 TPU 8i 芯片封装在一起。谷歌称，相比 TPU 8i，新系统的算力、片上 SRAM 和高带宽内存带宽均翻倍，用于处理整套代码库或长视频等长上下文任务。
  • TPU 8tx uses the same chip as TPU 8t, paired with a custom AMD host processor. The hardware isolates sensitive data and model weights during inference, and enterprises can also fine-tune models with private data on the same system. TPU 8tx 使用与 TPU 8t 相同的芯片，搭配定制 AMD 主机处理器。硬件在推理时隔离敏感数据和模型权重，企业也可以在同一套系统上用私有数据微调模型。

The new model can directly generate images with an Alpha channel, preserving transparent and translucent parts, and the generated elements can be directly used as independent design assets. 字节跳动 Seed 团队 11 月 9 日发布图像模型 Seedream 5.5。新模型可直接生成带 Alpha 通道的图片，透明和半透明的部分可以保留下来，生成的元素可直接用作独立设计素材。

The team also demonstrated the results of continuously editing the same character image for 100 rounds. Each round used the previous image as a base, modifying clothing, accessories, and desktop objects sequentially. In the later stages, details like rings and spectacle frames could still be adjusted individually. Seed stated that this version underwent specialized training for local editing to reduce color shifts and detail loss in unmodified areas during repeated generation. 团队也展示了同一张人物图连续编辑 100 轮的结果。每轮以前一张图为底稿，先后修改服装、配饰和桌面物件，到后段仍能单独调整戒指、镜框等细节。Seed 称，这一版针对局部编辑做了专项训练，以减少未修改区域在反复生成中的偏色和细节流失。

The new version also improves the rendering of lighting, materials, skin textures, and dense small text, and is being successively launched on Jiemeng, Doubao, Doubao Work, and Huoshan Fangzhou. 新版也改善了光照、材质、皮肤纹理和密集小字的渲染，正陆续在即梦、豆包、豆包工作和火山方舟上线。

百度披露 ERNIE Evolve，已用于优化文心 5.5 Flash 推理

Baidu Wenxin unveiled its automated research system, ERNIE Evolve, on November 9, and disclosed that it has used it to optimize the inference efficiency of Wenxin 5.5 Flash. The system proposes research directions based on historical experimental results, and then multiple agents divide tasks to select code starting points and modify code. Each round of changes must pass independent evaluation and review, and experimental code and results will be reserved for subsequent research. 百度文心 11 月 9 日公开了自动研究系统 ERNIE Evolve，并披露已用它优化文心 5.5 Flash 的推理效率。系统会根据历史实验结果提出研究方向，再由多个智能体分工选择代码起点、修改代码。每轮改动须通过独立评测和复核，实验代码和结果会留给后续研究使用。

Baidu listed three types of actual R&D tasks in its technical blog, including low-latency inference on Kunlunxin P800, deterministic inference throughput on GPUs, and single-concurrent decoding speed. In the P800 low-latency task, the team limited the scope of changes to chip adaptation plugins, keeping the model and deployment method unchanged. The system successively attempted operator fusion, speculative decoding, and scheduling and communication optimization. The optimal version achieved a generation speed of 623.6% of the initial baseline and passed the team's correctness checks. 百度在技术博客中列出了三类实际研发任务，包括昆仑芯 P800 上的低时延推理、GPU 上的确定性推理吞吐，以及单并发解码速度。在 P800 的低时延任务中，团队限定改动范围为芯片适配插件，保持模型和部署方式不变。系统先后尝试融合算子、投机解码以及调度和通信优化，最优版本的生成速度达到初始基线的 623.6%，并通过了团队的正确性检查。

Baidu stated that it plans to use this method for training system optimization, model capability research, and evaluation system iteration, and will continue to limit the system's permissions and resource scope. 百度称，接下来计划将这套方法用于训练系统优化、模型能力研究和评测体系迭代，并继续限制系统的权限和资源范围。
