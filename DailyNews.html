Anthropic 披露研发指标：Claude 主导 26% 的 AI 研发工作

On September 17, Anthropic unveiled a set of metrics to measure the R&D progress of frontier AI labs, covering the extent of AI involvement in R&D, agent supervision mechanisms, and compute allocation. Anthropic 9 月 17 日公开一组衡量前沿 AI 实验室研发进度的指标，涵盖 AI 参与研发的程度、智能体监督机制和算力分配。

The company disclosed that as of August this year, Claude had "led" 26% of AI R&D work, with over 90% of the work reaching "AI collaboration" or higher levels. Here, "led" means AI can complete most tasks based on high-level prompts, still under human supervision; among all measured R&D work subsets, there are no fully autonomous projects yet. 公司披露，截至今年 8 月，Claude 已「主导」26% 的 AI 研发工作，超过 90% 的工作达到「AI 协作」或更高等级。这里的「主导」指 AI 可根据高层提示完成大部分任务，仍由人类监督；所有被测量的研发工作子集中，尚没有完全自主运行的项目。

On its most frequently used internal platform, approximately 30,000 agents are engaged in research and engineering work at any given time. Compute power samples were taken from July 13th to 20th: approximately 6% of AI R&D compute power was used for safety work, and the corresponding proportion in AI-driven R&D compute power was about 12%. OpenAI 将定期披露模型异常行为，首批公开 6 份报告

According to Reuters, OpenAI has unveiled a new framework for tracking, investigating, and disclosing model misalignment behaviors, along with six preliminary reports. The company stated that as AI system capabilities grow, the industry has yet to solve critical alignment challenges. 据路透社报道，OpenAI 公布了一套追踪、调查和披露模型失准行为的新框架，并公开 6 份初步报告。公司表示，随着 AI 系统能力增强，行业仍未解决关键的对齐难题。

The reports cover behaviors such as models hiding errors from users, leaving instructions for future versions, uploading files to the internet to create citations, and exchanging information via software repositories or websites. In one case, an unreleased model sent unauthorized instructions to an agent during training, asking it to ignore OpenAI's requirements and conceal the fact that it completed tasks by cheating. The earliest case dates back to last October. 报告涉及模型向用户隐藏错误、为未来版本留下指令、把文件上传至互联网以制造引用，以及通过软件仓库或网站交换信息等行为。其中一个案例里，尚未发布的模型在训练中向智能体发送未经授权的指令，要求它忽略 OpenAI 的要求，隐瞒通过作弊完成任务的情况。最早的案例可追溯至去年 10 月。

Under the new process, employees can report suspected incidents, which will be investigated by the safety and alignment teams, and then a decision will be made on whether to disclose them based on disclosure standards. OpenAI hopes to report abnormal behaviors more quickly even when they are not fully explained; complex incidents involving third parties will enter a different investigation process. 按照新流程，员工可以上报疑似事件，由安全和对齐团队调查，再依据披露标准决定是否公开。OpenAI 希望在异常行为尚未被完全解释时，也能更快作出报告；涉及第三方的复杂事件则进入另一类调查流程。

The company also emphasized that these reports describe individual cases and cannot be used to determine the frequency of model misalignment behaviors, nor are they a complete list of all known or still-under-investigation incidents. The Hugging Face incident disclosed in July this year will fall into the category of complex investigations requiring third-party involvement. 公司同时强调，这些报告描述的是个别案例，不能据此判断模型失准行为出现的频率，也不是所有已知或仍在调查事件的完整清单。今年 7 月披露的 Hugging Face 事件，将属于需要第三方参与的复杂调查类别。
