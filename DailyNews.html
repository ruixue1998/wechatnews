During his visits to Asian regions including China, Tim Cook observed an increasing number of consumers using foldable devices from manufacturers like Samsung Electronics and Huawei. OpenAI 发布 Navier–Stokes 千禧年难题候选解，等待数学界检验

On September 8, OpenAI announced a candidate solution to the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem, simultaneously releasing a mathematical paper and a Lean formal proof. The company states that this proof provides a construction for the formation of singularities in smooth three-dimensional incompressible fluids within a finite time, corresponding to cases C and D in the Clay Mathematics Institute's problem statement. OpenAI 9 月 8 日公布一份 Navier–Stokes 存在性与光滑性问题的候选解，同时发布了数学论文和 Lean 形式化证明。公司称，这份证明给出了光滑三维不可压缩流体在有限时间内形成奇点的构造，对应克莱数学研究所问题表述中的 C 和 D 类情形。

OpenAI states that the proof was completed by an internal model with capabilities superior to GPT-6 Astra, with the system at one point coordinating approximately 10,000 concurrent agents. It took about 88 hours from initiation to obtaining the candidate solution, followed by GPT-6 Astra spending 17 hours to complete the Lean formalization and verification; the Navier–Stokes project generated approximately 2.7 million agent messages and 130 billion output tokens. OpenAI 称，证明由一个能力高于 GPT-6 Astra 的内部模型完成，系统一度协调约 1 万个并发 Agent。从启动到得到候选解用时约 88 小时，随后由 GPT-6 Astra 花费 17 小时完成 Lean 形式化和验证；Navier–Stokes 项目共产生约 270 万条代理消息和 1300 亿个输出 token。

This work also brought up a contemporary controversy regarding Euler equations research. Tristan Buckmaster, a mathematics professor at New York University, questioned whether OpenAI used project data from him and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge in Codex. OpenAI stated that the solution process did not access specific user data but could not completely rule out that de-identified usage data had participated in model improvements; the problems addressed by the proofs publicly released by both parties are also not the same. 这项工作也牵出了同期的 Euler 方程研究争议。纽约大学数学教授 Tristan Buckmaster 质疑 OpenAI 是否利用了他与 Anthropic 研究人员 Levent Alpöge 在 Codex 中的项目数据。OpenAI 称求解过程没有访问具体用户数据，但不能完全排除去标识化使用数据曾参与模型改进；双方公开的证明所针对的问题也并不相同。

This candidate solution has not yet undergone independent verification by the global mathematical community. According to Clay Mathematics Institute rules, a solution must first be published in a qualified publication, undergo at least two years of rigorous review, and gain widespread acceptance before being considered for an award. OpenAI stated it has no intention of applying for an award for this result. 这份候选解尚未经过全球数学界的独立检验。按克莱数学研究所规则，一项解答需先在合格出版物发表，至少经过两年严格审查并获得广泛认可，才会进入奖项考虑程序。OpenAI 称无意为该结果申请奖项。

两部门要求车企定期报告供应商账期，重点企业每年接受评估

On September 7, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the State Administration for Market Regulation issued a notice to further regulate payments made by automotive companies to suppliers for goods, engineering, and services. Car manufacturers are required to establish a supplier payment management system and submit semi-annual and annual implementation reports by the end of July and the end of January of the following year, respectively. 工业和信息化部、市场监管总局 9 月 7 日发布通知，进一步规范汽车企业向供应商支付采购货物、工程和服务等款项。车企需建立供应商账款支付管理制度，并在每年 7 月底和次年 1 月底前分别提交半年度、年度执行情况报告。

The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology will commission a third-party agency to conduct an annual survey and evaluation, covering all key automotive companies on the list. The evaluation will review company documents and data, verify on-site conditions, and interview employees, focusing on information such as payment terms, payment methods and proportions, and the scale of accounts payable. The results will be publicly released. 工信部将委托第三方机构每年开展一次调研评估，对名单内重点车企实现全覆盖。评估会查阅企业文件和数据、核验现场并访谈员工，关注账期、支付方式及占比、应付账款规模等信息，结果将公开发布。

For automotive companies with large accounts payable, those intentionally extending payment terms, or those frequently complained about by small and medium-sized enterprises, the competent authorities will conduct interviews and urge rectification; if violations are involved, they will be dealt with according to law. Previously, the industry launched a "60-day payment term commitment" in 2025, and this notice incorporates corporate reporting, third-party evaluation, complaint verification, and law enforcement coordination into the normalized management process. 对于应付账款规模较大、故意拉长账期或被中小企业投诉较多的车企，主管部门将约谈并督促整改；涉及违法违规的，将依法处理。此前行业在 2025 年发起「60 天账期承诺」，此次通知把企业报告、第三方评估、投诉核查和执法衔接写入常态化管理流程。
