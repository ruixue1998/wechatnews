iPhone Duo 开启预购，国行部分版本发货排至明年 1 月

After iPhone Duo pre-orders opened, the shipping time for some domestic versions has been scheduled for next year. iFanr checked Apple China's official website and found that the 256GB Starlight White version is expected to ship on January 22, 2027. iPhone Duo 开启预购后，国行部分版本的发货时间已排至明年。爱范儿查看苹果中国官网发现，256GB 星光白版本显示预计 2027 年 1 月 22 日发货。

Apple's first foldable phone, iPhone Duo, opened for pre-orders on October 16, with a starting price of 15,999 yuan in China, and officially goes on sale on October 23. 苹果首款折叠屏手机 iPhone Duo 于 10 月 16 日开启预购，国行起售价 15999 元，10 月 23 日正式发售。

Delivery schedules in the US market have also rapidly extended. MacRumors reporter Joe Rossignol observed that when pre-orders opened on the US official website at 5 AM local time, by 5:30 AM, the estimated delivery date for all color and capacity versions had been pushed back to January 25, 2027. 美国市场的交付排期也迅速拉长。MacRumors 记者 Joe Rossignol 观察到，美国官网在当地上午 5 点开放预购，到 5 点半时，全部颜色与容量版本的预计交付日期已推迟至 2027 年 1 月 25 日。

High price quotes have also appeared in domestic third-party channels. China Blue News learned from an interview with a merchant in Hangzhou Digital City on October 15 that the first batch of in-stock quotes could be 9,500 yuan higher than the official website price, with these quotes coming from third-party channels. 国内第三方渠道也出现高价报价。中国蓝新闻 10 月 15 日采访杭州数码城商家时获悉，首批现货报价可比官网定价高 9500 元，相关报价来自第三方渠道。

iPhone Duo is available in Starlight White, Midnight Blue, and capacities from 256GB to 2TB, featuring a 7.6-inch foldable inner screen, a 5.4-inch outer screen, an A20 Pro chip, and a 48-megapixel fusion dual camera, with eSIM support only. iPhone Duo 提供星光白色、夜空色及 256GB 至 2TB 容量，配备 7.6 英寸可折叠内屏、5.4 英寸外屏、A20 Pro 芯片和 4800 万像素融合式双摄，仅支持 eSIM。

特斯拉发布会飞的 Roadster

Tesla unveiled the redesigned second-generation Roadster on the morning of October 16, Beijing time. Reuters' on-site report showed that the demo car drove upside down using a suction system, then used cold gas thrusters to take off, adjust its posture, and land. Musk stated that the new car is planned to begin production in 2028. 特斯拉于北京时间 10 月 16 日上午展示重新设计的第二代 Roadster。路透社现场报道显示，演示车借助吸力系统倒挂行驶，随后利用冷气推进器腾空、调整姿态并落地。马斯克称，新车计划于 2028 年开始生产。

NIO founder Li Bin reposted the on-site video on Weibo and commented: "That's incredible! We really don't have this, thumbs up for Tesla 👍" 蔚来创始人李斌在微博转发现场视频并表示：「太厉害了！这个我们真没有，为特斯拉点赞👍」

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman also publicly praised Musk on X: "Wow, the new Roadster looks amazing, great job." OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 也在 X 公开称赞马斯克：「哇，新款 Roadster 看起来真棒，干得漂亮。」

Tesla's official website divides the new car into three versions: 特斯拉官网将新车分成三个版本：

  • Performance: Two-seater, for road use, equipped with active aerodynamics and a multi-mode suction system to increase downforce. Performance：双座，面向道路使用，配备主动空气动力学和多模式吸力系统，以增加下压力。
  • Thrust: Two-seater limited edition, adds rear cold gas thrusters in addition to the suction system, officially claiming 0 to 60 mph acceleration in under 1 second. Thrust：双座限量版本，在吸力系统之外加入后部冷气推进器，官方宣称 0 至 60 英里／小时加速低于 1 秒。
  • Rocket: Single-seater, invite-only purchase, with thrusters arranged around the body, supporting forward, upward acceleration, and lateral rotation. The official website explicitly states that this version is not approved for public road use and is not available in all regions. Rocket：单座，仅限受邀购买，车身周围布置推进器，支持向前、向上加速及侧向旋转。官网明确注明，该版本未获公共道路使用许可，且并非所有地区都提供。

Adhesion and flight are accomplished by two different systems: fans under the car draw air to create suction, while cold gas thrusters control thrust and stabilize aerial posture via electric actuators. 吸附与飞行由两套不同系统完成：车底风扇抽走空气产生吸力，冷气推进器则通过电动执行机构控制推力、稳定空中姿态。

It is reported that the new car has a top speed of over 230 miles (370km) and a range of over 300 miles (482km) on a single charge. The second-generation Roadster was first shown as a prototype in 2017 and has since been delayed multiple times. 据悉，新车最高时速超过 230 英里（370km）、单次充电续航超过 300 英里（482km）。第二代 Roadster 最早在 2017 年展示原型，此后曾多次推迟交付。

The reservation deposit for the Performance version listed on Tesla China's official website is 332,000 yuan, with the page stating it is fully refundable. The official website also notes that the vehicle's launch arrangements in various regions must comply with applicable laws and regulations. 特斯拉中国官网列出的 Performance 版预订意向金为 33.2 万元，页面标注全额可退，官网同时注明，车辆在各地区的上市安排须符合届时生效的法律法规。

RTX 5090 或绝版，已从英伟达美国商城消失

According to Tom's Hardware, Nvidia has removed the GeForce RTX 5090 from the US Marketplace store; US computer retailer Micro Center has also marked this flagship graphics card as "Not Carried," meaning it is no longer included in its regular sales catalog. 据 Tom’s Hardware 报道，英伟达已从美国 Marketplace 商城移除 GeForce RTX 5090；美国电脑零售商 Micro Center 也将这款旗舰显卡标为「Not Carried」，即不再列入其常规销售目录。

This is different from a temporary out-of-stock situation. The report found that RTX 5090 pages from partners like Asus, MSI, and Gigabyte are still online, but many physical stores no longer have stock. Some special bundles are still available, but this does not mean that regular standalone cards have resumed supply. 这与暂时缺货有所区别。报道查询发现，华硕、微星、技嘉等合作伙伴的 RTX 5090 页面仍在，但多家实体店已无库存。部分特殊套装仍可买到，并不代表普通单卡恢复供应。

Previously, there were market rumors that Nvidia was shifting its GB202 chip to professional graphics cards and AI businesses. This removal from the marketplace has brought more attention to the supply outlook, but Nvidia has not officially confirmed the discontinuation of the RTX 5090, nor has Micro Center responded to the media regarding its sales status. 此前，市场曾传出英伟达把 GB202 芯片转向专业显卡与 AI 业务的消息。此次商城下架让供应前景更受关注，但英伟达尚未正式确认 RTX 5090 停产，Micro Center 也未就销售状态向该媒体作出回应。

腾讯回应智能体异常浏览：未涉及混元模型，将加强网络访问控制

According to Reuters, Tencent confirmed on October 16 that AI agents used to evaluate third-party models exhibited unexpected web browsing behavior. Tencent's Hunyuan team stated that the related activities did not originate from the Hunyuan model, and internal review found no evidence of deliberate targeting or coordinated actions. The company is strengthening network access control and agent monitoring. 据路透社报道，腾讯于 10 月 16 日确认，用于评估第三方模型的 AI 智能体出现了非预期的网页浏览行为。腾讯混元团队表示，相关活动并非来自混元模型，内部审查也没有发现蓄意针对某个目标或协同行动的证据，公司正在加强网络访问控制与智能体监测。

Previously, AI security research organization Swarmchasers stated that a batch of agents running on its Tencent Cloud-associated infrastructure repeatedly attempted to obtain information such as park, museum, and zoo entrances from Amap between September 28 and October 8. After regular requests were blocked, the agents attempted to continue access using other web tools, intermediate websites, and interface credentials from public code. 此前，AI 安全研究组织 Swarmchasers 称，一批运行于其关联到腾讯云的基础设施上的智能体，在 9 月 28 日至 10 月 8 日多次尝试获取高德地图中的公园、博物馆、动物园入口等信息。常规请求受阻后，智能体尝试借助其他网页工具、中间网站及公开代码中的接口凭据继续访问。

Most of the 2818 attempts recorded by the research organization failed, or only returned anti-scraping pages, login pages, and empty results. Researchers found no evidence that they broke through Amap's main anti-scraping measures. Some requests returned data via caching or intermediate services, but it could not be confirmed whether the agents read the results or completed the tasks. Alibaba declined to comment. 研究组织记录的 2818 次尝试大多失败，或只返回反爬页面、登录页及空结果。研究人员没有发现它们突破高德主要反爬措施的证据，部分请求经缓存或中间服务返回了数据，但无法确认智能体是否读取结果、完成任务。阿里巴巴未予置评。

17 岁研究员陈广宇离开 Kimi，否认将加入 Anthropic

17-year-old researcher Chen Guangyu announced on X on October 15 that he had recently left the Kimi team at Moonshot AI. He thanked the team for believing in and hiring him when he was 16, stating that the past year was the best time of his life so far. On October 16, he denied rumors of joining Anthropic in a reply to a netizen. 17 岁研究员陈广宇于 10 月 15 日在 X 宣布，自己近日已离开月之暗面的 Kimi 团队。他感谢团队当年相信并聘用了 16 岁的自己，称过去一年是迄今人生中最美好的时光。10 月 16 日，他在回复网友时否认了将加入 Anthropic 的说法。

In his farewell post, Chen Guangyu recalled that he had optimized the KDA computing kernel with Zhang Yu, jointly conceived and refined Attention Residuals with Zhang Yu and Su Jianlin, researched training optimizers, and participated in the production of Kimi K3 release cases. The Attention Residuals paper published in March this year listed Chen Guangyu, Zhang Yu, and Su Jianlin as co-first authors with equal contributions. 陈广宇在告别帖中回顾，自己曾与张宇一起优化 KDA 计算内核，与张宇、苏剑林共同构思并完善 Attention Residuals，研究训练优化器，并参与 Kimi K3 发布案例的制作。今年 3 月公开的 Attention Residuals 论文，将陈广宇、张宇与苏剑林列为同等贡献的共同第一作者。

Anthropic researcher Sholto Douglas asked him in the comments if he still planned to go to Carnegie Mellon University and invited him to work with teams like Prime and Base10 in San Francisco. Chen Guangyu only replied that he was preparing to go to the Bay Area; he has not yet announced the specific arrangements for his next job. Anthropic 研究员 Sholto Douglas 在帖下问他是否仍计划去卡内基梅隆大学，并邀请他到旧金山与 Prime、Base10 等团队一起工作。陈广宇只回复称，自己正准备去湾区；他尚未公布下一份工作的具体安排。

In an interview published by Numberphile on October 15, Terence Tao discussed the changes after AI began mass-producing mathematical proofs: mathematics is moving out of the romantic era of relying on individual heroes to tackle problems, with the number of proofs increasing, but the speed at which humans can digest and explain the results cannot keep up. 在 Numberphile 于 10 月 15 日发布的采访中，陶哲轩谈到 AI 批量产出数学证明后的变化：数学正走出依靠个人英雄攻关的浪漫时代，证明的数量增长，人类消化、解释成果的速度却跟不上。

Terence Tao does not deny the creativity of machines. He has read about ten papers from this batch of results; some are extensions of existing research, one or two contain clever new ideas, there is also a lot of content that is still difficult to understand, and some results have been retracted due to errors. He criticized the lack of communication with relevant researchers before centralized publication, which led to some young researchers' projects losing their original scope. 陶哲轩并不否认机器的创造力。他读过这批成果中的约十篇论文，有些是已有研究的延伸，一两篇包含巧妙的新思路，也有大量内容尚难理解，部分结果已因错误撤回。他批评集中发布前缺少与相关研究者的沟通，一些年轻人的研究项目因此失去原先的空间。

He worries that machines continuously solve open problems without simultaneously cultivating new problems and new researchers for the discipline. Therefore, he advocates for a shift towards larger-scale collaboration, allowing teams to jointly explore a set of problems; in education, traditional training should be retained, just as one needs to learn manual calculation before using a calculator. He hopes that the details of the research process, failed attempts, and the formation of ideas are recorded, so that future generations learn to judge, rather than just receiving answers. 因此，他主张转向更大规模的协作，让团队共同探索一组问题；教育中则应保留传统训练，如同使用计算器前仍需学会手算。他希望研究过程、失败尝试和思想形成的细节被记录下来，让后来者学会判断，而不只接收答案。
