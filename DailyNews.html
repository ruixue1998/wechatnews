国行 Apple Watch 上线睡眠呼吸暂停通知功能

Early this morning, Apple pushed out iOS 27.0.2 and watchOS 27.0.1 updates, and Apple Watch in mainland China officially supports sleep apnea notifications. Apple had already launched this feature in 2024, and in September this year, its "Mobile Sleep Apnea Indication Alert Software" received approval from the National Medical Products Administration. 今天凌晨，苹果推送 iOS 27.0.2 和 watchOS 27.0.1 更新，国行 Apple Watch 正式支持睡眠呼吸暂停通知。该功能苹果早在 2024 年就已推出，今年 9 月，其「移动睡眠呼吸暂停迹象提示软件」获得国家药监局批准。

Supported models include Apple Watch Series 9 and later, Apple Watch Ultra 2 and later, and Apple Watch SE 3. This feature is only available to users aged 18 and above who have not previously been diagnosed with sleep apnea. Users must first enable "Track Sleep with Apple Watch" and then complete the setup in the "Health" App. 支持机型包括 Apple Watch Series 9 及后续机型、Apple Watch Ultra 2 及后续机型，以及 Apple Watch SE 3。该功能仅面向年满 18 周岁、此前未被诊断患有睡眠呼吸暂停的用户，需先开启「通过 Apple Watch 跟踪睡眠」，再在「健康」App 中完成设置。

This feature captures subtle body movements during sleep via an accelerometer, and an algorithm identifies signs of respiratory disturbances. Users need to wear the watch to sleep for at least 10 nights within 30 days, and the system evaluates once every 30 days. 该功能通过加速度计捕捉睡眠时的细微身体运动，并由算法识别呼吸紊乱迹象。用户需在 30 天内至少佩戴手表入睡 10 个晚上，系统每 30 天评估一次。

If at least half of the effective monitoring nights are determined to have "increased respiratory disturbances," users may receive notifications of moderate to severe sleep apnea signs and can export a PDF health report for doctor's reference. This feature is not a real-time alarm and will not wake the user when symptoms occur. 若至少一半的有效监测夜晚被判定为「呼吸紊乱上升」，就可能收到中度至重度睡眠呼吸暂停迹象通知，并可导出 PDF 健康报告供医生参考。该功能并非实时报警，也不会在症状发生时唤醒用户。

According to statistics, over 1 billion people worldwide suffer from sleep apnea, with nearly 80% remaining undiagnosed. 据统计，全球超过 10 亿人患有睡眠呼吸暂停，其中近 80% 尚未确诊。

华为称下一代麒麟芯片已就绪，9050 Pro GPU 性能提升 40%

Huawei held a Kirin media communication conference at Lianqiuhu, Shanghai, on October 13. Xu Songyu, Minister of Wireless Terminal Chip Product Management Department of Huawei's Semiconductor Business, for the first time unveiled the detailed architecture diagram of Kirin 9050 Pro and introduced its CPU, GPU, and image processing capabilities. 华为 10 月 13 日在上海练秋湖举行麒麟媒体沟通会。华为半导体业务无线终端芯片产品管理部部长徐松余首次公布麒麟 9050 Pro 的详细架构图，并介绍 CPU、GPU 和影像处理能力。

The Kirin 9050 Pro features a 9-core, 16-thread Lingxi CPU. Coupled with HarmonyOS application parallelization optimization, Huawei claims a 31% reduction in stuttering in typical scenarios. The Maliang GPU adopts a fourth-generation microarchitecture and second-generation ray tracing hardware acceleration, boosting performance by 40%, reducing power consumption by 45%, and improving ray tracing performance by 78%. 麒麟 9050 Pro 搭载 9 核 16 线程灵犀 CPU，配合鸿蒙应用并行化优化，华为称典型场景卡顿降低 31%。马良 GPU 采用第四代微架构及第二代光线追踪硬件加速，性能提升 40%、功耗降低 45%、光追性能提升 78%。

The imaging section uses a new generation AI ISP, supporting 200-megapixel fusion processing, with a 2x increase in pixel throughput; it also supports 18EV high dynamic range, real-time pixel-level skin tone segmentation, and BT.2020 color gamut. 影像部分采用新一代 AI ISP，支持 2 亿像素融合处理，像素吞吐率提升 2 倍；同时支持 18EV 高动态范围、实时像素级肤色分割及 BT.2020 色域。

Xu Songyu stated that the Kirin 9050 series has been mass-produced with the support of China's semiconductor industry, but the logic folding path still needs to address issues such as toolchains, process variation compensation, inter-layer interconnection overhead, and energy consumption, and called for cooperation between industry and academia. 徐松余表示，麒麟 9050 系列已在中国半导体产业界支持下量产，但逻辑折叠路径仍需解决工具链、工艺差异补偿、跨层互联开销和能耗等问题，并呼吁产业界与学术界合作。

Xu Songyu also revealed that the next generation of Kirin chips is ready, and all current test results meet expectations. He believes that logic folding technology can continue to evolve over the next 10 years, and the specific technologies to be adopted at each stage still need to be gradually discussed. 徐松余还透露，下一代麒麟芯片已准备就绪，目前所有测试结果均符合预期。他认为逻辑折叠技术未来 10 年可以持续演进，各阶段具体采用哪些技术仍需逐步讨论。
