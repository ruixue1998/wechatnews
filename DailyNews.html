9 月消费电子价格普涨，存储设备同比上涨 46.7%

The National Bureau of Statistics released September price data on October 14. Prices for data storage devices, tablet computers, computers, and mobile phones rose by 46.7%, 23.6%, 21.8%, and 14.9% year-on-year, respectively. The increases for all four product categories expanded, collectively pushing the CPI up by approximately 0.23 percentage points year-on-year. 国家统计局于 10 月 14 日公布 9 月物价数据。数据存储设备、平板电脑、计算机和移动电话机价格分别同比上涨 46.7%、23.6%、21.8% 和 14.9%，四类产品涨幅均有所扩大，合计拉动 CPI 同比上涨约 0.23 个百分点。

On a month-on-month basis, prices for mobile phones, computers, data storage devices, and tablet computers rose by 3.7%, 2.0%, 1.7%, and 1.5%, respectively. The National Bureau of Statistics attributed this month-on-month price increase to rising costs, with these four product categories collectively contributing to a month-on-month CPI increase of approximately 0.04 percentage points. 按月比较，移动电话机、计算机、数据存储设备和平板电脑价格分别上涨 3.7%、2.0%、1.7% 和 1.5%。国家统计局将这组环比涨价归因于成本走高，四类产品合计影响 CPI 环比上涨约 0.04 个百分点。

The year-on-year and month-on-month increases in electronic product prices reflect different comparison periods, and not every device on sale was priced up by the same margin. 当月全国 CPI 同比上涨 0.9%、环比上涨 0.2%，扣除食品和能源的核心 CPI 同比上涨 1.0%。电子产品价格的同比和环比涨幅反映不同的比较时段，并非每一款在售设备都按同样幅度提价。
