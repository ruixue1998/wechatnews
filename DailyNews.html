苹果收紧员工 AI 预算，部分工程师每日额度从 300 美元降至 200 美元

According to Bloomberg, Apple last month reduced the daily spending limit for some engineers using AI models from $300 to $200, and is considering further lowering the quota for some engineering teams to $100. At the same time, the company is still pushing employees to use AI more deeply in development and internal operations. 据彭博社报道，苹果上月将部分工程师使用 AI 模型的每日开支上限从 300 美元降至 200 美元，并考虑把部分工程团队的额度进一步降至 100 美元。与此同时，公司仍在推动员工把 AI 更深入地用于开发和内部运营。

Many teams in non-engineering departments already have a daily limit of $100, and Apple has also discussed lowering some teams to $25; employees who genuinely need it can apply for an exemption from senior management. These budgets are used to pay for the tokens consumed by model processing requests, and some teams are being asked to switch to cheaper or local models. 非工程部门的许多团队已有每日 100 美元上限，苹果还讨论过把部分团队降至 25 美元；工作确有需要的员工可以向高层申请豁免。这些预算用于支付模型处理请求所消耗的 Token，部分团队正被要求改用更便宜的模型或本地模型。

Internal reactions have been mixed: some employees believe the previous quotas were rarely fully utilized, while others worry that budget cuts signal broader cost compression. An Apple spokesperson declined to comment. 内部反应并不一致：一些员工认为此前额度很少用满，另一些则担心削减预算预示着更广泛的成本压缩。苹果发言人拒绝置评。

ChatGPT 新增 GIF 生成，可调播放速度、逐帧修改

ChatGPT now supports generating GIFs directly from text prompts or uploaded photos, allowing users to adjust animation playback speed, view and modify individual frames, and then share the finished product. This feature is launched with OpenAI's Images 2.6 model. ChatGPT 开始支持用文字提示或上传照片直接生成 GIF，并允许调整动画播放速度、查看和修改单帧，再把成品分享出去。该功能随 OpenAI 的 Images 2.6 模型一起推出。

According to The Verge, an official demo showed the process of generating a dancing kitten GIF with a single prompt, with the generation interface offering speed adjustment and frame-by-frame viewing options. Previously, ChatGPT could not natively complete GIF generation in a single step. 据 The Verge 报道，官方演示展示了用一句提示生成跳舞小猫动图的过程，生成界面提供速度调整和逐帧查看选项。此前，ChatGPT 无法在单一步骤中原生完成 GIF 生成。

OpenAI stated that Images 2.6 also improves image generation speed and is being rolled out to ChatGPT and Codex users on web, desktop, and mobile platforms. The API also adds two new models, GPT-Image-2.6 Flare and Sunburst, focusing on speed and image quality, respectively. OpenAI 表示，Images 2.6 也提高了图片生成速度，正向网页、桌面和移动端的 ChatGPT 与 Codex 用户推送。API 同时新增 GPT-Image-2.6 Flare 和 Sunburst 两款模型，分别侧重速度与画质。

彭博调查：69% 中国受访者至少等到明年才换手机

According to a Bloomberg Intelligence survey conducted in October, approximately 69% of Chinese respondents stated they would not replace their mobile phones until at least 2027, a proportion roughly double that of May. More and more respondents plan to extend their phone replacement cycle to two to three years. 据彭博行业研究 10 月开展的调查，约 69% 的中国受访者表示，至少到 2027 年才会更换手机，这一比例约为 5 月的两倍。越来越多受访者计划将换机周期延长至两至三年。

Bloomberg reported that battery life, camera performance, and memory remain key purchasing factors. 87% of respondents believe that 16GB or more memory is suitable for daily use; among the 11 reasons listed for replacing a phone, AI ranked last at 8%, down from 12% a year ago. 彭博报道指出，电池续航、拍摄性能和内存仍是主要购买因素。87% 的受访者认为，16GB 或更大内存适合日常使用；在列出的 11 个换机理由中，AI 以 8% 排在末位，低于一年前的 12%。

The appeal of foldable screens is also limited: 5.8% of respondents are using or have decided to purchase a foldable phone. Analysts believe that AI's ability to drive Chinese phone demand independently is limited, but iPhone users' higher acceptance of paying for AI features may enhance Apple's appeal. 折叠屏的吸引力同样有限：5.8% 的受访者正在使用或已决定购买折叠手机。分析师认为，AI 单独推动中国手机需求的能力有限，但 iPhone 用户对为 AI 功能付费的接受度更高，可能增强苹果的吸引力。

手机越卖越贵：第三季度出货量降 19%，营收反增 3%

Preliminary data released by Counterpoint Research shows that global smartphone shipments in Q3 2026 decreased by 19% year-on-year, but revenue increased by 3% year-on-year, reaching $114 billion. The average selling price at wholesale increased by 27% year-on-year to a record $442, with the increased proportion of high-end models and price increases driven by storage costs offsetting the decline in sales. Counterpoint Research 发布的初步数据显示，2026 年第三季度全球智能手机出货量同比下降 19%，营收却同比增长 3%，达到 1140 亿美元。按批发价格计算的平均售价同比上涨 27%，达到创纪录的 442 美元，高端机型占比提高与存储成本推动的涨价抵消了销量下滑。

Apple's Q3 mobile phone revenue increased by 17% year-on-year, accounting for 50% of the global market, setting a record for the same period, while shipments remained largely flat year-on-year. Counterpoint attributed the rise in average price to the increased launch price of the iPhone 18 Pro series, the non-simultaneous release of the basic iPhone 18 model, and price increases for some older models in September. 苹果第三季度手机营收同比增长 17%，占全球市场的 50%，创同期纪录，出货量则同比基本持平。Counterpoint 将其均价上涨归因于 iPhone 18 Pro 系列首发价格提高、基础款 iPhone 18 未同步上市，以及部分旧款机型在 9 月涨价。

Samsung ranked second with a 19% revenue share, with revenue increasing by 14% year-on-year, and maintained its position as the top seller with a 22% shipment share. Xiaomi's revenue remained largely flat year-on-year, while OPPO and vivo decreased by 26% and 22% respectively; OPPO's data in the report includes OnePlus and realme. 三星以 19% 的营收份额排第二，营收同比增长 14%，并以 22% 的出货量份额保持销量第一。小米营收同比基本持平，OPPO 和 vivo 则分别下降 26% 和 22%；报告中的 OPPO 数据包含一加和 realme。

宇树王兴兴：明年大概率是机器人行业或模型的临界点

On November 4, Wang Xingxing, founder of Unitree Robotics, stated at the Internet Entrepreneur Forum of the World Internet Conference Wuzhen Summit that next year will likely be a tipping point for the robotics industry or robot models. 11 月 4 日，宇树科技创始人王兴兴在世界互联网大会乌镇峰会互联网企业家论坛上表示，大概率明年将是机器人行业或机器人模型的临界点。

Previously, at the China International Industry Fair on October 12, Wang Xingxing explained that Unitree has been conducting pilot projects with automotive companies since 2024, but moving from single-point trials to large-scale application still requires improving generalization capabilities and operational efficiency. 此前在 10 月 12 日的中国国际工业博览会现场，王兴兴曾解释，宇树从 2024 年起已与汽车企业开展落地试点，但从单点试验走向大规模应用，仍需提升泛化能力和作业效率。

He believes that some commercial service scenarios targeting businesses may progress faster, while home services require higher capabilities to adapt to complex environments. 他认为，部分面向企业的商业服务场景可能推进得更快，家庭服务对适应复杂环境的能力要求更高。

He also admitted that his belief in AI was insufficient in the past; with technological advancements, his confidence is growing daily. He believes that many people still do not fully utilize AI, and technological changes may be faster than industry expectations. 他还坦言，过去对 AI 的相信程度不够；随着技术进步，信心正与日俱增。他认为很多人对 AI 的使用仍不充分，技术变化可能快于行业预期。

Regarding computing power allocation, he suggested that computations with high real-time requirements, such as motion control, should remain on the robot's body, while complex decisions and large-scale scene adaptation should leverage the cloud as much as possible; the robot's onboard computing power can be configured according to customer needs. 在算力分配上，他提出，将运动控制等实时性要求高的计算留在机器人本体，复杂决策与大范围场景适应则尽量借助云端；本体算力按客户需要选配。

If future models are small enough to run directly on the robot's body, he believes that would also be a more ideal choice. 如果未来模型足够小、能够直接在本体运行，在他看来也是更理想的选择。

雷军分享小米人形机器人「实习」新进展：工厂作业达 8 小时 5 分钟，中途两次充电

Yesterday, Lei Jun released a factory operation video of Xiaomi's humanoid robot, showcasing CyberOne 02 completing the sorting of center console side cover plates in Xiaomi Auto's final assembly workshop, with a cumulative operation time of 8 hours and 5 minutes. 昨天，雷军发布小米人形机器人的工厂作业视频，展示 CyberOne 02 在小米汽车总装车间完成中控台侧盖板排序，累计作业达到 8 小时 5 分钟。

The side cover plate is a large, irregularly shaped flexible workpiece. The robot needs to retrieve the cover plate from the raw material box, adjust its posture, and place it into the corresponding rack slot according to the vehicle model's production line sequence. 侧盖板是尺寸较大、形态不规则的柔性工件，机器人要按车型上线顺序，从原料箱取出盖板、调整姿态并放入对应料架格口。

The two humanoid robots in the video operate in sync with the production line rhythm and coordinate with AMRs (Autonomous Mobile Robots) transporting materials to adjust their retrieval routes and stride. 视频中的两台人形机器人随产线节拍运转，并配合运送物料的 AMR 调整取料路线与步幅。

During the test, the robots charged after cumulative operations of 3 hours and 2 minutes, and 6 hours and 3 minutes, respectively. In the latter half, one robot dropped a part, which was picked up by a wheeled robot. 测试期间，机器人分别在累计作业 3 小时 2 分钟和 6 小时 3 分钟后充电。后半程一台机器人掉落零件，由轮式机器人捡起。

Regarding the question of why humanoid robots are used in factories, Lei Jun responded that the hardware of general-purpose humanoid robots does not need to be modified and can adapt to more positions and scenarios. 对于为何在工厂使用人形机器人的疑问，雷军回应， 通用人形机器人的硬件不用改动，可以适应更多岗位和场景。

中国信通院等 20 家单位发布智能体公约，抵制算法压榨

On November 4, 20 units including CAICT, Huawei, Tencent, Alibaba, Ant Group, and Douyin jointly launched the 'Self-Discipline Convention for AI Agent Development' at the World Internet Conference Wuzhen Summit, proposing eight self-discipline measures covering mutual trust, interconnection, interoperability, security protection, governance, and user rights. 11 月 4 日，中国信通院、华为、腾讯、阿里、蚂蚁、抖音等 20 家单位在世界互联网大会乌镇峰会联合发起《智能体发展自律公约》，提出互信、互联、互操作、安全防护、治理、用户权益等八条自律措施。

The convention proposes jointly resisting the use of AI agents for algorithmic exploitation, gray/black market activities, and other behaviors; safeguarding users' rights to know, choose, and have ultimate control over important matters, preventing the misuse of personal information, excessive catering, and cognitive manipulation, and strengthening protection for minors, the elderly, and other groups. 公约提出，共同抵制利用智能体实施算法压榨、黑灰产等行为；保障用户知情权、选择权和对重要事项的最终控制权，防范个人信息滥用、过度迎合与认知操纵，加强对未成年人、老年人等群体的保护。

Regarding cross-vendor collaboration, the convention advocates for the development of technologies and standards for AI agent registration and resolution, capability discovery, protocol compatibility, and mutual recognition, and for improving mechanisms for categorized and tiered authorization, behavioral boundaries, rights, obligations, and responsibility allocation. Security protection measures include operational monitoring, behavior control, and abnormal circuit breaking to prevent unauthorized operations, privacy leaks, and loss of control over behavior. 在跨厂商协作上，公约倡议发展智能体注册与解析、能力发现、协议兼容与互认等技术和标准，并完善分类分级授权、行为边界、权利义务与责任划分机制。安全防护措施则包括运行监测、行为管控和异常熔断，防范越权操作、隐私泄露及行为失控。

宝马加快重组，全年汽车业务利润率预期降至盈亏平衡附近

BMW announced on November 4 that it would accelerate product and organizational restructuring, with some models using fifth-generation battery technology being phased out earlier than planned, and the streamlining of management functions also implemented faster than initially scheduled. These measures will have a non-operating profit impact on the automotive business in the fourth quarter. 宝马 11 月 4 日宣布加快产品与组织重组，部分使用第五代电池技术的车型将提前退出，管理职能精简也将比原计划更快实施。相关措施将在第四季度对汽车业务产生非经营性利润影响。

In the third quarter, BMW Group's pre-tax profit was 2.083 billion euros, a year-on-year decrease of 10.6%, with an EBIT margin of 3.4% for the automotive business. The company lowered its full-year automotive business reported EBIT margin forecast from the previous 1% to 3% down to -0.5% to +0.5%; the operating margin excluding restructuring measures is still expected to be above 2.0%. 第三季度，宝马集团税前利润为 20.83 亿欧元，同比下降 10.6%，汽车业务 EBIT 利润率为 3.4%。公司将全年汽车业务报告口径的 EBIT 利润率预期，从此前的 1% 至 3% 下调至负 0.5% 至正 0.5%；剔除重组措施的经营性利润率仍预计高于 2.0%。

BMW stated that demand for the Neue Klasse new generation models is strong, and production shifts have been increased ahead of schedule. The accelerated ramp-up of production capacity also allows older technologies and some older models to be phased out earlier. The model is also equipped with a pixel decoder of about 9.6 million parameters, which can directly output segmentation masks and transparency information, without needing to connect to a separate SAM model. 团队在训练中专门核对「图里没有」的样本，同时训练误框与漏检两类问题；其自动标注流程累计处理超过 1 亿张图片、约 36 亿个目标框。模型还配有约 960 万参数的像素解码器，可直接输出分割掩码及透明度信息，无需另接 SAM 模型。

The project uses the Apache 2.0 license, making model weights, technical reports, tutorials, evaluation code, and the Mask2Alpha matting annotation tool publicly available, and provides a web demo. The current version supports image input, allowing for both online trial and local deployment. 项目采用 Apache 2.0 许可，公开了模型权重、技术报告、教程、评测代码与抠图标注工具 Mask2Alpha，并提供网页 Demo。目前版本支持图片输入，既能在线试用，也能在本地部署。
