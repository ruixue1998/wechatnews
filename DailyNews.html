Omdia：今年全球智能手机出货量预计下降 12%

Omdia released a forecast yesterday that global smartphone shipments will decrease by 12% year-on-year this year to 1.097 billion units; however, total shipment value will increase by 12% to $651.6 billion, with the average selling price increasing by 27% year-on-year to $594. Omdia 昨日发布预测，今年全球智能手机出货量将同比下降 12%，至 10.97 亿部；但总出货额将增长 12%，至 6516 亿美元，平均售价同比提高 27%，至 594 美元。

The report predicts that the proportion of models priced below $200 will decrease from 40.6% in 2025 to 25.6% in 2027, while the proportion of models priced above $800 will increase from 21.1% to 28.4%. Omdia believes that extended replacement cycles, the expansion of the second-hand and refurbished phone market, constrained consumer purchasing power, coupled with phone manufacturers shifting towards higher-profit products, are accelerating the market's migration towards higher price segments. 报告预计，低于 200 美元的机型占比会从 2025 年的 40.6% 降至 2027 年的 25.6%，800 美元以上机型的占比则将从 21.1% 升至 28.4%。Omdia 认为，换机周期延长、二手与翻新机市场扩张、消费者购买力承压，加上手机厂商转向利润更高的产品，正在加快市场向高价位段迁移。

未满 16 周岁使用社交、游戏和 AI 服务拟强制进入未成年人模式

The Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) announced on September 18 the "Regulations of the State Council on Ensuring the Healthy and Safe Use of Networks by Minors (Draft for Comment)," with the deadline for public comments being October 17. The draft includes network platforms, smart terminal manufacturers, and app stores in the responsibility system for protecting minors online. 国家网信办 9 月 18 日公布《国务院关于保障未成年人健康安全使用网络的规定（征求意见稿）》，公开征求意见截止日期为 10 月 17 日。征求意见稿将网络平台、智能终端厂商和应用商店一并纳入未成年人网络保护责任体系。

For users under 16, online games, audio/video, social services, and AI services that may affect cognition should be provided through a minor mode. Platforms need to have the ability to identify minors, using methods such as legal identity documents, national network identity authentication, non-storage one-time facial verification, and behavioral characteristic recognition, while ensuring personal information security. 对未满 16 周岁的用户，网络游戏、音视频、社交服务以及可能影响认知的 AI 服务，应当通过未成年人模式提供。平台需要具备未成年人识别能力，可使用法定身份证件、国家网络身份认证、非存储式一次性人脸核验和行为特征识别等方式，同时保障个人信息安全。

The draft also prohibits providing virtual intimate relationship services such as virtual relatives and virtual partners to minors, and requires algorithms not to induce emotional dependence, addiction, or excessive consumption in minors. Smart terminals need to support mode linkage, one-click switching, exit verification, and anti-circumvention; relevant functions are also proposed to be included in telecommunication equipment network access license testing. 征求意见稿还禁止向未成年人提供虚拟亲属、虚拟伴侣等虚拟亲密关系服务，并要求算法不得诱导未成年人情感依赖、沉迷或过度消费。智能终端需要支持模式联动、一键切换、退出核验和防绕过；相关功能还拟纳入电信设备进网许可检测。

亚马逊 Bedrock 接入 Kimi K3，支持 100 万 token 上下文

Amazon AWS announced on September 18 that Kimi K3 from Moonshot AI has been integrated into Amazon Bedrock. Developers can call the model through Bedrock Runtime's OpenAI-compatible Responses, Chat Completions interfaces, and Invoke and Converse APIs. 亚马逊 AWS 9 月 18 日宣布，月之暗面的 Kimi K3 已接入 Amazon Bedrock。开发者可通过 Bedrock Runtime 的 OpenAI 兼容 Responses、Chat Completions 接口，以及 Invoke 和 Converse API 调用模型。

Kimi K3 supports native visual input and a 1 million token context, and is the first open-weight model on Bedrock to support explicit prompt caching. Caching can reuse stable prefixes such as code repositories, tool definitions, and documents, reducing latency and input costs caused by repeatedly passing the same context in long tasks. Kimi K3 支持原生视觉输入和 100 万 token 上下文，是 Bedrock 首个支持显式提示词缓存的开源权重模型。缓存可以复用代码仓库、工具定义和文档等稳定前缀，减少长任务反复传入相同上下文带来的延迟和输入成本。

The model is currently available through US geographic regions and global cross-region inference configurations. AWS states that inference data will not be shared with model providers, nor will it be used to train underlying models, and zero data retention and zero operator access are enabled by default. 模型目前通过美国地理区域与全球跨区域推理配置提供。AWS 称，推理数据不会与模型提供方共享，也不会用于训练底层模型，并默认启用零数据保留和零运维人员访问。

IDC：上半年全球四足机器人出货量增长 92.5%

IDC's "Global Quadruped Robot Market Share, 1H26" released today shows that global quadruped robot shipments exceeded 49,000 units in the first half of 2026, nearly doubling compared to the same period last year. Education, scientific research, and household consumption contributed approximately 60% of shipments, while industry applications such as government, industrial manufacturing, and public utilities contributed over two-thirds of the revenue with less than 40% of shipments. IDC 今日发布的《全球四足机器人市场份额，1H26》显示，2026 年上半年全球四足机器人出货量超过 4.9 万台，较去年同期接近翻倍。教育科研与家用消费贡献约 60% 的出货量，政府、工业制造和公共事业等行业应用则以不到 40% 的出货量贡献了超过三分之二的收入。

Industry applications deployed over 19,000 units in the first half of the year, of which industrial manufacturing deployed over 4,100 units, with a significantly higher growth rate than the overall market. IDC summarized this structure as "shipments driven by consumer research, revenue driven by industry applications." 行业应用上半年部署量超过 1.9 万台，其中工业制造部署超过 4100 台，增速明显高于整体市场。IDC 将这一结构概括为「出货靠消费科研、收入靠行业应用」。

Regarding manufacturers, Chinese companies such as Unitree Robotics, Deep Robotics, ZHIYUAN Robotics, and Juwei Technology cover consumer, research, and industrial scenarios; Deep Robotics leads in the industry application market. European and American manufacturers like ANYbotics and Boston Dynamics are more focused on government, oil and gas, and special operations. 厂商方面，宇树科技、云深处、智元机器人和具微科技等中国企业覆盖消费、科研和工业场景；云深处在行业应用市场居首。ANYbotics、波士顿动力等欧美厂商则更集中于政府、油气和特种作业。

Xiaomi stated that MiMo-V2.6-Pro scored 46 points in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, surpassing Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8 Max, but still lagging behind closed-source models like Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra. 小米称，MiMo-V2.6-Pro 在 Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 中获得 46 分，超过 Kimi K3 和 Qwen3.8 Max，但与 Claude Fable 5.1、GPT-6 Astra 等闭源模型仍有差距。

It is reported that the Live RL training for MiMo-V2.6-Flash and Pro lasted less than 6 days, with training costs of approximately $850,000 and $2.62 million respectively. Each completed 30 training steps, generating a cumulative total of about 750,000 trajectories. 据悉，MiMo-V2.6-Flash 和 Pro 的 Live RL 训练历时不到 6 天，训练成本分别约 85 万美元和 262 万美元，各完成 30 步训练，累计产生约 75 万条轨迹。

After training, both models improved by approximately 17 points and 14 points respectively on DeepSWE v1.1. Each training update used 1568 samples, supported a 1M context length, and the token count per training step reached 2.7 billion to 3.7 billion. 训练后，两款模型在 DeepSWE v1.1 上分别提升约 17 分和 14 分。训练单次更新使用 1568 个样本，支持 1M 上下文长度，单步训练 Token 量达到 27 亿至 37 亿。

In terms of capabilities, MiMo-V2.6 further integrates multimodal perception, 3D spatial reasoning, and Computer Use capabilities, which can be used for generating 3D open-world games, Blender modeling, controlling simulated robotic arms, and operating common office and productivity software. 能力方面，MiMo-V2.6 将多模态感知、3D 空间推理和 Computer Use 能力进一步整合，可用于生成 3D 开放世界游戏、Blender 建模、控制仿真机械臂，以及操作常见办公和生产力软件。

Xiaomi also launched the MiMo Desktop client, with MiMo-V2.6-Pro offering an UltraSpeed mode that provides up to 20 times faster output. 小米同时上线 MiMo Desktop 桌面客户端，其中 MiMo-V2.6-Pro 提供最高 20 倍输出速度的 UltraSpeed 模式。

The MiMo-V2.6 series has also been launched on the MiMo Open Platform, with API pricing consistent with the previous generation: Pro's regular input and output prices are 3 yuan and 6 yuan per million tokens respectively, while Flash's are 1 yuan and 2 yuan respectively. MiMo-V2.6 系列也已登陆 MiMo 开放平台，API 定价沿用上一代：Pro 常规输入、输出价格分别为每百万 Token 3 元和 6 元，Flash 分别为 1 元和 2 元。
