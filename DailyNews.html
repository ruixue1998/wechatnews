DeepSeek 发新论文，公开 Agent 训练沙箱 DSec

DeepSeek recently unveiled DSec, its internal sandbox infrastructure used for Agent training and evaluation. The paper was submitted to arXiv on September 19th, with Liang Wenfeng listed among the authors. It describes an operational platform already in production, not a new model release. DeepSeek 近日公开了内部 Agent 训练与评测使用的沙箱基础设施 DSec。论文于 9 月 19 日提交至 arXiv，梁文锋列在作者名单中。它介绍的是已投入生产的运行平台，并非新模型发布。

During training, Agents need to open code repositories, execute commands, and call tools, with each task requiring an independent environment that can persistently save its state. DSec provides four sandbox backends—function calls, containers, micro-VMs, and full VMs—through a unified SDK, allowing isolation methods to be chosen based on the task. The platform also loads images on demand from a distributed file system, reducing concentrated download pressure during environment startup. Agent 在训练中需要打开代码仓库、执行命令和调用工具，每次任务都要有可持续保存状态的独立环境。DSec 通过统一的 SDK 提供函数调用、容器、微型虚拟机和完整虚拟机四种沙箱后端，并按任务选择隔离方式。平台还从分布式文件系统按需载入镜像，减少环境启动时的集中下载压力。

The paper states that a production unit with approximately 160 nodes processes about 3 million sandbox instances daily. The peak concurrency in the production environment exceeds 380,000, with over 5,000 instances created per second; reinforcement learning training and evaluation sandbox tasks for DeepSeek V3.2 to V4.1 all run on DSec. These scale and performance figures are from the team's system reports and are not yet independently measured by a third party. 论文称，一个约有 160 台节点的生产单元每天处理约 300 万个沙箱实例。生产环境的峰值并发超过 38 万个，每秒可创建超过 5000 个；DeepSeek V3.2 至 V4.1 的强化学习训练与评测沙箱任务均运行在 DSec 上。这些规模与性能数字来自团队的系统报告，尚非第三方独立测量。

iOS 27.2 Beta 2 加入运动数据限制，可阻止「摇一摇」广告跳转

According to actual tests by iFanr/APPSO, iOS 27.2 Beta 2 adds a new setting called "Restrict Motion Data" under Apple accounts in the Chinese region. 据爱范儿/APPSO 实测，iOS 27.2 Beta 2 在国区 Apple 账户下新增了一个名为「Restrict Motion Data」的设置。

In this version, by selecting an app under "Settings - Privacy & Security - Motion & Fitness," you can restrict it from reading motion data such as accelerometer and gyroscope. Restricted apps can no longer use phone shaking to trigger splash screen ad jumps; regular splash screen ads and other interactions like tapping and swiping will still appear. 该版本中，在「设置—隐私与安全—运动与健身」中选择应用，可限制其读取加速度计和陀螺仪等运动数据。被限制的应用无法再用手机晃动触发开屏广告跳转；普通开屏广告和点击、上滑等其他交互仍会出现。

It's worth noting that this setting follows the account region logged into the App Store, not the phone's sales region. After switching to a non-Chinese account, the entry and previously set app list will disappear. 值得注意的是，这项设置跟随 App Store 登录的账户区域，而非手机销售地区。切换到非中国区账户后，入口和此前设置的应用名单会消失。

微软向 DARPA 开放 Majorana 2 量子系统，支持现场独立评估

Microsoft will open a quantum computing center in Maryland, USA, providing researchers from the US Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA) direct access to Majorana 2 chips and related quantum computing systems. Previously, external researchers could only access this hardware remotely through Microsoft's facilities in Redmond and Europe. 微软将在美国马里兰州开设一座量子计算中心，让美国国防高级研究计划局（DARPA）的研究人员直接接触 Majorana 2 芯片和相关量子计算系统。此前，外部研究者只能通过微软在雷德蒙德及欧洲的设施远程访问这套硬件。

On-site access allows DARPA to inspect the equipment using its own boot sequences and testing methods, without having to rely entirely on interfaces provided by Microsoft. DARPA's evaluation goal is to determine by 2033 whether different quantum computing approaches have economic value, while Microsoft plans to launch commercial systems by 2029. 现场访问允许 DARPA 使用自有启动序列和测试方法检查设备，不必完全依赖微软提供的接口。DARPA 的评估目标是在 2033 年前判断不同量子计算路线是否具备经济价值，微软则计划在 2029 年前推出商业系统。

Majorana 2 continues Microsoft's topological quantum computing roadmap. The new center is located near Washington, making it convenient for government researchers to continuously verify the hardware's stability, control methods, and scalability, rather than just reviewing test results submitted by the vendor. Majorana 2 延续微软的拓扑量子计算路线。新中心靠近华盛顿，方便政府研究人员持续验证硬件的稳定性、控制方法和扩展能力，而不是只查看厂商提交的测试结果。

京东方玻璃基封装载板试验线贯通，24 层产品送交客户验证

BOE disclosed at its 2026 semi-annual performance briefing that its glass-based packaging substrate pilot line has been fully operational, and 22-layer and 24-layer products have been submitted to customers for verification. The company had previously completed full-process technologies such as TGV opening, deep hole copper filling, build-up, and wiring, and had sent samples to some domestic customers. 京东方在 2026 年半年度业绩说明会上披露，玻璃基封装载板试验线已经全线贯通，22 层和 24 层产品已送交客户验证。公司此前已完成 TGV 开孔、深孔填铜、增层和布线等全流程工艺，并向部分国内客户送样。

Glass-based substrates are primarily aimed at advanced packaging for large-sized computing chips. Compared to organic materials, glass has potential in terms of flatness, thermal stability, and high-density wiring, but chip mounting, electrical performance, signal conduction, and system reliability tests still need to be completed before mass production. 玻璃基载板主要面向大尺寸算力芯片的先进封装。相比有机材料，玻璃在平整度、热稳定性和高密度布线方面具有潜力，但量产前仍需完成芯片贴装、电性能、信号导通和系统可靠性测试。

BOE stated that it hopes to reach mass production decision conditions in the first or first half of 2027. Current progress is still in the customer verification stage, and the pilot line being operational does not mean it has entered large-scale mass production. 京东方表示，希望在 2027 年一季度或上半年达到量产决策条件。当前进展仍处于客户验证阶段，试验线贯通不等于已经进入大规模量产。

美国调查 Comma.ai 辅助驾驶设备，涉及 5 起碰撞事故

The U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration has launched a preliminary investigation into Comma.ai's aftermarket driver-assistance devices. Regulators received 5 reports of accidents where vehicles using the devices collided with slower or stationary vehicles in the same lane, with 2 of these accidents resulting in 3 fatalities; another 4 accidents collectively reported up to 11 injuries. The investigation targets the performance of the device and system, and at this stage, not all accident causes can be attributed to the same software configuration. 美国国家公路交通安全管理局对 Comma.ai 的后装辅助驾驶设备展开初步调查。监管机构收到 5 起车辆在使用相关设备时撞上同车道慢行或静止车辆的事故报告，其中 2 起事故造成 3 人死亡；另有 4 起事故合计报告最多 11 人受伤。调查针对的是设备及系统表现，现阶段尚不能将所有事故原因归于同一种软件配置。

Users can propose meal or weekly shopping needs in a conversation, and Instacart will generate a checkout-ready cart based on the selected store's real-time inventory and personal preferences, which the user then confirms for purchase. 生鲜配送平台 Instacart 宣布，计划把自家的购物连接器接入 Meta 的个人 AI Agent Muse。用户可在对话中提出一顿饭或一周的采购需求，由 Instacart 根据所选商店的实时库存与个人偏好生成待结算购物车，再由用户确认购买。

Instacart has already integrated similar capabilities into platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. This announcement confirms that Muse will soon be integrated; Meta demonstrated shopping and payment scenarios when introducing Muse in early September, but this grocery connector has not yet been released as a live feature. Instacart 已将类似能力接入 ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini 等平台。此次公告确认的是 Muse 即将接入；Meta 9 月初介绍 Muse 时已展示购物和支付场景，但这项生鲜连接器尚未作为已上线功能发布。

YouTube 推出 Shorts 连续剧功能，短剧可按季和集播放

YouTube announced the launch of Shorts Series at the Made on YouTube event on September 23rd, allowing creators to arrange short videos into seasons and episodes, add custom thumbnails, and enable viewers to play them consecutively in order. The feature began rolling out to creators that day, covering web, mobile, and TV platforms. YouTube 在 9 月 23 日的 Made on YouTube 活动上宣布推出 Shorts Series，创作者可以把短视频编排成季和集，添加自定义缩略图，观众也能按顺序连续播放。该功能当天开始向创作者推出，覆盖网页、手机和电视端。

YouTube stated that in the first half of 2026, the platform's micro-series had over 6.5 billion views, with watch time increasing by over 50% year-on-year; micro-series views on TV increased by over 90% year-on-year. Short videos were originally primarily watched on mobile, and this update brings a serial viewing path to larger screens. YouTube 表示，2026 年上半年平台微短剧的观看次数超过 65 亿次，观看时长同比增长超过 50%；电视端的微短剧观看次数同比增长超过 90%。短视频原本以手机观看为主，这次更新把连续剧式的观看路径带到了大屏。

YouTube also added a voice input comment feature for TV, allowing users to comment directly on the TV while watching. Community posts are also planned to be available on TV in the coming months. YouTube 同时为电视端增加语音输入评论功能，用户观看时可直接在电视上发表评论。社区帖子也计划在未来几个月进入电视端。

《活久久》定档 12 月 31 日，张艺凡与陈立农主演

The romantic film 'Long Live Love' has released its first trailer and announced its theatrical release in mainland China on December 31st. The film is directed by Chen Guohui, starring Zhang Yifan and Chen Linong, with Lin Yongjian, Guo Keyu, and others also appearing. 爱情电影《活久久》发布首支预告，并宣布于 12 月 31 日在中国内地上映。影片由陈国辉执导，张艺凡、陈立农领衔主演，林永健、郭柯宇等参演。

The trailer revolves around the married life of Xu Qian and Qiao Yize: the two promised to accompany each other at the wedding, and then jointly faced sudden challenges. The film connects their story with a journey from a hospital room to the wilderness and the seaside. 预告围绕许倩和乔一泽的婚后生活展开：两人在婚礼上约定相伴，随后共同面对突如其来的考验。影片以一段从病房到旷野、海边的旅程串联两人的故事。

《雷·冈恩》首曝预告，布拉德·伯德拍未来都市侦探故事

Netflix released the first trailer for the animated film "Ray Gunn," directed by Brad Bird. The film is set in the retro-futuristic city of Metropia, where humans and aliens coexist. Private detective Raymond Gunn, voiced by Sam Rockwell, still relies on field investigations to solve cases; his partner is the one-eyed alien Eye-Ra, voiced by Tom Waits. Star Venus Nova, voiced by Scarlett Johansson, gets involved in the case, leading the story into the city's celebrity circles and underworld. Netflix 发布布拉德·伯德执导的动画电影《雷·冈恩》首支预告。影片设定在复古未来都市 Metropia，人类与外星人共处，山姆·洛克威尔配音的私家侦探雷蒙德·冈恩仍靠实地调查办案；汤姆·威茨配音的独眼外星人 Eye-Ra 是他的搭档。斯嘉丽·约翰逊配音的明星 Venus Nova 卷入案件，将故事引向城市的名流圈与地下世界。

Bird describes the film as a combination of film noir detective story and sci-fi adventure, with the creators developing this work from a project he conceived in his early years. The film is scheduled for a limited release in select theaters and 70mm cinemas on December 4, and will be available on Netflix on December 18. 伯德将影片描述为黑色侦探片与科幻冒险的结合，主创从他早年构思的项目发展出这部作品。影片计划 12 月 4 日在部分影院及 70mm 影院限量上映，12 月 18 日上线 Netflix。
